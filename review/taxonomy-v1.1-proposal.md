# Taxonomy v1.1: proposed changes for clarity (for your decision, nothing applied yet)

Based on how the 115 sub-topics actually behaved across California's 5,041 bills (6,417 tags). Three kinds of problem showed up.

## 1. Sub-topics too small to say anything (merge)

17 of 115 sub-topics got 8 or fewer tags in a full session. A marker on a meter built from 3 tags is not informative.

| Today | Tags | Proposed |
|---|---|---|
| Guns: Firearms in schools and public places | 3 | merge into **Carry and ownership** |
| Guns: Extreme risk protection orders | 4 | merge with Safe storage → **Safe storage and extreme-risk orders** |
| Guns: Safe storage and child access | 5 | (as above) |
| Guns: Dealers, ammunition and manufacturing | 10 | merge with Background checks → **Purchase, licensing and dealers** |
| Guns: Weapon and magazine types | 9 | keep, rename **Weapon and magazine types** |
| Guns: Violence prevention programs | 5 | keep (funding only) |
| Immigration: Employment verification | 2 | fold into **State cooperation with federal enforcement** |
| Immigration: In-state tuition and education access | 5 | merge with Benefits → **Benefits, education and services for immigrants** |
| AI: Cybersecurity | 6 | merge with Data privacy → **Data privacy and security** |
| Children: Paid family leave and family benefits | 6 | move to Labor → **Worker benefits (paid leave, unemployment insurance)** |

## 2. Sub-topics that are really several things (split or re-axis)

These came out mostly neutral, unclear or mixed because one axis cannot describe what is inside them.

| Sub-topic | Neutral/unclear/mixed | Problem | Proposed |
|---|---|---|---|
| Emergency powers | 63% | mostly procedure | make it axis N (no direction) |
| Curriculum and classroom content | 59% | bills require instruction, not restrict it | keep, add a definition: *restricts* means bans or limits content, *expands* means requires or adds it |
| Gambling and sports betting | 57% | few bills | keep, merge into Consumer protection and fees if still small |
| Long-term care and disability services | 49% | mostly programs and funding | axis F (funding and services) |
| Agriculture and food | 49% | grab bag | rename **Food and agriculture**, axis N |
| Child welfare and foster care | 46% | mostly program design | axis F |
| Higher education (tuition, tenure, DEI) | 45% | two different questions | split into **Higher education costs and aid** (F) and **Campus policy (speech, DEI, tenure)** (R) |
| Climate and emissions | 40% | many reporting and study bills | keep; most neutrals are legitimately "reporting only" |

## 3. A catch-all topic: Government administration

It is the largest topic (1,123 bills) because **429 are ceremonial or commemorative** (naming a highway, honoring a person) and 277 are agency structure. Neither is a policy position. They dominate the "bills by topic" bar and give the topic's meter little meaning.

Proposed:
- Take **Ceremonial and commemorative measures** out of the topic list entirely: a separate "Not a policy topic" count, excluded from charts and meters.
- Rename the topic **Government and administration** and keep: State agencies and programs, Local control and preemption, Ethics and transparency, Public employees and pensions.
- Fold **Constitutional amendments and resolutions** and **Ballot measures** (from Elections) into one sub-topic, **State constitution and ballot measures**.
- Emergency powers becomes axis N (above).

## 4. The meter itself: one problem

A topic's average mixes two different questions. Access and rights tags (expanding services, protecting a right) average positive in almost every topic. Regulation tags (tightening rules) average negative. A topic's single marker mostly reflects how many of each kind it has.

| Topic | Access and rights (avg, n) | Regulation (avg, n) | Single marker |
|---|---|---|---|
| Immigration | +0.63 (41) | −0.67 (36) | **+0.03** |
| Labor and employment | +0.92 (49) | −0.30 (157) | **−0.01** |
| Children and families | +0.85 (54) | −0.32 (106) | **+0.07** |
| Health care | +0.72 (229) | −0.29 (271) | +0.17 |
| Criminal justice | +0.51 (151) | −0.63 (220) | −0.17 |
| Consumer protection | +0.46 (13) | −0.62 (433) | −0.59 |

Immigration and Labor both look "balanced" on one meter when they actually contain two strong, opposite patterns.

Proposed: keep the **single meter as the headline on the overview**, and on each topic page show **two markers, one for access and rights and one for regulation**, wherever each has at least 8 tags. Add a one-line note on the overview that a marker near the middle can hide a split.

Optional wording fix: "restrictive" next to a consumer-protection rule can read as anti-consumer. If you prefer, label the ends **More rules / restrictions** and **Fewer rules / restrictions**.

## Net effect

115 sub-topics → about 92. Retagging all 5,041 California bills with the revised list: about $3 and 35 minutes. Existing review work and the legislator pages carry over (they are rebuilt from the new tags).
