Find my legislators

Enter your street address and city to see who represents you in the Florida Legislature, then what each of them has authored and how they have voted.

Addresses are matched by the U.S. Census Bureau’s geocoder, called from your browser; StateTrack never sees them.

Demo for Florida’s 2025–26 session. Bill data: Florida Legislature (flsenate.gov, flhouse.gov). Topics and directions are labelled by an AI model from the bill’s title and the bill summary, and are approximate. How it works
