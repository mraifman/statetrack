SB 7018 (2025) · OGSR/Parental Consent Requirements Before Terminating a Pregnancy

Removes the scheduled repeal of the public records exemption protecting identifying information about minors who petition courts to waive parental consent for abortion.

What the bill does (FL bill summary (Senate bill page))

OGSR/Parental Consent Requirements Before Terminating a Pregnancy; Amending provisions relating to an exemption from public records requirements for certain information that could identify a minor petitioning a court to waive parental consent requirements before terminating a pregnancy; deleting the scheduled repeal of the exemption, etc.
