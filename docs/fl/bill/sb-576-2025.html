SB 576 (2025) · Service of Process

Expands registered agent office hours for service of process, allows personal service on receivers during receivership, and revises substituted service requirements.

What the bill does (FL bill summary (Senate bill page))

Service of Process; Expanding the hours during which registered agents are required to keep the designated registered office open for the purpose of process service; authorizing service of process by personally serving the receiver for specified domestic entities in receivership during pendency of the receivership; requiring that a certain substituted service of process be issued in the name of the party to be served in care of the Secretary of State; specifying that registered agents must have been designated under a specified provision for a specified purpose, etc.
