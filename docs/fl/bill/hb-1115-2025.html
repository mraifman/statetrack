What the bill does (FL bill summary (Senate bill page))

Education; Requires that certain surtax revenues which are shared with school districts must also be shared with charter schools on proportionate basis in accordance with certain provisions; provides school district reporting requirements relating to specified revenues shared with charter schools; revises provisions relating to charter schools & schools of hope.
