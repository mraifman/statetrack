What the bill does (FL bill summary (Senate bill page))

Tax Credits for Providing Housing for Homeless Employees; Creates specified tax credits for certain businesses that provide housing for employees; provides requirements to receive such credit; provides maximum amount of tax credits that may be distributed; requires department to approve credits before they may be used; requires such approval be done in specified order.
