SB 1472 (2025) · Public Records/School Security Guards

Exempts certain information about school security guards held by state, local law enforcement, school districts, or charter schools from public records requirements, with future legislative review.

What the bill does (FL bill summary (Senate bill page))

Public Records/School Security Guards; Providing that certain information relating to school security guards held by the Department of Law Enforcement, a law enforcement agency, a school district, or a charter school is exempt from public records requirements; providing for future legislative review and repeal of the exemption; providing a statement of public necessity, etc.
