HB 5701 (2026) · Petroleum Cleanup Programs

Makes permanent the bar on certain cleanup deductibles, copays and monetary caps for petroleum contamination, and shifts specified costs to the Inland Protection Trust Fund.

What the bill does (FL bill summary (Senate bill page))

Petroleum Cleanup Programs; Removes 2025-2026 fiscal year limitation on prohibiting certain deductibles and copays, prohibiting enforcement of certain monetary caps, requiring that certain costs be absorbed at the expense of the Inland Protection Trust Fund, and providing exceptions.
