SB 1066 (2026) · Tributaries of the St. Johns River

Creates a project lead, a required Ocklawaha River restoration plan under a general permit, and an advisory council on northeast Florida river and springs recreation.

What the bill does (FL bill summary (Senate bill page))

Tributaries of the St. Johns River; Citing this act as the "Northeast Florida Rivers, Springs, and Community Investment Act"; requiring the Department of Environmental Protection, by a specified date, to hire a project lead to oversee the implementation of the act; requiring the department to develop, by a specified date, a project plan for the restoration of the Ocklawaha River; providing that the project plan is an environmental restoration or enhancement project subject to a general permit from the department and water management districts; providing for the creation of the Northeast Florida River and Springs Recreation and Economic Development Advisory Council by a specified date, etc.
