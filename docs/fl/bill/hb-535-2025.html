HB 535 (2025) · Public Lodging and Public Food Service Establishments

Requires restaurants charging operations fees to give notice, bars double-charging with automatic gratuity, adds receipt disclosures, and bars private lawsuits.

What the bill does (FL bill summary (Senate bill page))

Public Lodging and Public Food Service Establishments; Revises notification requirements for removing guests from public lodging & public food service establishments; requires public food service establishments that charge operations charge to provide specified notice; prohibits public food service establishments that charge operations charge that is used to compensate employees from also charging automatic gratuity; requires bills & receipts to contain certain information; prohibits private cause of action.
