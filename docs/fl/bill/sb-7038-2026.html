SB 7038 (2026) · Education

Requires public colleges and universities to waive tuition for eligible Florida State Guard members and requires fee-based services for blind or visually impaired people to disclose free alternatives.

What the bill does (FL bill summary (Senate bill page))

Education; Requiring each Florida College System institution and state university to waive tuition and fees for members of the Florida State Guard if certain conditions are met; requiring entities that offer fee-based services to individuals who are blind or visually impaired to disclose in writing whether the service may be obtained elsewhere at no cost; authorizing school district career centers to use alternative methods adopted by the board in lieu of common placement tests to assess students in basic communication and computation skills; providing that a fee waiver only applies to a full-time undergraduate student, beginning with a specified academic year, etc.
