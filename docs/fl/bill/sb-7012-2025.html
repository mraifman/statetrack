SB 7012 (2025) · Child Welfare

Omnibus child welfare bill: removes DCF exemption authority for background screening, adds recruitment and treatment foster care pilot programs subject to appropriation, and protects research incentives.

What the bill does (FL bill summary (Senate bill page))

Child Welfare; Requiring the Department of Children and Families to maintain copies of certain assessments and tools used to assess children for certain placement; removing authority for the department to grant exemptions from working with children or the developmentally disabled; subject to an appropriation, requiring the department to develop a child protective investigator and case manager recruitment program for a specified purpose; subject to an appropriation and beginning on a specified date, requiring the department to develop a 4-year pilot program for treatment foster care; specifying that incentives provided to state employees for participating in research or evaluation with the Florida Institute for Child Welfare do not violate certain laws or require certain reporting, etc.
