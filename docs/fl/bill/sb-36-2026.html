SB 36 (2026) · Use of Professional Nursing Titles

Allows licensed advanced practice nurses with specified doctoral degrees to use doctor titles, but requires advertising to also state their nursing license.

What the bill does (FL bill summary (Senate bill page))

Use of Professional Nursing Titles; Providing that licensed advanced practice registered nurses who hold specified degrees may use titles and abbreviations associated with those degrees in a specified manner; prohibiting such persons from holding themselves out to patients or prospective patients as a doctor in advertising without also clearly denoting their licensed profession, etc.
