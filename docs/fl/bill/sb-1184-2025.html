SB 1184 (2025) · Residual Market Insurers

Revises residual market insurance rules, bars post-loss benefit assignments on property policies, and conditions third-party liability suits on a prior judgment or settlement.

What the bill does (FL bill summary (Senate bill page))

Residual Market Insurers; Revising the conditions for insurance coverage to be eligible for export; transferring to surplus lines insurance those provisions relating to liability of insureds and deductibles; prohibiting policyholders from assigning post-loss insurance benefits under property insurance policies; requiring settlements or verdicts against insureds as a condition precedent to the accrual or maintenance of causes of actions against liability insurers by persons who are not insureds, etc.
