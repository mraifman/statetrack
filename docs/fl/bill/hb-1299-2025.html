HB 1299 (2025) · Department of Health

Revises the repeal date of the mRNA vaccine definition, requires medical marijuana centers to report theft or loss, and revises MOBILE Act licensure eligibility.

What the bill does (FL bill summary (Senate bill page))

Department of Health ; Revising the repeal date of the definition of the term "messenger ribonucleic acid vaccine"; providing for contingent retroactive operation; requiring medical marijuana treatment centers to notify the Department of Health through e-mail within a specified timeframe after an actual or attempted theft, diversion, or loss of marijuana; revising eligibility criteria for licensure by endorsement under the MOBILE Act, etc.
