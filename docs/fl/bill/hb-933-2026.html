HB 933 (2026) · Children's Initiatives

Establishes two children's initiatives run by nonprofits that must follow public records, meeting and procurement rules, and will build community child and family service networks.

What the bill does (FL bill summary (Senate bill page))

Children's Initiatives; Establishes Bay County 32401 Children's Initiative in Bay County & Pompano RYZE Children's Initiative in Broward County; provides for projects to be managed by corporations not for profit that are not subject to control, supervision, or direction by any department of state; requires corporations to be subject to state public records & meeting requirements & procurement of commodities & contractual services requirements; requires designated children's initiatives to assist in creation of community-based service networks & programming that provides certain services for children & families residing in disadvantaged areas of state; provides for evaluation, fiscal management, & oversight of projects.
