SB 182 (2026) · Education

Bars certain charter schools from dismissing students for academic performance, requires cursive instruction in specified grades, and creates a teacher training and mentoring program.

What the bill does (FL bill summary (Senate bill page))

Education; Providing that students may not be dismissed from certain charter schools based on academic performance; requiring students in specified grades to receive instruction in cursive writing; requiring, subject to legislative appropriation, each district school board to adopt rules to require the display of portraits of George Washington and Abraham Lincoln at each public school in the district; establishing the School Teacher Training and Mentoring Program within the Department of Education, etc.
