HB 4077 (2026) · City of Punta Gorda, Charlotte County

Redraws Punta Gorda Utility District boundaries and shifts some utility functions to Charlotte County, requiring interlocal agreements and continued water and wastewater service.

What the bill does (FL bill summary (Senate bill page))

City of Punta Gorda, Charlotte County; Provides boundaries of Punta Gorda Utility District; authorizes Charlotte County to assume responsibility for utility functions in lands outside district; authorizes City of Punta Gorda to continue to own & maintain assets outside district; requires city & county to enter into interlocal agreements for specified purposes; provides reporting requirements; requires city to continue to provide potable water & wastewater utility service to certain customers & to maintain potable water & wastewater utility service infrastructure; requires continuation of powers, duties, & obligations of district & city until transfer.
