What the bill does (FL bill summary (Senate bill page))

Educator Preparation; Requiring the Department of Education to establish a workgroup to update and revise the Florida Educator Accomplished Practices; requiring the department to submit workgroup findings to the Governor and the Legislature by a certain date; establishing guidelines for teacher preparation program uniform core curricula, etc.
