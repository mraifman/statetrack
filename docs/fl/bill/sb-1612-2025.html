What the bill does (FL bill summary (Senate bill page))

Financial Services; Revising the conditions for insurance coverage to be eligible for export; requiring state financial institutions to pay a semiannual assessment for specified time periods; authorizing the office to issue a specified certificate under certain circumstances; authorizing certain elected officers, directors, or committee members of a credit union to be reimbursed for certain expenses, etc.
