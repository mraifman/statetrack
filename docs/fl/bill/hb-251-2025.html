HB 251 (2025) · Holocaust Remembrance Day

Requires the Governor to annually proclaim Holocaust Remembrance Day and authorizes its observance in public schools and by public exercise.

What the bill does (FL bill summary (Senate bill page))

Holocaust Remembrance Day; Requires Governor to proclaim specified day annually as "Holocaust Remembrance Day"; authorizes "Holocaust Remembrance Day" to be observed in this state's public schools & be observed by public exercise as Governor may designate; authorizes specified instruction.
