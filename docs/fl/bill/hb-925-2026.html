HB 925 (2026) · Clerks of the Court

Lets clerks of court use excess funds in their combined budgets and shifts some penalty revenue from municipalities to the fine and forfeiture fund.

What the bill does (FL bill summary (Senate bill page))

Clerks of the Court; Authorizing the cumulative excess of funds to be used in the development of the total combined budgets of the clerks of the court; conforming provisions to changes made by the act; revising the definition of the term “publicly accessible website”; increasing the percentage of certain penalties which must be deposited into the fine and forfeiture fund and decreasing the percentage of certain penalties that must be paid to a municipality, etc.
