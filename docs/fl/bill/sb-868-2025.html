SB 868 (2025) · Social Media Use by Minors

Requires social media platforms to offer decryption for warrant-backed law enforcement requests, give parents access to minors' messages, and bars minors from disappearing messages.

What the bill does (FL bill summary (Senate bill page))

Social Media Use by Minors; Requiring social media platforms to provide a mechanism to decrypt end-to-end encryption when law enforcement obtains a warrant or subpoena; requiring social media platforms to allow a parent or legal guardian of a minor account holder to view all messages; prohibiting minor account holders from using or accessing messages that are designed to disappear or self destruct, or are ephemeral in nature, etc.
