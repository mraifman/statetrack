What is the Governor vetoing?

Of 514 bills that reached the Governor in the 2025–26 session, 21 were vetoed (4.1%) and 493 were signed. This page shows which topics, directions and authors the vetoes fall on.

493Became Law

Direction compares each bill with current Florida law, as everywhere else on this site. The Governor’s veto messages (the stated reasons) are not part of this data; each bill shows its own summary and votes.

Where the Governor lands

On each topic, the hollow circle is the average direction of the bills the Governor signed and the filled circle is the average of the bills the Governor vetoed, measured against current law.
