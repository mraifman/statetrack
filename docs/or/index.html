How to read the meter

Every direction is measured against the status quo: current Oregon law, as the measure summary describes it. Permissive means the bill moves the law toward more permission, access or fewer rules. Restrictive means it moves toward less permission or more rules. A bill that continues, studies or clarifies current law is neutral and is left out of the averages. By default the site leaves out the 2923 bills the official record marks as died, failed in committee or vetoed. Bills still listed as in committee cannot pass once the session has ended, but they are only hidden if the record says they died.

Where each topic leans

Each marker is the average direction of the bills tagged under that topic, from restrictive to permissive. Neutral and unclear tags are left out. A marker near the middle can hide a split: open the topic to see access and regulation separately.
