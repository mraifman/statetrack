Find my legislators

Enter your street address and city to see who represents you in the Oregon Legislature, then what each of them has authored and how they have voted.

Addresses are matched by the U.S. Census Bureau’s geocoder, called from your browser; StateTrack never sees them.

Demo for Oregon’s 2025–26 session. Bill data: Oregon Legislature OData service. Topics and directions are labelled by an AI model from the bill’s title and the measure summary, and are approximate. How it works
