What the bill does (OR measure summary (OLIS plain-language digest + catchline))

The Act specifies which federal rules BOLI must use when adopting rules that set work hour limits for minors. Clarifies which federal rules apply to the scope of the rulemaking authority of the Bureau of Labor and Industries regarding rules that govern the hours a minor can work.
