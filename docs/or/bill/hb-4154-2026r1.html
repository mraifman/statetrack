HB 4154 (2026R1) · Relating to student attendance data; and declaring an emergency.

Makes student attendance data collected by schools public and requires compiling data on regular attenders and chronically absent students.

What the bill does (OR measure summary (OLIS plain-language digest + catchline))

Makes public data that are collected about students going to school. Requires the compilation of data about students who are regular attenders or who are chronically absent. [<i>Takes effect July 1, 2026.</i>] <b>Declares an emergency, effective on passage.</b>
