HB 4031 (2026R1) · Relating to the siting of renewable energy facilities; and prescribing an effective date.

Exempts certain federally tax-credit-eligible renewable energy facilities from the EFSC site certificate if they are placed in service by 2030 and meet local land use and construction deadlines.

What the bill does (OR measure summary (OLIS plain-language digest + catchline))

Creates an exception to needing a site certificate from EFSC. Exempts an energy facility from needing a site certificate from the Energy Facility Siting Council if the energy facility produces power from a renewable energy source, qualifies for certain federal renewable energy tax credits and <b>is placed in service on or before December 31, 2030, or a date allowed by exception and a local land use application for the facility is submitted </b>[<i>construction begins</i>] on or before December 31, 2028. Takes effect on the 91st day following adjournment sine die.
