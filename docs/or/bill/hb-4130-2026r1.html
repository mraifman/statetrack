HB 4130 (2026R1) · Relating to farm use.

Extends the farm-use property tax special assessment to land under processing facilities and clarifies what preparing farm products means.

What the bill does (OR measure summary (OLIS plain-language digest + catchline))

<b>Digest: The Act would grant the tax break for land that is in "farm use" to land under a processing facility. The Act would clarify what "preparing" farmland products and by-products means in the context of the tax break.</b> [<i>Digest: The Act would clarify what "preparing" farmland products and by-products means in the context of the tax break for land that is in "farm use.".</i>] <b>Allows property tax special assessment for land under processing facilities.</b> Clarifies<b>, for purposes of the special assessment,</b> the meaning of "preparing" products or by-products raised for human or animal use on a farm unit for purposes of the definition of "farm use."
