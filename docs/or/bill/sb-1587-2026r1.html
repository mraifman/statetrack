SB 1587 (2026R1) · Relating to data brokers; and prescribing an effective date.

Bars public bodies from disclosing personal information to data brokers unless the broker attests it won't be used to enforce federal immigration law.

What the bill does (OR measure summary (OLIS plain-language digest + catchline))

<b>Digest: The Act says that a public body may not give certain data to a data broker unless the data broker says that it will not be used to enforce immigration law. The Act provides certain exceptions.</b> [<i>Digest: The Act says that a public body may not give certain data to a data broker unless the data broker says that it will not be used to enforce immigration law. A public body can also give out data under public records law or a court order.</i>] Prohibits public bodies from disclosing personally identifiable information to a data broker unless the data broker attests that the information will not be sold or transferred to any entity that will use it to enforce federal immigration law. Does not apply if disclosure is required under public records law or by a court order.<b> Does not apply if disclosure is made under the same terms and conditions under which the information is available to the general public.</b> [<i>Declares an emergency, effective on passage.</i>] <b>Takes effect on the 91st day following adjournment sine die.</b>
