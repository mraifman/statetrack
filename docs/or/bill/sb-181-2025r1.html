SB 181 (2025R1) · Relating to animals.

Bars research facilities from using public funds for medically unnecessary lab research on dogs or cats in certain pain and distress categories, and orders a Department of Agriculture study.

What the bill does (OR measure summary (OLIS plain-language digest + catchline))

<b>Digest: The Act concerns lab research on dogs and cats. The Act says that research facilities may not use public funds for certain types of lab research.</b> [<i>Digest: The Act tells an agency to study rules related to animals. The Act tells the agency to report on the study.</i>] [<i>Directs the State Department of Agriculture to study rules related to animals. Directs the department to report to the committees or interim committees of the Legislative Assembly related to natural resources no later than September 15, 2026.</i>] <b>Prohibits a research facility from using public funds to directly fund medically unnecessary laboratory research on dogs or cats that is classified under certain pain and distress categories.</b>
