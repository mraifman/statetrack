HB 3694 (2025R1) · Relating to student athletes; and declaring an emergency.

Modifies Oregon law on compensation for student athletes at post-secondary institutions and declares an emergency, effective on passage.

What the bill does (OR measure summary (OLIS plain-language digest + catchline))

The Act updates the law related to pay for student athletes. Modifies provisions of law related to compensation for student athletes enrolled in a post-secondary institution of education. <b>Declares an emergency, effective on passage.</b>
