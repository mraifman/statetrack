Creates an ethics-law exception letting public officials be paid by post-secondary institutions for teaching courses.

What the bill does (OR measure summary (OLIS plain-language digest + catchline))

Allows a public official to get paid for teaching at certain schools. Provides an exception to the prohibition against a public official using official position or office for financial gain or avoidance of financial detriment. Permits a public official to receive compensation from a post-secondary institution in exchange for teaching one or more courses at the institution.
