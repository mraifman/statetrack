HB 3195 (2025R1) · Relating to children's advocacy centers; and declaring an emergency.

Removes the cap on how much a children's advocacy center can receive from a one-time grant fund and changes grant eligibility rules.

What the bill does (OR measure summary (OLIS plain-language digest + catchline))

The Act removes the limit on the maximum amount that a CAC can receive from the CAC One-Time Grant Fund. Modifies eligibility requirements for distributions from the Children's Advocacy Center One-Time Grant Fund. Declares an emergency, effective on passage.
