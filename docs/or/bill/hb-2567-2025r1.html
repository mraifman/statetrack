HB 2567 (2025R1) · Relating to heat pump programs.

Extends the rental home heat pump program to 2032, allows extra grant money and rural contractor incentives, and requires grantees to protect applicants' personal data.

What the bill does (OR measure summary (OLIS plain-language digest + catchline))

Directs the use of best practices to keep personal data under the heat pump program secure and private. Requires an eligible entity that receives a grant under the Heat Pump Deployment Program to agree to use best practices to maintain the security and confidentiality of personal information of individuals who apply for or receive financial assistance under the program from the eligible entity. <b>Allows the State Department of Energy to award additional grant moneys to an eligible entity utilizing an existing performance agreement if additional funds become available.</b> Allows the State Department of Energy to provide an additional incentive amount under the rental home heat pump program for contractors who install heat pumps in rural or frontier communities. Extends the sunset of the rental home heat pump program from January 2, 2026, to January 2, 2032. Requires the department to provide a report each even-numbered year to the Legislative Assembly on the grants and rebates under the program.
