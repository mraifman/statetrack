What the bill does (OR measure summary (OLIS plain-language digest + catchline))

The Act allows a parent to leave a newborn in a safety device if the device meets certain standards. Modifies the safe haven law to allow a parent to anonymously leave an infant in a newborn safety device. Allows an authorized facility to install a newborn safety device. Prescribes standards for newborn safety devices.
