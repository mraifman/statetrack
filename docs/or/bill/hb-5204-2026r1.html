What the bill does (OR measure summary (OLIS plain-language digest + catchline))

The Act makes budget changes for the current biennium. <b>Establishes and</b> modifies certain biennial appropriations made from the General Fund to specified state agencies and the Emergency Board. Establishes and modifies limitations on expenditures for certain biennial expenses for specified state agencies. Declares an emergency, effective on passage.
