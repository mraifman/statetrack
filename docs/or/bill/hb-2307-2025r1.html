What the bill does (OR measure summary (OLIS plain-language digest + catchline))

This Act exempts lamps purchased by school districts from a ban on sales of some fluorescent lamps. Exempts, until January 2, 2030, lamps purchased by a school district from the prohibition on the sale of certain fluorescent lamps. Declares an emergency, effective on passage.
