HB 3865 (2025R1) · Relating to telephone solicitations.

Restricts telemarketing calls and texts by limiting call hours and frequency, and bans misrepresenting caller identity or purpose under the Unlawful Trade Practices Act.

What the bill does (OR measure summary (OLIS plain-language digest + catchline))

Says that a person may not make a sales call by voice or text unless the person follows certain rules. Prohibits as an unlawful practice under the Unlawful Trade Practices Act a person's initiating a telephone solicitation outside specified hours or more than three separate times within a 24-hour period[<i>,</i>]<b> or</b> misrepresenting or falsifying the person's identity[<i>,</i>]<b> or</b> the purpose of the call [<i>or the location from which the person is calling or failing to make required disclosures or inquiries during the call</i>]. Includes text messaging within the definition of "telephone solicitation."
