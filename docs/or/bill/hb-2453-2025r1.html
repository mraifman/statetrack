HB 2453 (2025R1) · Relating to education; and declaring an emergency.

Removes the requirement that a school district's educational equity advisory committee advise the board and limits that committee's duties; takes effect immediately.

What the bill does (OR measure summary (OLIS plain-language digest + catchline))

<b>Digest: Limits the duties of an equity committee that serves a school district.</b> [<i>Digest: Directs ODE to study the state of education in this state.</i>] [<i>Requires the Department of Education to study the adequacy of public education in this state. Directs the department to submit findings to the interim committees of the Legislative Assembly related to education not later than September 15, 2026.</i>] <b>Removes the requirement that an educational equity advisory committee must advise a school district board. Declares an emergency, effective on passage.</b>
