SB 1539 (2026R1) · Relating to providing animal health care.

Lets individuals verify whether cattle are pregnant without a veterinary license in certain circumstances, with board rules and a 2031 sunset.

What the bill does (OR measure summary (OLIS plain-language digest + catchline))

<b>Digest: The Act says a person might be allowed to do certain jobs with animals, like checking if cows are pregnant, even if they are not veterinarians. Allows the OSVMEB to adopt rules to explain when this is okay.</b> [<i>Digest: The Act says a person might be allowed to do certain jobs with animals, like checking if cows are pregnant, even if they are not veterinarians. Allows the ODA to adopt rules to explain when this is okay.</i>] Allows an individual to verify whether cattle are pregnant without holding a valid license issued by the Oregon State Veterinary Medical Examining Board under certain circumstances. Permits the [<i>State Department of Agriculture</i>]<b> board</b> to adopt rules. <b>Sunsets on January 1, 2031.</b>
