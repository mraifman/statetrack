What the bill does (OR measure summary (OLIS plain-language digest + catchline))

The Act lets local officials act on certain budgets after saying they have a conflict. Permits local public officials to participate in discussion and debate and to vote on the adoption of a local budget that includes compensation for the public official or a relative of the public official after announcing an actual conflict of interest.<b> Declares an emergency, effective on passage.</b>
