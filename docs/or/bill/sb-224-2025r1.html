SB 224 (2025R1) · Relating to addresses of individuals associated with candidate campaign committees.

Bars the Secretary of State from publishing residence addresses of individuals affiliated with political committees on the state's electronic filing system.

What the bill does (OR measure summary (OLIS plain-language digest + catchline))

<b>Digest: The Act keeps the SOS from posting the address of those persons who are linked to a political committee on the SOS website.</b> [<i>Digest: The Act keeps the SOS from posting the address of those persons who are linked to a campaign committee on the secretary's website.</i>] Prohibits the Secretary of State from publishing the residence address of certain individuals who are affiliated with a [<i>candidate's principal campaign</i>] <b>political</b> committee on the electronic filing system maintained by the secretary.
