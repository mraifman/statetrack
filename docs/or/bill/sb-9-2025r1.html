SB 9 (2025R1) · Relating to taking the drive test.

Directs rural Oregon DMV field offices to prioritize drive test requests from applicants living in rural Oregon or within 50 miles.

What the bill does (OR measure summary (OLIS plain-language digest + catchline))

The Act says that the DMV should prioritize drive tests offered in rural Oregon to folks who live in rural Oregon. Directs the field offices of the Department of Transportation located in rural counties to prioritize requests for drive tests when the request is from an applicant who lives in rural Oregon or within 50 miles of the field office.
