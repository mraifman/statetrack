HB 2492 (2025R1) · Relating to courts.

Changes the process for setting aside convictions, dismissals, contempt findings and insanity judgments, lengthens the wait for some contempt set-asides, and bars set-aside of traffic dismissals.

What the bill does (OR measure summary (OLIS plain-language digest + catchline))

<b>Digest: The Act changes the process for a person to set aside offenses and contempt.</b> [<i>Digest: The Act tells the State Court Administrator to look at courts and make a report.</i>] [<i>Requires the State Court Administrator to study courts and submit findings to the interim committees of the Legislative Assembly related to the judiciary not later than September 15, 2026.</i>] [<i>Sunsets on January 2, 2027.</i>] <b>Modifies the process for setting aside convictions, dismissals and contempt of court findings and guilty except for insanity judgments. Increases the waiting period for setting aside certain types of contempt of court findings. Provides that the required time period prior to filing the motion, during which the person is required to have no convictions or specified contempt findings, applies to motions to set aside convictions only. Provides that the dismissal of a traffic violation citation may not be set aside.</b>
