What the bill does (OR measure summary (OLIS plain-language digest + catchline))

The Act would let a surviving spouse claim the unused amount of the first spouse's estate tax exclusion. Allows an exclusion from the Oregon taxable estate of a surviving spouse for the unused portion of exclusion that applied to the prior deceased spouse of the decedent. Applies to estates of decedents who die on or after January 1, 2026. Takes effect on the 91st day following adjournment sine die.
