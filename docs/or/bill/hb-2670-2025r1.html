HB 2670 (2025R1) · Relating to education for children with a traumatic brain injury.

Broadens the definition of brain injury as a health impairment, making more children with brain injuries eligible for special education.

What the bill does (OR measure summary (OLIS plain-language digest + catchline))

Expands the types of brain injuries that can cause a child to receive special education. Broadens the description of what constitutes a [<i>brain injury</i>] <b>health impairment</b> for determining when a child is considered a child with a disability for purposes of qualifying for special education.
