HB 2306 (2025R1) · Relating to courts.

Lets any county create a justice of the peace district including the county seat, and raises the marriage solemnization fee with CPI indexing.

What the bill does (OR measure summary (OLIS plain-language digest + catchline))

<b>Digest: The Act lets any county have a justice of the peace district that includes the county seat. The Act ups a charge for getting married and indexes it to the CPI.</b> [<i>Digest: The Act lets Marion County have a justice of the peace district that includes the county seat. The Act ups a charge for getting married and indexes it to the CPI.</i>] Allows [<i>the Marion County</i>] <b>a county court or</b> board of county commissioners <b>in any county</b> to establish a justice of the peace district that includes the county seat. Increases the personal payment that may be charged for marriage solemnization and directs the State Court Administrator to adjust the payment amount to reflect changes in the Consumer Price Index.
