HB 4052 (2026R1) · Relating to a tax credit for de novo banks; and prescribing an effective date.

Creates a three-year corporate excise tax credit for newly commenced Oregon-chartered de novo banks opening between 2027 and 2033.

What the bill does (OR measure summary (OLIS plain-language digest + catchline))

The Act makes a new tax credit for new banks. Creates a corporate excise tax credit for each of the first three years that a bank does business in this state. Applies only to Oregon-chartered banks that commence business in tax years beginning on or after January 1, 2027, and before January 1, 2033. Takes effect on the 91st day following adjournment sine die.
