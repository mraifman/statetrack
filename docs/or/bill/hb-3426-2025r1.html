HB 3426 (2025R1) · Relating to unmanned aircraft systems.

Makes it an offense to use a drone to interfere with firefighting or search and rescue, and modifies penalties for drone interference with emergency responders.

What the bill does (OR measure summary (OLIS plain-language digest + catchline))

<b>Digest: The Act says that it is an offense to use a drone to interfere with firefighting and search and rescue activities. The Act changes some penalties for use of drones.</b> [<i>Digest: The Act says that it is an offense to use a drone to interfere with firefighting and search and rescue activities.</i>] Expands offenses relating to the use of an unmanned aircraft system to interfere with official duties to include firefighting and search and rescue efforts. <b>Modifies penalties for using an unmanned aircraft system to interfere with law enforcement, firefighting, search and rescue or emergency response efforts.</b>
