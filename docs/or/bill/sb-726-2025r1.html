SB 726 (2025R1) · Relating to landfill emissions monitoring; and prescribing an effective date.

Requires operators of Benton County municipal solid waste landfills to monitor surface methane emissions and report the data.

<b>Digest: This Act tells the EQC to make landfills in Benton County test and fix methane gas leaks.</b> [<i>Digest: This Act tells the EQC to make landfills test and fix methane gas leaks.</i>] Requires the owner or operator of a municipal solid waste landfill <b>located in Benton County</b> to conduct surface emissions monitoring and report data as specified in the Act.
