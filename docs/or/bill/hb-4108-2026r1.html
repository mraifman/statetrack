HB 4108 (2026R1) · Relating to annexation of land noncontiguous to a city.

Allows the City of Eugene to annex noncontiguous land when all landowners petition and four eligibility criteria are met.

What the bill does (OR measure summary (OLIS plain-language digest + catchline))

This Act lets Eugene add some noncontiguous land to the city. Allows the City of Eugene to annex noncontiguous land upon receipt of a petition from all owners of the land that satisfies four eligibility criteria.
