HB 2336 (2025R1) · Relating to internal audits.

What the bill does (OR measure summary (OLIS plain-language digest + catchline))

The Act modifies the time for audits by state agencies and reporting on audits by DAS. Modifies the timing of internal audits performed by state agencies and reporting on internal audits by the Oregon Department of Administrative Services.
