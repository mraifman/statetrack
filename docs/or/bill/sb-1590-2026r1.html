SB 1590 (2026R1) · Relating to public lands; and declaring an emergency.

Prohibits state public bodies from assisting federal privatization of certain federally owned public lands, sunsetting in 2032, effective immediately.

What the bill does (OR measure summary (OLIS plain-language digest + catchline))

This Act prohibits state public bodies from helping the feds sell their public lands. Prohibits public bodies from assisting the federal government with privatization of certain federally owned lands. Sunsets January 2, 2032. Declares an emergency, effective on passage.
