HB 2343 (2025R1) · Relating to the Columbia Basin endorsement.

Extends sunsets on Columbia Basin endorsement fees for fishing licenses, the fund receiving those fees, and the youth and pioneer license exemptions.

“Extends a sunset on the Columbia River Fisheries Enhancement Fund.”

What the bill does (OR measure summary (OLIS plain-language digest + catchline))

The Act extends a sunset on a fund. The Act extends a sunset on certain agency authority. The Act extends a sunset on an exemption. Extends a sunset on the Columbia River Fisheries Enhancement Fund. Extends a sunset on State Fish and Wildlife Commission authority to charge for endorsements to certain Columbia Basin fishing licenses and deposit endorsement moneys in the fund. Extends a sunset on youth license and pioneer license exemptions from endorsement charges.
