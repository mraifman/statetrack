HB 2541 (2025R1) · Relating to rights of certain agricultural workers to express milk in the workplace; and declaring an emergency.

Gives certain agricultural workers the right to express breast milk at work, adding workplace accommodation obligations.

What the bill does (OR measure summary (OLIS plain-language digest + catchline))

The Act changes laws regarding expressing breast milk at work. The Act takes effect when the Governor signs it. Provides certain agricultural workers with rights to express breast milk in the workplace. Declares an emergency, effective on passage.
