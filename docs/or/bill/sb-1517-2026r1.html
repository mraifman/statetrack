SB 1517 (2026R1) · Relating to civil litigation; and declaring an emergency.

Lets sports, fitness and recreation operators require participants to waive ordinary negligence claims, except certain non-waivable claims, and makes inherent risk a legal question.

What the bill does (OR measure summary (OLIS plain-language digest + catchline))

<b>Digest: The Act says that an operator can make a person doing sports, fitness or recreation waive some claims for negligence. The Act says that some types of claims may not be waived. The Act says that whether a risk is inherent to an activity is a question of law.</b> [<i>Digest: The Act says that an operator can make a person doing sports, fitness or recreation waive some claims for negligence. The Act says that some types of claims may not be waived.</i>] Provides that an operator may require a participant in a sport, fitness or recreational activity to release the operator for claims for ordinary negligence under certain circumstances. Specifies certain types of claims that may not be waived. <b>Provides that in a claim for damages for injuries sustained while a participant is engaged in a sport, fitness or recreational activity, whether a risk is inherent to the activity is a question of law.</b> Declares an emergency, effective on passage.
