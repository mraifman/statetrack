SB 217 (2025R1) · Relating to the paying of net metering customer-generators for the excess kilowatt-hours generated during a billing period.

Requires utilities to pay net-metering customer-generators for excess kilowatt-hours and makes donating those credits to low-income assistance optional.

What the bill does (OR measure summary (OLIS plain-language digest + catchline))

Makes a power company pay a person for excess power that the person makes through net metering. Requires an electric utility to pay a net metering customer-generator for the excess kilowatt-hours generated during a billing period. Removes the requirement that a customer-generator grant, but allows a customer-generator to donate, any credit for excess kilowatt-hours generated by the customer-generator to the electric utility's low-income assistance program.
