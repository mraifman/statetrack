What the bill does (OR measure summary (OLIS plain-language digest + catchline))

The Act would make new laws for deductions from public employees' wages to recoup amounts that were paid in error. Provides conditions and procedures under which a public employer may deduct amounts of erroneous overpayments from a public employee's wages.
