HB 3582 (2025R1) · Relating to statutes of limitation; and declaring an emergency.

Eliminates the statute of limitations for civil claims based on child sexual abuse or sexual assault, applying prospectively, and takes effect on passage.

What the bill does (OR measure summary (OLIS plain-language digest + catchline))

<b>Digest: The Act changes the law about the time limit for some claims based on allowing, permitting or encouraging child abuse and sexual assault. The Act gets rid of the time limit for claims based on child sexual abuse or sexual assault.</b> [<i>Digest: The Act gets rid of the time limit for filing claims based on sexual assault or child sexual abuse.</i>] <b>Modifies the qualifications for certain statutes of limitations for civil actions based on allowing, permitting or encouraging child abuse and sexual assault. Prospectively</b> eliminates the statute of limitations for civil actions based on sexual assault or child sexual abuse. <b>Declares an emergency, effective on passage.</b>
