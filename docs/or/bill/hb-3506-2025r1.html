What the bill does (OR measure summary (OLIS plain-language digest + catchline))

This Act transfers money from a tax deferral account to OHA's Healthy Homes fund and to DOR to promote tax deferrals. Transfers moneys from the Senior Property Tax Deferral Revolving Account to the Healthy Homes Repair Fund for the purpose of supporting seniors and individuals with disabilities and to the Department of Revenue to increase public knowledge of property tax deferral programs.
