HB 2649 (2025R1) · Relating to tuition and fees for public universities; and declaring an emergency.

Lets certain public university boards set special tuition and fees for qualifying students in an East Asia cooperation program, without HECC approval.

What the bill does (OR measure summary (OLIS plain-language digest + catchline))

<b>Digest: The Act allows certain higher learning schools to set tuition rates for certain students from East Asia.</b> [<i>Digest: The Act lets certain higher learning schools increase their costs without HECC approval.</i>] [<i>Permits certain public universities in this state to increase tuition and fees without the approval of the Higher Education Coordinating Commission.</i>] <b>Permits the governing boards of certain universities in this state to establish a tuition rate for certain qualifying students participating in a program developed by the boards for cooperation in higher education and economic development with certain countries in East Asia. Requires the rate of tuition to be a specified amount. Permits the boards to establish fees for services used by the students and the requirements for a student to be qualified. Declares an emergency, effective on passage.</b>
