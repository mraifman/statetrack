SB 64 (2025R1) · Relating to payment of dependent care tax credits in installments; and prescribing an effective date.

Directs ODHS to seek a federal SNAP waiver so recipients can receive dependent care tax credits in advance installments; sunsets 2031.

What the bill does (OR measure summary (OLIS plain-language digest + catchline))

The Act tells ODHS to seek a waiver of certain federal SNAP requirements. Requires the Department of Human Services to seek a waiver of federal requirements in the Supplemental Nutrition Assistance Program in order to allow recipients of supplemental nutrition assistance to receive advance installment payments of the tax credit for dependent care expenses. Sunsets January 2, 2031. Takes effect on the 91st day following adjournment sine die.
