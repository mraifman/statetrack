SB 558 (2025R1) · Relating to cannabis market growth; and prescribing an effective date.

Expands cannabis licensees' ability to share samples at temporary events, sell wholesale at events, and transfer usable marijuana between producers.

What the bill does (OR measure summary (OLIS plain-language digest + catchline))

The Act lets some marijuana licensees give samples and sell some things at events and give samples to their workers. Allows a marijuana licensee to provide samples of marijuana items to other marijuana licensees at temporary events registered with the Oregon Liquor and Cannabis Commission. Allows the commission to establish by rule a temporary events registration system. Allows a marijuana wholesaler to sell marijuana items to a marijuana retailer at a temporary event. Allows a marijuana producer to provide samples of seeds and immature marijuana plants to permitted workers. Allows a marijuana [<i>producer, marijuana processor and marijuana wholesaler</i>] <b>licensee</b> to provide samples of marijuana items in specified amounts to permitted workers. <b>Allows a marijuana producer to transfer usable marijuana to or receive usable marijuana from another marijuana producer.</b> Takes effect on the 91st day following adjournment sine die.
