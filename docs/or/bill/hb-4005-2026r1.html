HB 4005 (2026R1) · Relating to Water Professionals Appreciation Week; and prescribing an effective date.

Designates the first weekend of October each year as Water Professionals Appreciation Week to celebrate state water workers.

What the bill does (OR measure summary (OLIS plain-language digest + catchline))

Makes a yearly celebration of water workers in this state that takes place in October. Designates the first Saturday of October of each year through the end of the Sunday of the following weekend as Water Professionals Appreciation Week. <b>Takes effect on the 91st day following adjournment sine die.</b>
