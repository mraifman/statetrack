HB 3342 (2025R1) · Relating to water; and prescribing an effective date.

Modifies Oregon water rights regulation and administration; the text gives no specifics, and the change takes effect 91 days after sine die.

What the bill does (OR measure summary (OLIS plain-language digest + catchline))

The Act changes laws related to the regulation of water rights in this state. The Act goes into effect 91 days after sine die. Modifies provisions of law related to the regulation and administration of water rights in this state. Takes effect on the 91st day following adjournment sine die.
