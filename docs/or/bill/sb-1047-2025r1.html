SB 1047 (2025R1) · Relating to access to resources for development in Curry County; and declaring an emergency.

Requires Curry County and the Water Resources Department to expedite permit reviews for specified lands and appropriates money to Curry County for that work.

What the bill does (OR measure summary (OLIS plain-language digest + catchline))

This Act makes the county and OWRD speed review of permits for specific projects. Requires Curry County and the Water Resources Department to expedite review of applications for use on specified lands. [<i>Appropriates moneys to the Oregon Department of Administrative Services for distribution to Curry County to expedite review.</i>] Declares an emergency, effective on passage.
