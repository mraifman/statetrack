Lets the Higher Education Coordinating Commission seek court injunctions against career schools or persons violating licensure requirements and standards.

What the bill does (OR measure summary (OLIS plain-language digest + catchline))

Allows the HECC to seek a court order to require career schools to comply with certain laws. Permits the Higher Education Coordinating Commission to seek an injunction against a person or career school that is engaged, has engaged or is about to engage in any activity in violation of career school licensure requirements and standards. Specifies what an injunction may require.
