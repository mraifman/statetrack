HB 4120 (2026R1) · Relating to smoking policies in residential tenancies.

Lets landlords of multifamily housing convert premises to nonsmoking, including for existing tenants, on 180 days' written notice.

What the bill does (OR measure summary (OLIS plain-language digest + catchline))

This Act lets landlords restrict smoking in apartments. Allows residential landlords of multifamily housing to convert premises to nonsmoking for existing tenants upon [<i>90</i>]<b> 180</b> days' written notice. Applies to new and existing rental agreements.
