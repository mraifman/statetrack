SB 904 (2025R1) · Relating to self-insured employers.

Lets school districts apply for exemption from the financial-security rules that self-insured employers must meet for workers' compensation claims.

What the bill does (OR measure summary (OLIS plain-language digest + catchline))

The Act would let school districts apply to be exempt from the law that requires self-insured employers to submit proof that they will be able pay workers' comp claims. Includes school districts among the self-insured employers that may apply for exemption from the rules requiring such employers to demonstrate financial viability and provide security to the Director of the Department of Consumer and Business Services.
