HB 2806 (2025R1) · Relating to license fees for commercial instruments.

Makes permanent the Agriculture Department's authority to raise commercial weighing and measuring instrument license fees up to 3% yearly, plus temporary extra increases.

What the bill does (OR measure summary (OLIS plain-language digest + catchline))

The Act allows an agency to increase certain license fees. Makes permanent the authority of the State Department of Agriculture to annually increase license fees for commercial weighing or measuring instruments by up to three percent, subject to certain maximum limits. Authorizes the department to increase the license fees by a certain additional percentage for two fiscal years.
