What the bill does (OR measure summary (OLIS plain-language digest + catchline))

The Act allows the State Fire Marshal to recover costs from persons who start fires. The Act allows certain actions related to cost recovery. Authorizes the State Fire Marshal to seek cost recovery for the actual cost of fire abatement. Authorizes the Attorney General to take certain actions to investigate liability for the actual cost of fire abatement, upon request by the State Fire Marshal. Authorizes deputy state fire marshals to take certain actions to preserve evidence and investigate liability for the actual cost of fire abatement.
