What the bill does (OR measure summary (OLIS plain-language digest + catchline))

The Act makes changes to the rental assistance program for certain people who are under 25 years old. Modifies eligibility criteria for the long-term rental assistance program for certain individuals under 25 years of age. Expands the list of individuals and entities the Housing and Community Services Department must consult with in designing the program.
