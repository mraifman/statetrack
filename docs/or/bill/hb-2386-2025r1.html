What the bill does (OR measure summary (OLIS plain-language digest + catchline))

Gives ODVA the power to make rules to carry out the laws on unclaimed remains of veterans and family. Provides the Department of Veterans' Affairs with authority to adopt rules to implement the laws concerning the unclaimed remains of veterans, veterans' spouses and dependents and survivors of veterans.
