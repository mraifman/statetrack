HB 2748 (2025R1) · Relating to the use of nursing titles.

Prohibits nonhuman entities, such as AI systems, from using nursing titles and abbreviations.

What the bill does (OR measure summary (OLIS plain-language digest + catchline))

The Act says that an entity that is not a human may not call itself a nurse or similar title. Prohibits a nonhuman entity from using [<i>the title "nurse" or other similar titles</i>] <b>specified nursing titles and abbreviations</b>.
