HB 4072 (2026R1) · Relating to arraignment; and prescribing an effective date.

Changes the deadline for arraigning released persons and excludes specified periods from that deadline; takes effect 91 days after sine die.

What the bill does (OR measure summary (OLIS plain-language digest + catchline))

The Act changes the time period within which a person released from custody must be arraigned. The Act takes effect on the 91st day after sine die. Excludes specified periods of time from the time period within which a person released from custody must be arraigned. Takes effect on the 91st day following adjournment sine die.
