SB 504 (2025R1) · Relating to bioengineering for the protection of coastal resources.

Directs the Land Conservation and Development Commission to adopt guidance rules on nature-based, nonstructural shoreline stabilization by 2028, with General Fund money for that work.

What the bill does (OR measure summary (OLIS plain-language digest + catchline))

This Act makes LCDC, ODSL and OPRD adopt rules for nature-based solutions to protect the coast. Requires the Land Conservation and Development Commission to adopt rules by January 1, 2028, to provide guidance on nonstructural, nature-based solutions for shoreline stabilization. <b>Appropriates moneys from the General Fund to the Department of Land Conservation and Development for the purpose of adopting rules.</b> Allows the Department of State Lands and the State Parks and Recreation Department to adopt conforming rules by January 1, 2029.
