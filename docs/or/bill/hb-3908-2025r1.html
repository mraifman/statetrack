HB 3908 (2025R1) · Relating to party membership registration requirements; and declaring an emergency.

Raises the share of registered voters a party must have to qualify as a major political party from 5% to 10%, effective July 1, 2025.

What the bill does (OR measure summary (OLIS plain-language digest + catchline))

The Act increases the percentage of voters in the state required for a party to be a major party. Increases the party registration requirement to be a major political party in this state from five percent to 10 percent. Declares an emergency, effective July 1, 2025.
