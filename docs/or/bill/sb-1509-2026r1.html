SB 1509 (2026R1) · Relating to electors of President and Vice President of the United States; and prescribing an effective date.

Requires presidential electors to vote as pledged, lets unfaithful electors be replaced, sets elector qualifications, and pays mileage to attending electors.

What the bill does (OR measure summary (OLIS plain-language digest + catchline))

The Act allows for the switch of electors of President and Vice President if they do not vote like they pledged. Establishes the qualifications of an elector of President and Vice President of the United States. Provides the contents of a certificate of ascertainment of electors. Provides that an elector who does not vote in accordance with the signed pledge of electors vacates the office of elector. Provides for the filling of the vacancy. Provides for mileage expenses to be given to electors who attend the meeting of electors and whose ballot is not invalid. Takes effect on July 1, 2026.
