HB 3127 (2025R1) · Relating to reports of death.

Requires death reports to be filed with the Center for Health Statistics through the state's electronic reporting system, starting July 2027.

What the bill does (OR measure summary (OLIS plain-language digest + catchline))

The Act would tell people to report deaths to the state using an electronic system. Requires use of the state electronic reporting system to report deaths to the Center for Health Statistics. [<i>Takes effect July 1, 2027.</i>].
