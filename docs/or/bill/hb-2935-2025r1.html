What the bill does (OR measure summary (OLIS plain-language digest + catchline))

The Act tells the CJC to study pretrial release. The Act tells the CJC to provide the results of the study to the legislature by May 1, 2027. Directs the Oregon Criminal Justice Commission to study pretrial release and include certain data in the study. Requires the commission to provide the results of the study to the interim committees of the Legislative Assembly related to the judiciary no later than May 1, 2027. Sunsets January 2, 2028.
