HB 2679 (2025R1) · Relating to neonicotinoid pesticides; prescribing an effective date.

Restricts certain neonicotinoid pesticides to restricted-use status and bans their application on residential landscapes, with exceptions, operative January 1, 2027.

What the bill does (OR measure summary (OLIS plain-language digest + catchline))

This Act puts limits on the use of pesticides that have some neonicotinoids in them. Directs the State Department of Agriculture to classify certain pesticides containing neonicotinoids as restricted-use. Prohibits application of restricted-use neonicotinoid pesticides on residential landscapes, subject to certain exceptions. Becomes operative on January 1, 2027. Takes effect on the 91st day following adjournment sine die.
