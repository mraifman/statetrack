SB 827 (2025R1) · Relating to the Oregon Solar and Storage Rebate Program.

Expands the Oregon Solar and Storage Rebate Program to cover energy storage systems paired with previously installed solar systems.

Makes program give rebates for energy storage systems that will be paired with solar systems. Expands the Oregon Solar and Storage Rebate Program to offer rebates for energy storage systems that will be paired at the time of installation with previously purchased and installed solar electric systems.
