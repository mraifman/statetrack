HB 2807 (2025R1) · Relating to the liability of the Psychiatric Security Review Board.

Grants the Psychiatric Security Review Board immunity from liability for certain functions and for acts of persons under its jurisdiction, except wanton and willful misconduct.

What the bill does (OR measure summary (OLIS plain-language digest + catchline))

The Act says that the PSRB is immune from liability for some acts of the board and acts of persons let go by the board. Provides that the Psychiatric Security Review Board is immune from liability for the performance of certain functions of the board. Provides that, except when the board commits wanton and willful misconduct, the board is immune from liability for the acts and omissions of a person who is or was under the jurisdiction of the board.
