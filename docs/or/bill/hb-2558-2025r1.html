HB 2558 (2025R1) · Relating to activities regulated by the State Marine Board; and prescribing an effective date.

Requires crew members on Oregon guided marine trips to register with the State Marine Board and raises outfitter and guide registration fees.

Defines "crew member" for the purposes of outfitter and guide laws.</b> Increases the registration fee for all outfitters and guides. Becomes operative November 1, 2025.
