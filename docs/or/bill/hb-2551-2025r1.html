HB 2551 (2025R1) · Relating to security on campus.

Requires public university boards to ensure background checks from DPSST are requested and reviewed before commissioning special campus security officers, and lets DPSST share applicant information.

What the bill does (OR measure summary (OLIS plain-language digest + catchline))

The Act tells a public university to look at data from DPSST about a person who wants to be a campus security officer. The Act says that DPSST can share the data. Directs the governing board of a public university to [<i>request and review</i>] <b>ensure that</b> background information about special campus security officers <b>is requested from the Department of Public Safety Standards and Training and reviewed</b> before commissioning the officer. Allows the department [<i>of Public Safety Standards and Training</i>] to share information about applicants with a public university.
