HB 3544 (2025R1) · Relating to water.

Creates uniform procedures for hearing contested cases about water use, without changing substantive water rules.

What the bill does (OR measure summary (OLIS plain-language digest + catchline))

The Act creates a standard process for contested cases about the use of water. Establishes uniform processes for hearing contested case proceedings relating to the use of water.
