HB 3003 (2025R1) · Relating to Department of Human Services annual child welfare reporting.

Changes the list of data the Department of Human Services must include in its annual child welfare report on foster children.

What the bill does (OR measure summary (OLIS plain-language digest + catchline))

The Act makes changes to the data that DHS must put in its annual reports about foster children. Modifies the list of data that the Department of Human Services must include in its annual child welfare report.
