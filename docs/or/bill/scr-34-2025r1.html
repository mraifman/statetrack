What the bill does (OR measure summary (OLIS plain-language digest + catchline))

Sets bill limits and dates for bill requests for the 2026 short session. Establishes limitations and deadlines for legislative measures for the 2026 regular session of the Eighty-third Legislative Assembly. Provides that certain labor negotiations are not legislative deliberations and are to be conducted under executive session.
