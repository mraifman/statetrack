HB 3041 (2025R1) · Relating to the Educator Advancement Council; and prescribing an effective date.

Moves the Educator Advancement Council's legal basis from an intergovernmental agreement into state statute, with no stated change in its function.

What the bill does (OR measure summary (OLIS plain-language digest + catchline))

The Act changes the type of law that provides for a council related to educators. Modifies the source of law that establishes the Educator Advancement Council from intergovernmental agreement to state statute. Takes effect on the 91st day following adjournment sine die.
