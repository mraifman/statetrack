HB 2944 (2025R1) · Relating to penalties for failure to comply with certain requirements under the laws governing public employee collective bargaining.

Directs the Employment Relations Board to impose civil penalties on public employers with a history of violating public employee collective bargaining requirements, with exceptions and extenuating-circumstance considerations.

What the bill does (OR measure summary (OLIS plain-language digest + catchline))

The Act makes changes to the PECBA. The Act creates penalties for violating certain provisions of the PECBA. Directs the Employment Relations Board to impose civil penalties against a public employer that has a history of failing to comply with certain requirements under the Public Employee Collective Bargaining Act. <b>Provides that a violation resulting from certain events is not subject to a civil penalty.</b> Permits the board to consider extenuating circumstances when determining the amount of the civil penalty to impose.
