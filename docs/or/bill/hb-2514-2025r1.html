HB 2514 (2025R1) · Relating to education service district indebtedness.

Raises the cap on bond funds an education service district can raise for capital costs and lets it exceed component district limits.

What the bill does (OR measure summary (OLIS plain-language digest + catchline))

This Act raises the cap on the funds an education service district can raise for capital costs by issuing bonds. Permits an education service district to contract bonded indebtedness for capital costs in excess of limits applicable to component school districts.
