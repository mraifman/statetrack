What the bill does (OR measure summary (OLIS plain-language digest + catchline))

This Act requires the OHCS to create a statewide shelter program. Requires the Housing and Community Services Department to establish a statewide shelter program through which regional coordinators are selected to administer funding to shelter providers.<b> Sunsets January 2, 2034.</b> Requires the department to adopt rules on or before January 1, 2026, and to select regional coordinators on or before May 1, 2026. Declares an emergency, effective on passage.
