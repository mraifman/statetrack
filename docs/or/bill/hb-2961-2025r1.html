HB 2961 (2025R1) · Relating to the capacity for electric vehicle charging required in certain newly constructed buildings.

Requires newly constructed commercial, multifamily (10+ units) and mixed-use buildings in metro areas to install a set share of parking spaces with EV charging capacity.

What the bill does (OR measure summary (OLIS plain-language digest + catchline))
