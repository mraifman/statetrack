HB 2982 (2025R1) · Relating to small watercraft.

Raises some boat fees, deposits the money into an aquatic invasive species fund, and requires a waterway access permit for nonmotorized boats with exceptions.

What the bill does (OR measure summary (OLIS plain-language digest + catchline))

The Act increases some boat fees. The money raised will be put into a fund to prevent and control plants and animals that do not belong in the waters of this state. Increases boating fees and deposits moneys into the Aquatic Invasive Species Prevention Fund. Provides that a waterway access permit is required while operating a nonmotorized boat of any length.<b> Creates exceptions.</b>
