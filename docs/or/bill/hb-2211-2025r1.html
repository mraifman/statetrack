HB 2211 (2025R1) · Relating to dental subcontractors; and prescribing an effective date.

Defines dental subcontractors and directs the Oregon Health Authority to adopt requirements for those contracting with coordinated care organizations.

What the bill does (OR measure summary (OLIS plain-language digest + catchline))

The Act makes changes to laws about certain dental care providers. Defines a "dental subcontractor." Directs the Oregon Health Authority to adopt requirements for a dental subcontractor that contracts with a coordinated care organization. Takes effect on the 91st day following adjournment sine die.
