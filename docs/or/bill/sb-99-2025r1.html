SB 99 (2025R1) · Relating to property tax incentive benefit programs for brownfields; and prescribing an effective date.

Extends the sunset date for property tax incentive programs for brownfields and property on brownfields, keeping those breaks in place longer.

What the bill does (OR measure summary (OLIS plain-language digest + catchline))

The Act would extend the sunset of certain property tax breaks for brownfields. Extends the sunset for the property tax incentive benefit programs for brownfields and property on brownfields. Takes effect on the 91st day following adjournment sine die.
