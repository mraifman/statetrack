SB 1173 (2025R1) · Relating to product liability civil actions; and prescribing an effective date.

Shields certain entities that provide products as part of health care services from product liability suits, unless they were involved in designing or manufacturing the product.

What the bill does (OR measure summary (OLIS plain-language digest + catchline))

<b>Digest: The Act says that some product liability claims may not be filed against some entities that provide products as part of health care.</b> [<i>Digest: The Act says that some product liability claims may not be filed against a health care facility.</i>] Provides that [<i>a health care facility is</i>] <b>certain entities providing a product as part of health care services are</b> not a manufacturer, distributor, seller or lessor of [<i>a</i>] <b>the</b> product for purposes of a product liability civil action if the [<i>health care facility</i>] <b>entity</b> was not involved in the design or manufacture of the product.
