HB 2725 (2025R1) · Relating to the inclusion of ports in strategic investment program agreements; and prescribing an effective date.

Limits which types of port districts must be parties to strategic investment program agreements for eligible projects in their territory.

What the bill does (OR measure summary (OLIS plain-language digest + catchline))

The Act would limit the kind of port district that must be a party to a contract in the strategic investment program. Limits the kind of port districts that are required to be involved in strategic investment program agreements with respect to eligible projects that are located in the port district's territory. Takes effect on the 91st day following adjournment sine die.
