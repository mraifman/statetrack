HB 3024 (2025R1) · Relating to the reduction of unemployment benefits due to disqualification; and prescribing an effective date.

Eliminates the reduction in maximum unemployment benefits for workers disqualified for cause who have not earned four times their weekly benefit.

What the bill does (OR measure summary (OLIS plain-language digest + catchline))

The Act would get rid of the cut in maximum benefits when a worker is barred from benefits due to not working for cause and has not earned four times weekly benefits. Eliminates the reduction of an individual's maximum benefit amount by eight times the individual's weekly benefit amount after the individual is disqualified from the receipt of benefits for cause and has not earned at least four times the individual's weekly benefit amount. Takes effect on the 91st day following adjournment sine die.
