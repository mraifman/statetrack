HB 4069 (2026R1) · Relating to the safety of behavioral health workers; and prescribing an effective date.

Requires behavioral health employers to implement a written safety policy or plan protecting the physical safety of their workers.

What the bill does (OR measure summary (OLIS plain-language digest + catchline))

The Act requires a safety plan in certain mental health and SUD treatment settings. Requires a behavioral health employer to [<i>develop and</i>] implement a written safety <b>policy or</b> plan <b>for the physical safety of individuals working for the behavioral health employer</b>.
