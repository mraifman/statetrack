SB 537 (2025R1) · Relating to violence in health care settings.

Requires certain health care employers and home care settings to adopt workplace violence prevention measures, excludes Oregon State Hospital, and requires a DCBS incident report.

What the bill does (OR measure summary (OLIS plain-language digest + catchline))

The Act creates new laws for workplace violence prevention in health care settings. Creates workplace violence prevention requirements for certain health care entities. <b>Excludes the Oregon State Hospital from such requirements.</b> Requires the Department of Consumer and Business Services to prepare and submit a consolidated report of workplace violence incidents. Requires certain health care employers and home care settings to take certain actions to protect workers against risks of violence and other safety-related risks.
