HB 3064 (2025R1) · Relating to health care coverage beginning at perimenopause.

Requires certain health insurers and the state employee benefit boards to cover perimenopause, menopause and postmenopause treatment.

What the bill does (OR measure summary (OLIS plain-language digest + catchline))

Tells some health insurers, OEBB and PEBB to cover care for menopause symptoms. Requires certain health insurers, the Oregon Educators Benefit Board and the Public Employees' Benefit Board to cover treatment for perimenopause, menopause and postmenopause.
