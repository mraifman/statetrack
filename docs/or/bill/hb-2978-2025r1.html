What the bill does (OR measure summary (OLIS plain-language digest + catchline))

The Act makes changes about a program to reduce cars hitting wildlife. The Act tells agencies to create a related advisory group. Makes certain changes concerning a program to reduce wildlife-vehicle collisions. Instructs the Department of Transportation and the State Department of Fish and Wildlife to establish an advisory group. Makes certain changes related to reporting on the program.
