HB 2728 (2025R1) · Relating to member data under the Public Employees Retirement System; and prescribing an effective date.

Requires PERS to include information about overtime use in calculating a retiring member's final average salary in the member's letter.

What the bill does (OR measure summary (OLIS plain-language digest + catchline))

The Act tells PERS to put data about overtime in the letter sent to a retiring member of PERS. Directs the Public Employees Retirement System to include information about the use of overtime hours in calculating the final average salary of a member of the system who is retiring. Takes effect on the 91st day following adjournment sine die.
