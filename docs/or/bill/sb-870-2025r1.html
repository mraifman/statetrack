SB 870 (2025R1) · Relating to public records disclosure of OLCC permittee personal information.

Exempts the residential address and personal phone number of certain OLCC permit holders from public records disclosure.

What the bill does (OR measure summary (OLIS plain-language digest + catchline))

Makes the home address and phone number of some OLCC permittees exempt from public disclosure. Exempts from public records disclosure the residential address and personal phone number of an individual who holds a specified permit issued by the Oregon Liquor and Cannabis Commission.
