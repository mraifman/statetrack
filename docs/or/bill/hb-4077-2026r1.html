HB 4077 (2026R1) · Relating to the securitization of a public utility's insurance program; and prescribing an effective date.

Lets public utilities, with Public Utility Commission approval, securitize debt through bonds to fund self-insurance or captive insurance program costs.

Authorizes a public utility, upon approval by the Public Utility Commission, to issue bonds and securitize debt for costs and expenses incurred or to be incurred by the public utility associated with a self-insurance or captive insurance program. Takes effect on the 91st day following adjournment sine die.
