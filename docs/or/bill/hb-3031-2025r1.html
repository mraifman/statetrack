HB 3031 (2025R1) · Relating to housing; and declaring an emergency.

Creates a program letting the Oregon Infrastructure Finance Authority fund roads, pipes and other infrastructure supporting housing, with a new fund and a housing study.

What the bill does (OR measure summary (OLIS plain-language digest + catchline))

<b>Digest: This Act requires OIFA to finance roads and pipes to support new home building.</b> [<i>Digest: The Act tells OHCS to study housing.</i>] [<i>Requires the Housing and Community Services Department to study housing. Directs the department to submit findings to the interim committees of the Legislative Assembly related to housing not later than September 15, 2026.</i>] <b>Establishes a program through which the Oregon Infrastructure Finance Authority may provide financial assistance for infrastructure to support housing development. Establishes the Housing Infrastructure Project Fund for the program. Declares an emergency, effective July 1, 2025.</b>
