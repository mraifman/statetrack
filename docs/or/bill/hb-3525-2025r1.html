HB 3525 (2025R1) · Relating to exempt wells that supply ground water for domestic purposes.

Requires landlords renting dwellings with exempt wells in ground water quality management areas to test drinking water for arsenic, coliform, lead and nitrates.

What the bill does (OR measure summary (OLIS plain-language digest + catchline))

This Act requires landlords to test tenants' wells for contaminants. Requires, for each dwelling unit rented by a landlord for which the source of drinking water is an exempt well in a ground water <b>quality</b> management area, that the landlord collect and test samples of drinking water from the dwelling unit for arsenic, coliform bacteria, lead and nitrates. Requires the Oregon Health Authority to adopt implementation rules. Becomes operative on January 1, 2027.
