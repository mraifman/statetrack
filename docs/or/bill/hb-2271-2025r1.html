HB 2271 (2025R1) · Relating to employer taxes.

Gives employers a one-year credit against unemployment insurance taxes if their 2025 rate drops at least 2.5 points from 2024.

What the bill does (OR measure summary (OLIS plain-language digest + catchline))

<b>Digest: The Act would give a credit off their UI taxes to employers whose rate for 2025 is less than their rate for the prior year by at least two and a half percentage points.</b> [<i>Digest: The Act would give a credit against UI taxes to employers whose rate for 2025 is less than their rate for the prior year by at least three percentage points.</i>] Provides a credit against an employer's unemployment insurance taxes for calendar <b>year 2025</b> [<i>years 2025, 2026 and 2027</i>] if the employer's tax rate for calendar year 2025 is at least [<i>three</i>] <b>2.5</b> percentage points less than the employer's tax rate for calendar year 2024.
