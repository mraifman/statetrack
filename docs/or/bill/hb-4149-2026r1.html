HB 4149 (2026R1) · Relating to homeless students.

Requires school districts to adopt policies for immediate enrollment, placement and services for homeless students, and directs ODE to designate a state coordinator.

What the bill does (OR measure summary (OLIS plain-language digest + catchline))

Requires school districts and ODE to help homeless students. Directs school districts to adopt policies that provide for the immediate enrollment, school placement and provision of services to homeless students. Directs the Department of Education to designate a state coordinator for the education of homeless students.
