SB 817 (2025R1) · Relating to Land Use Board of Appeals fees.

Raises filing fees for some Land Use Board of Appeals notices of intent and motions to intervene, and eliminates fee awards to respondents when petitioners do not file.

What the bill does (OR measure summary (OLIS plain-language digest + catchline))

This bill raises fees for some LUBA filings. Increases fees for filing a notice of intent to appeal and a motion to intervene with the Land Use Board of Appeals. Eliminates the awarding of fees for filing a notice of intent to appeal with the board to the respondent if a petitioner does not file a petition for review.
