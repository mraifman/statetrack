What the bill does (OR measure summary (OLIS plain-language digest + catchline))

The Act allows the OBA to create a grant program for people to become CPAs. Allows the Oregon Board of Accountancy to create a grant program to provide scholarships for students seeking accounting degrees in college or individuals who wish to become certified public accountants in Oregon. Declares an emergency, effective July 1, 2025.
