What the bill does (OR measure summary (OLIS plain-language digest + catchline))

The Act says that some hearsay about some sex crimes can come into evidence though the speaker is available. Provides that certain hearsay statements related to certain sex crimes are not inadmissible in evidence, even though the declarant is available as a witness. Takes effect on the 91st day following adjournment sine die.
