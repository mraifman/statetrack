SB 1137 (2025R1) · Relating to autologous breast reconstruction.

Requires health benefit plans to cover autologous breast reconstruction and related services, with rules on cost-sharing, utilization review, reimbursement and network adequacy.

What the bill does (OR measure summary (OLIS plain-language digest + catchline))

This Act tells a health benefit plan to cover certain types of breast reconstruction services with certain rules. Requires health benefit plans to cover autologous breast reconstruction procedures and related services with specific requirements related to out-of-pocket costs, cost-sharing, utilization review, reimbursement rates and network adequacy.
