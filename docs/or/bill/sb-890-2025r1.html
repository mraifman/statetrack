SB 890 (2025R1) · Relating to the Oregon Sunshine Committee.

Changes Oregon Sunshine Committee report dates and reviewing committees, and disestablishes the public records subcommittee.

“Disestablishes the subcommittee on public records and changes the committee that reviews the Oregon Sunshine Committee reports”

What the bill does (OR measure summary (OLIS plain-language digest + catchline))

Makes changes to the OSC reports including dates and the committee which reviews the reports. Disestablishes the subcommittee on public records and changes the committee that reviews the Oregon Sunshine Committee reports to the committees or interim committees of the Legislative Assembly related to the judiciary. Modifies dates the reports from the Oregon Sunshine Committee are due.
