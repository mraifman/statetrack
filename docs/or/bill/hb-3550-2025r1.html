HB 3550 (2025R1) · Relating to an exemption for certain minor league baseball players from certain laws governing employment conditions.

Exempts minor league baseball players covered by a collective bargaining agreement from certain state employment-condition laws.

What the bill does (OR measure summary (OLIS plain-language digest + catchline))

The Act exempts certain sports players from some state laws about working conditions. Exempts from certain laws governing certain employment conditions minor league baseball players who are subject to a collective bargaining agreement that provides for payment of wages and other working conditions.
