HB 5018 (2025R1) · Relating to the financial administration of the State Board of Licensed Social Workers; and declaring an emergency.

Appropriates General Fund money to the State Board of Licensed Social Workers and caps its biennial spending from fees and other revenues.

What the bill does (OR measure summary (OLIS plain-language digest + catchline))

The Act creates an agency budget. [<i>Appropriates moneys from the General Fund to the State Board of Licensed Social Workers.</i>] Limits biennial expenditures from fees, moneys or other revenues, including Miscellaneous Receipts, but excluding lottery funds and federal funds, collected or received by the [<i>board</i>]<b> State Board of Licensed Social Workers</b>. Declares an emergency, effective July 1, 2025.
