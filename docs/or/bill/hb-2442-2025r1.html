HB 2442 (2025R1) · Relating to providing proof of United States citizenship when registering to vote.

Requires proof of U.S. citizenship for first-time voter registrants and repeals the state's electronic voter registration system.

What the bill does (OR measure summary (OLIS plain-language digest + catchline))

The Act tells a person to show certain forms to register to vote. The Act ends the electronic voter registration system. Requires evidence of citizenship for persons registering to vote for the first time in this state. Specifies when registration of qualified person occurs. Repeals the electronic voter registration system.
