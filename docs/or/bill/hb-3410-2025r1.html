HB 3410 (2025R1) · Relating to health care; and declaring an emergency.

Amends Oregon's medical corporate-practice and noncompete laws, makes the noncompete ban retroactive, and takes effect immediately as an emergency measure.

What the bill does (OR measure summary (OLIS plain-language digest + catchline))

Makes changes to an Act that keeps people from running both a company that does not do medical work and another entity that does medical work. Goes into effect right away. Amends portions of an Act that restricts management services organizations from owning a majority interest in professional medical entities and that prohibits noncompetition agreements, nondisparagement agreements and nondisclosure agreements between medical licensees and other persons. Makes a prohibition on the use of noncompetition agreements retroactive. Declares an emergency, effective on passage.
