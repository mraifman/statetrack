SB 430 (2025R1) · Relating to disclosures required in connection with online transactions.

Requires online sellers to display prices that include all mandatory fees, except government taxes and reasonable shipping costs, and enforces violations under the Unlawful Trade Practices Act.

What the bill does (OR measure summary (OLIS plain-language digest + catchline))

Says that a person may not show or offer a price for goods or services online that does not include all of the fees and charges that a buyer must pay. Says that taxes and fees to ship the goods or provide the services do not need to be shown. Prohibits a person that offers or sells goods or services online [<i>to a resident of this state</i>] from advertising, displaying or offering a price for the goods or services that does not include all fees or charges that a purchaser must pay to complete a transaction for the goods or services, other than taxes or fees that a governmental body imposes on the transaction<b>,</b> [<i>or</i>] reasonable charges that the person actually incurs to ship the goods or provide the services<b> or a service fee that is calculated according to distance or a purchaser's selections</b>. Specifies exemptions. Punishes a violation of the Act as a unlawful practice under the Unlawful Trade Practices Act. [<i>Takes effect on the 91st day following adjournment sine die.</i>].
