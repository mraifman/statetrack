HB 2942 (2025R1) · Relating to reimbursement of pharmacy services; and prescribing an effective date.

Requires OHA and coordinated care organizations to reimburse pharmacists the same as other health care providers for certain HIV-related services.

What the bill does (OR measure summary (OLIS plain-language digest + catchline))

Tells OHA and CCOs to pay pharmacists the same as health care providers for certain services related to HIV care. Requires the Oregon Health Authority and coordinated care organizations to reimburse pharmacies and pharmacists in the same manner as other health care providers for certain services related to HIV treatment. Takes effect on the 91st day following adjournment sine die.
