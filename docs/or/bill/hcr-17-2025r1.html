What the bill does (OR measure summary (OLIS plain-language digest + catchline))

Honors Cambodian Americans who live in Oregon and the U.S. and all of the lives that have been lost in war and conflict in that part of Asia. Recognizes and honors Cambodian Americans in Oregon and the United States and all those lost in the Cambodian Genocide.
