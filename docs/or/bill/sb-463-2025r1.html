SB 463 (2025R1) · Relating to reporting on the soundness of the state's Insurance Fund; and declaring an emergency.

Requires the Department of Administrative Services to report to the Legislature every even-numbered year on the soundness of the state's Insurance Fund.

What the bill does (OR measure summary (OLIS plain-language digest + catchline))

Tells a state agency to do a report about how well the state's Insurance Fund is doing. Requires the Oregon Department of Administrative Services to submit by January [<i>15</i>]<b> 31</b> of each even-numbered year a report to the Legislative Assembly concerning the soundness of the state's Insurance Fund. Specifies the contents of the report and directs agencies of state government and participants in the fund to cooperate with and assist the department in carrying out the department's duties under the Act. Declares an emergency, effective on passage.
