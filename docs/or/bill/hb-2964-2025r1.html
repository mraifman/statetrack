HB 2964 (2025R1) · Relating to affordable housing; and declaring an emergency.

Requires the Housing and Community Services Department to award loans for affordable housing predevelopment costs, adopt rules by June 2026, and appropriates General Fund money.

What the bill does (OR measure summary (OLIS plain-language digest + catchline))

<b>Digest: This Act makes OHCS provide loans for costs of low cost housing predevelopment.</b> [<i>Digest: This Act creates a temporary program for OHCS to award grants and loans for housing prep.</i>] Requires the Housing and Community Services Department to award [<i>grants</i>]<b> loans</b> for the predevelopment costs of affordable housing [<i>and a grant to assist nonprofits receiving grants or loans under this Act. Sunsets January 2, 2028</i>].<b> Requires the department to adopt initial rules by June 1, 2026, and to combine other existing predevelopment loan programs.</b> [<i>Establishes the Affordable Housing Predevelopment Loan Fund. Continuously appropriates moneys in the fund to the department to award loans for predevelopment costs of affordable housing.</i>] [<i>Appropriates moneys from the General Fund to the department for grants and loans.</i>] Declares an emergency, effective on passage.
