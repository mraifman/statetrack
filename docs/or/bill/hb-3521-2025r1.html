HB 3521 (2025R1) · Relating to residential tenancy hold deposits.

Requires landlords who revoke a rental offer after taking a hold deposit to pay minimum damages unless the deposit is returned within five business days.

What the bill does (OR measure summary (OLIS plain-language digest + catchline))

<b>Digest: This Act awards an applicant money from a landlord who breaks a promise to rent a home and does not return the deposit soon after.</b> [<i>Digest: This Act awards damages if a landlord revokes an offer of housing.</i>] Requires residential landlords to pay a minimum amount of damages for breaching an agreement to later execute a rental agreement <b>of a habitable dwelling unit</b> in exchange for holding a deposit<b>, unless the deposit is returned within five business days or repayment is excused based on an act of God</b>.
