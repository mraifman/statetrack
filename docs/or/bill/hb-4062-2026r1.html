What the bill does (OR measure summary (OLIS plain-language digest + catchline))

The Act would have Business Oregon look at its programs and try to streamline them. Requires the Director of the Oregon Business Development Department to evaluate the programs administered by the department and provide recommendations for streamlining them. Declares an emergency, effective on passage.
