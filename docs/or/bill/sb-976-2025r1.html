SB 976 (2025R1) · Relating to providing animal health care; and declaring an emergency.

Lets non-veterinarians verify cattle pregnancy without a state veterinary license in certain cases, with ODA rules, and creates an equine dentistry task force.

What the bill does (OR measure summary (OLIS plain-language digest + catchline))

The Act says a person might be allowed to check and see if cows are pregnant, even if the person is not a veterinarian. Allows the ODA to adopt rules to explain when this is okay. Allows an individual to verify whether cattle are pregnant without holding a valid license issued by the Oregon State Veterinary Medical Examining Board under certain circumstances. Permits the State Department of Agriculture to adopt rules. [<i>Establishes the Task Force on Equine Dentistry. Directs the task force to report on findings to the interim committees of the Legislative Assembly related to agriculture no later than December 15, 2025.</i>] Declares an emergency, effective on passage.
