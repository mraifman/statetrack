Lets the Employment Department director waive the unpaid waiting week for unemployment claimants unable to work due to a state-declared severe weather emergency.

What the bill does (OR measure summary (OLIS plain-language digest + catchline))

The Act would let the unpaid first week of an unemployment claim be waived for a worker who is away from work due to a severe weather emergency. Authorizes the Director of the Employment Department to waive the unpaid waiting week period for unemployment insurance claimants who are unable to perform services due a state-declared emergency related to severe weather. Takes effect on the 91st day following adjournment sine die.
