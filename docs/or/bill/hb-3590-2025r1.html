HB 3590 (2025R1) · Relating to pediatric care; prescribing an effective date.

Bars out-of-network pediatric providers from balance billing and requires insurers and state benefit boards to pay them like in-network providers in certain cases.

What the bill does (OR measure summary (OLIS plain-language digest + catchline))

This Act tells a children's care provider to bill the same for in-network and out-of-network care sometimes and makes some insurers, OEBB and PEBB pay these care providers in a way that is similar. Takes effect 91 days after it passes. Prohibits out-of-network providers from balance billing for pediatric care in certain circumstances. Requires insurers, the Oregon Educators Benefit Board and the Public Employees' Benefit Board to reimburse out-of-network providers in the same way as in-network providers for the same pediatric care and services in certain circumstances. Takes effect on the 91st day following adjournment sine die.
