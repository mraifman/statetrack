HB 2975 (2025R1) · Relating to crimes constituting domestic violence; and prescribing an effective date.

Provides that a DV-involving crime's admission or finding is not an element for merger purposes, so such convictions may not merge for sentencing.

What the bill does (OR measure summary (OLIS plain-language digest + catchline))

<b>Digest: The Act says that a pleading, admission or finding that criminal conduct constitutes DV is not an element of the crime. The Act takes effect on the 91st day after sine die.</b> [<i>Digest: The Act says that a finding that a crime constitutes DV is not an element of the crime. The Act takes effect on the 91st day after sine die.</i>] Provides that <b>a pleading,</b> an admission or a finding that<b> criminal conduct constitutes</b> a crime [<i>constitutes</i>]<b> involving</b> domestic violence is not an element of the crime for merger purposes. Takes effect on the 91st day following adjournment sine die.
