SB 302 (2025R1) · Relating to kindergarten through grade 12 education.

Directs the Department of Education to audit assessments given to kindergarteners and report findings to interim education committees by September 15, 2026.

What the bill does (OR measure summary (OLIS plain-language digest + catchline))

<b>Digest: Tells ODE to do an audit of the tests given to kindergarteners.</b> [<i>Digest: Directs ODE to study the state of education in this state.</i>] Requires the Department of Education to [<i>study the adequacy of public education in this state for kindergarten through grade 12</i>]<b> conduct an audit of assessments administered to kindergarteners in public schools</b>. Directs the department to submit findings to the interim committees of the Legislative Assembly related to education not later than September 15, 2026.
