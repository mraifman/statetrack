HB 3792 (2025R1) · Relating to consumer protection for utility ratepayers.

Doubles the minimum low-income electric bill assistance charge on utility customers to $40 million and raises the per-site customer cap to $1,000 monthly.

What the bill does (OR measure summary (OLIS plain-language digest + catchline))

Raises the amount to be collected to help with electric bills for low-income customers. Tells the PUC to look every two years at the amount to be collected. Increases from $20 million to $40 million the minimum amount to be collected from the customers of electric companies for low-income electric bill payment and crisis assistance. Directs the Public Utility Commission to reassess every two years the community level of need for low-income electric bill payment and crisis assistance. Allows the commission to adjust the amount to be collected based on its findings and changes to electricity rates. Requires the commission to report to the Legislative Assembly if the commission increases the amount to be collected by more than 2.5 percent. Increases from $500 to $1,000 per month per customer site the maximum amount that a customer may be required to pay for low-income electric bill payment and crisis assistance.
