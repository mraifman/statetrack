What the bill does (OR measure summary (OLIS plain-language digest + catchline))

The Act makes a tax credit bigger for some EMS providers and keeps the tax credit in the law until the year 2030. Increases the tax credit allowed to rural volunteer emergency medical services providers from $250 per tax year to $1,000 per tax year. Requires the Office of Rural Health to annually submit to the interim committees of the Legislative Assembly related to health care and to the Emergency Medical Services Program a report that includes the number of taxpayers eligible for the tax credit. Extends the tax credit sunset to January 1, 2030.
