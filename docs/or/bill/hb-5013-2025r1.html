HB 5013 (2025R1) · Relating to the financial administration of the Commission on Judicial Fitness and Disability; and declaring an emergency.

Appropriates General Fund moneys to the Commission on Judicial Fitness and Disability for its biennial expenses and declares an emergency effective July 1, 2025.

What the bill does (OR measure summary (OLIS plain-language digest + catchline))

The Act creates an agency budget. Appropriates moneys from the General Fund to the Commission on Judicial Fitness and Disability for biennial expenses. Declares an emergency, effective July 1, 2025.
