SB 834 (2025R1) · Relating to facilities that provide mental health treatment.

Bars state hospitals from providing inpatient mental health services to minors and makes several administrative changes to hospital leadership, commitment criteria, and evaluations.

What the bill does (OR measure summary (OLIS plain-language digest + catchline))

The Act makes changes to some laws about places that provide mental health treatment. Prohibits state hospitals from providing inpatient services to individuals who are under 18 years of age. Modifies the description of when a qualifying mental disorder is resistant to treatment for purposes of involuntary civil commitment of an extremely dangerous person. Directs the Oregon Health Authority to designate a licensed physician to serve as the chief medical officer of a state hospital, regardless of whether the superintendent of the hospital is a licensed physician. Requires certain evaluations to be conducted by a certified evaluator.
