What the bill does (OR measure summary (OLIS plain-language digest + catchline))

Causes this state to join a compact for school psychologists. Enacts the School Psychologist Interstate Licensure Compact. Prescribes that the purpose of the Compact is to facilitate the interstate practice of school psychology in educational or school settings. Describes the rights and responsibilities under the Compact.
