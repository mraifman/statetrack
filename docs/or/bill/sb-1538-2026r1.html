SB 1538 (2026R1) · Relating to conduct of public education; and prescribing an effective date.

Requires school districts to admit all students regardless of immigration status, bars immigration-based discrimination in education, and adds AG model policies on immigration enforcement at schools.

What the bill does (OR measure summary (OLIS plain-language digest + catchline))

Tells schools how to respond to immigration issues. Requires district school boards to admit all students to all schools and <b>instructional</b> programs of the school district. Prohibits discrimination in education related to immigration or citizenship status.<b> Makes limited exceptions.</b> Adds requirements for model policies prepared by the Attorney General related to immigration enforcement at public schools. Requires school districts and education service districts to [<i>adopt related policies and procedures and to provide training to district employees related to the policies and procedures</i>]<b> provide applicable model policies to employees</b>. Takes effect July 1, 2026.
