SB 599 (2025R1) · Relating to immigration status discrimination in real estate transactions; and declaring an emergency.

Bans landlords and property sellers from discriminating against tenants or applicants based on immigration or citizenship status, with statutory penalties.

What the bill does (OR measure summary (OLIS plain-language digest + catchline))

<b>Digest: This Act bans a landlord from biases in renting a home based on immigration status.</b> [<i>Digest: This Act bans biases in the rent or sale of property based on immigration status.</i>] Prohibits landlords from inquiring about or [<i>disclosing</i>]<b> discriminating on the basis of a tenant's or applicant's</b> immigration <b>or citizenship</b> status<b>,</b> [<i>or</i>] rejecting an applicant [<i>due to immigration status. Prohibits discrimination based on immigration status for real property transactions.</i>] <b>based on the type of identifying documentation or disclosing or threatening disclosure of an applicant's or a tenant's immigration or citizenship status for improper purposes. Authorizes statutory penalties. Becomes operative 30 days after the effective date. Declares an emergency, effective on passage.</b>
