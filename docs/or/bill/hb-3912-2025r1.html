HB 3912 (2025R1) · Relating to use of the title "doctor"

Requires people using the title "doctor" in a health care context to state their doctoral profession on specified materials, including social media and name badges.

What the bill does (OR measure summary (OLIS plain-language digest + catchline))

The Act makes a person who says they are a doctor on some materials say in what health care profession they are a doctor. Requires an individual who uses the title "doctor" in connection with a health care profession to designate on specified material, including social media and professional name badges, the health care profession in which the individual earned a doctoral degree.
