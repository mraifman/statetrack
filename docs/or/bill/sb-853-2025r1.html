SB 853 (2025R1) · Relating to Oregon Racing Commission license fees; and declaring an emergency.

Raises the maximum amounts the Oregon Racing Commission may charge for certain licenses, and declares an emergency effective on passage.

What the bill does (OR measure summary (OLIS plain-language digest + catchline))

The Act increases the amount that the ORC may charge for licenses. Increases the maximum amount of certain license fees charged by the Oregon Racing Commission. Declares an emergency, effective on passage.
