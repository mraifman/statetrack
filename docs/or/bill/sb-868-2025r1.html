SB 868 (2025R1) · Relating to special education for children with a low incidence disability.

Directs the Oregon Department of Education to study special education costs, develop a cost formula, and report on it.

What the bill does (OR measure summary (OLIS plain-language digest + catchline))

Directs ODE to make a report on the cost of certain programs for special education. Directs the Department of Education to develop a formula and to submit a report related to the calculation of the cost of operating and administering a local, county or regional program of special education.
