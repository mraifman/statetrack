What the bill does (OR measure summary (OLIS plain-language digest + catchline))

Allows the State Fire Marshal and an agency to take actions for fire protection. Makes changes related to a fire policy council. Grants certain authorities to the State Fire Marshal and the Department of the State Fire Marshal. Makes certain changes related to the Governor's Fire Service Policy Council.
