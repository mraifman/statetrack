What the bill does (OR measure summary (OLIS plain-language digest + catchline))

The Act would allow grant funding to be used for people at a different type of county facility. Expands the definition of "local correctional facility" under the Oregon Jail-Based Medications for Opioid Use Disorder Grant Program to allow people at other types of county facilities to receive opioid use disorder treatment and transition planning services.
