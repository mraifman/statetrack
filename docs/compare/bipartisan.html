Bipartisan

Where sponsors cross party lines. A bill counts as bipartisan when its listed sponsors include at least one Democrat and one Republican. Each cell shows the share of a state’s bills on the topic that are bipartisan, how many bills that is out of, and the same share among bills that became law.

States list sponsors differently: Massachusetts bills often carry many co-sponsors, while a bill in another state may name only a few, so compare patterns across topics within a state before comparing states. Only bills with at least one individual legislator sponsor count; committee bills are left out. Independents don’t count toward either side. DC’s Council has no Republican members.
