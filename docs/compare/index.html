The same issues, side by side. Each number is the average direction of a state’s bills on a sub-topic, from −2 (Restrictive) to +2 (Permissive), measured against that state’s current law. Click a sub-topic to see every state’s bills on it.

A lean describes what legislators proposed, not the state’s politics, and it includes bills that died, so it reflects the whole session’s agenda. Faint cells rest on fewer than 8 directional tags; “n/a” means fewer than 3.

More ways to compare

  • Matching bills: near-identical bills filed in different states.
  • Timeline: which state filed on a topic first, and when.
  • Enactment gap: how much of what is proposed becomes law, and how the lean shifts.
  • Bipartisan: where sponsors cross party lines.
  • Governors: vetoes and signing across states.
  • Pending bills: live bills in sessions that are still open.
  • Data: download the tagged bills as CSV.
