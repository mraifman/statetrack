Driver-Assistance Systems (ADAS)

partially automated features where a human driver must stay in control and supervise (SAE level 2: lane-keeping, adaptive cruise control, 'driver support'): Restrictive: adds requirements, limits, reporting or liability; Permissive: reduces them. A drunk-driving or other traffic offense by a human using these features is Traffic safety, not ADAS, unless the bill regulates the feature itself
