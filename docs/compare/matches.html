Matching bills

The same idea, filed in more than one state: model legislation, copycat bills and parallel efforts. Bills are grouped by how closely their titles and plain-language summaries match.

Strong match means the summaries read nearly the same. Related means they cover the same specific idea but may differ in detail, so check the text. Matching uses the model-written summaries, so thin summaries can miss matches. Generic budget and ceremonial bills are left out.
