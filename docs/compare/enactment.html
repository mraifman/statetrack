How much of what gets proposed becomes law. Each cell shows the share of a state’s bills on the topic that became law, with the average direction of all its bills on the topic, then of the ones that became law (−2 Restrictive to +2 Permissive).

Massachusetts and DC are still in session, so their numbers show progress so far, not outcomes (shaded lighter). Other states’ sessions are over, so every bill that did not pass counts as not enacted. States differ a lot in how many bills they file and in how committees work, so compare the lean shift more than the raw rate.
