Data

Download the tagged bills for all 6 states as plain CSV files that open in Excel, Sheets, R or Python.

  • statetrack_bills.csv (10.1 MB)
    One row per bill: state, bill, title, status, dates, lead author, topics, plain-language summary and links.
  • statetrack_tags.csv (2.8 MB)
    One row per tag: the topic and sub-topic the model assigned to a bill, its direction against current law (and −2 to +2 score), mechanism, funding effect and confidence.
  • statetrack_legislators.csv (0.0 MB)
    One row per legislator: party, chamber, district and bills authored or co-authored.
  • statetrack_topic_matrix.csv (0.0 MB)
    The numbers behind the comparison pages: bills, directional tags, mean direction and bills that became law, per state and sub-topic.

Reading the columns

  • direction: how the bill changes current law on a sub-topic. Restrictive moves toward less permission or more rules; Permissive toward more permission, access or fewer rules; Mixed, Neutral and Unclear are left out of averages. The score runs −2 (Strongly Restrictive) to +2 (Strongly Permissive).
  • status: Became Law, Vetoed, Passed (awaiting the executive), In Committee Process, In Floor Process, Pending Referral, Died, Failed Passage in Committee. DC resolutions that were adopted show as Adopted.
  • Labels are written by an AI model from each bill’s title and official summary. They are approximate; the quoted evidence for each call is on the bill’s page.
  • State session calendars differ, and Massachusetts and DC are still in session, so “Died” is not the same thing everywhere.
