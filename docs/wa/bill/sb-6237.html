SB 6237 · Rental property flood discl.

Requires landlords to disclose possible flood hazard location and advise tenants that the landlord's insurance does not cover their possessions.

What the bill does (WA Substitute House Bill Report (Summary of Bill))

The property may be located in a special flood hazard area or an area of potential flooding. Information about potential hazards, including whether the property may be at risk of flooding, is available from the county. The landlord's insurance does not cover the tenant's personal possessions, and the tenant should consider purchasing flood and renter's insurance to insure the renter's possessions from loss due to fire, flood, or other risks.
