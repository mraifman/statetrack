SB 5106 · Eid al-Fitr & Eid al-Adha

Designates Eid al-Fitr and Eid al-Adha as legislatively recognized days in Washington, with no policy effect.

What the bill does (WA Substitute House Bill Report (Summary of Bill))

The first day of the tenth month of the Islamic calendar, also known as Eid al-Fitr, is designated as a legislatively recognized day. The tenth day of the twelfth month of the Islamic calendar, also known as Eid al-Adha, is designated as a legislatively recognized day.
