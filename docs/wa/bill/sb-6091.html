SB 6091 · Real estate/exclusive market

Prohibits brokers from marketing residential property only to exclusive buyer or broker groups, and makes violations grounds for DOL discipline.

What the bill does (WA Substitute House Bill Report (Summary of Bill))

Real estate brokers are prohibited from marketing the sale or lease of residential real estate to a limited or exclusive group of buyers or brokers unless the property is concurrently marketed to the general public and all other brokers, except as reasonably necessary to protect the health and safety of the owner or occupant. Marketing to the general public does not require an owner to allow access onto the residential real estate or into the residence. The Washington Law of Real Estate Pamphlet is updated to state that property must be marketed publicly and not to an exclusive group of buyers or brokers only, unless the health or safety of the owner or occupant requires such. A violation of this prohibition is grounds for disciplinary action by the Department of Licensing.
