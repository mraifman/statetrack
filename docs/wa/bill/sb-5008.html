What the bill does (WA Senate Bill Report (Orig.) (Summary of Bill))

OSPI is directed to establish a temporary grant program to support the use of assessment, diagnostic, and learning tools for K-12 students in math and English language arts, subject to appropriations. OSPI must award grants based on need, ensure that grant recipients vary by geographic location and size, and report on the outcomes of the grant program by November 1st of each year. The grants are intended to help support public schools and school districts purchase or maintain access to a digital platform with tools that will identify students' individual needs and monitor student progress. This platform must have a criterion-referenced component. Applicants for the grants must describe their plan for the funding and how they will provide professional development, so staff can effectively use these tools. School districts, charter schools, and state-tribal education compact schools may apply for these grants. OSPI must report on the outcomes of the grant program, including specific metrics, each year that grants are awarded. The grant program expires on December 31, 2028.
