HB 1023 · Cosmetology compact

Establishes the Cosmetology Licensure Compact, letting licensed cosmetologists practice across state lines with a multistate license, and creates a multistate commission.

What the bill does (WA Substitute House Bill Report (Summary of Bill))

Compact Establishment . The Cosmetology Licensure Compact (Compact) is established, which allows licensed cosmetologists to practice across state lines in Compact states with a multistate license. In administering and managing Washington single-state cosmetology licenses and multistate licenses under the Compact, the Department of Licensing must consider these licenses as separate professional licensing programs and track and manage revenues and costs generated by each license separately. Compact Commission . The Compact is administered by a Compact commission (Commission), which is a joint government agency whose membership consists of all member states that have enacted the Compact. Membership of the Commission consists of one delegate selected by each member state's licensing authority. The delegate must be an administrator of the state's licensing authority or their designated staff.
