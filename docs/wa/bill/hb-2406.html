HB 2406 · LNI communication methods

Lets the Labor and Industries Department send many notices electronically, with paper options, and changes some appeal and rulemaking notice timelines.

What the bill does (WA House Bill Report (Summary of Bill))

General Notices and Communications from the Department of Labor and Industries . Numerous statutes governing how the Department sends notices in various programs are amended to allow notices to be sent electronically. Before using an electronic method for the first time, the Department must provide recipients the option to receive notices nonelectronically. Notices Under the Industrial Insurance Act . Notices of orders closing a claim may be sent electronically if the recipient chooses. The copy of the order must contain a statement saying the order is final within 60 days from the date the order is communicated through nonelectronic means and 65 days from the date the order is communicated through electronic means. Notices and Appeals Under the Washington Industrial Safety and Health Act . The Department has a total of 75 working days in which to make a redetermination when there has been an appeal, instead of a 30-day period with a 45-day extension period. The Department must provide information about public hearings for rulemaking to the newspapers, rather than publishing in the newspaper, at least 20 days, rather than 30 days, prior to the hearing. Substitute Bill Compared to Original Bill: The substitute bill removes certain sections from the bill, thereby restoring current law on notices related to: (1) transportation network companies and drivers; (2) petitions to de-certify a self-insured employers; (3) elections for third-party actions in workers' compensation cases; and (4) other workers' compensation orders, except that the bill still allows for closing orders to be sent electronically if the recipient chooses.
