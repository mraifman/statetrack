HB 1009 · Pharmacy commission members

Adds a pharmacy technician member to the Pharmacy Quality Assurance Commission and lets a pharmacist seat be filled by a pharmacy owner or operator.

What the bill does (WA House Bill Report (Summary of Bill))

An additional pharmacy technician member is added to the Pharmacy Quality Assurance Commission (PQAC). One of the positions on the PQAC designated for a pharmacist is replaced with a position that may be filled by either: (1) a pharmacist; or (2) a public member who is an owner, operator, or officer of a pharmacy and who is not a pharmacist or a pharmacy technician.
