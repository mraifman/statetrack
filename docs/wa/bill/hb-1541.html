HB 1541 · Veterans affairs adv. comm.

Changes the membership makeup and appointment criteria for the Veterans Affairs Advisory Committee, including the number of appointees from veterans organizations and at-large seats.

What the bill does (WA Second Engrossed Substitute House Bill Report (Summary of Bill))

The Governor may appoint two representatives from the veterans' homes, based on nominations from the homes. The Governor is required to appoint six, rather than 10, members from nationally recognized veterans service organizations with an active chapter in the state. The Governor is required to appoint six, rather than two, at-large members. The member appointments must reflect military experience and personal attributes, in addition to geographic diversity, minority viewpoints, and women veterans. Reference to the 1992 member appointments is removed.
