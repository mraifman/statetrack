HB 1109 · Public facilities districts

Extends the maximum period for two local sales and use taxes that finance public facilities district regional centers and theaters from 40 to 55 years.

What the bill does (WA House Bill Report (Summary of Bill))

The maximum timeframes within which the PFDs are authorized to levy two local sales and use taxes, one to finance the construction of regional centers and one to finance the construction of regional theaters, are extended from 40 years to 55 years, provided that bonds have not yet retired.
