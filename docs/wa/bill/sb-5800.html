What the bill does (WA Senate Bill Report (Summary of Bill))

General Bond Authorization. Bonding of up to $7 billion is authorized for the design, right-of-way, and construction of highway projects or improvements, with all proceeds from the sale of any such bonds to be deposited in the Move Ahead WA Account, which resides in the motor vehicle fund. The bonds are authorized as general obligation bonds of the state. The bonds must be paid first from excise taxes on fuel and vehicle-related fees and are secondly backed by the full faith and credit of the state. Language states the Legislature agrees to continue to impose these excise taxes on fuel and vehicle-related fees in amounts sufficient to pay bonds costs. No bonds may be offered for sale without prior legislative appropriation of the net proceeds of the sale of the bonds. The treasurer is authorized to transfer amounts needed to the Highway Bond Retirement Account to pay debt service. State Route 520 Bond Authorization Increase. Existing State Route 520 bond authorization is increased by $500 million, from $1.95 billion to $2.45 billion.
