SB 6037 · Fire protection districts

Changes fire protection district formation rules, including city levy caps, a new $0.25 per $1,000 district levy outside limits, notice rules, and consultation duties.

What the bill does (WA Senate Bill Report (Summary of Bill))

The requirement that a city’s or town’s property tax levy must be reduced as a part of the formation of a district after July 1, 2026, is removed. Instead, a city or town creating a fire protection district must reduce its statutory maximum tax rate by the combined tax rate imposed by the newly formed fire protection district, with the exception of cities with a population over 500,000. For the city or town participating in a district formed after July 1, 2026, with the exception of cities over 500,000 in population as of 2025, the city's or town's property tax levy may not exceed $5.90 per $1000 of assessed valuation. Districts may impose up to a $0.25 per $1,000 of assessed value levy that is outside of the $5.90 per $1,000 limit in statute. Notice of the public hearing on a resolution adopted by the legislative authority of a city or town must be published on a city website or local newspaper, rather than a newspaper of general circulation, for three consecutive weeks. The legislative authority may provide additional notice of the hearing by mail or other manner deemed necessary by the authority to notify affected persons. Prior to adopting a resolution, the legislative authority of the city or town must include labor organizations representing employees who perform fire prevention, fire suppression, and emergency medical services within the city or town, to consult on development of the proposed financing plan, governance structure, and anticipated impacts on service delivery and employment conditions; and if the city or town is located within the boundaries of a public hospital district, the city or town must consult with the public hospital district on any impact of the financing plan on the public hospital district property tax levy. If a district formed by resolution is governed by a board of fire commissioners, the fire commissioners must be independently elected at the same election where the resolution to authorize the formation of the district is submitted to voters. A district may contract with the city or town within its boundaries for services including, but not limited to, administrative services like information technology, financial, and human resources functions. EFFECT OF CHANGES MADE BY WAYS & MEANS COMMITTEE (Second Substitute): Requires a city or town, prior to adopting a resolution, to consult with labor organizations on the development of the proposed financing plan, governance structure, and anticipated impacts on service delivery and employment conditions. Requires a city or town located within the boundaries of a public hospital district, prior to adopting a resolution, to consult with the public hospital district on any impact of the financing plan on the public hospital district property tax levy. Makes a technical clarification. EFFECT OF CHANGES MADE BY LOCAL GOVERNMENT COMMITTEE (First Substitute): Requires that a city or town participating in a district may not have the city’s or town’s property tax levy rate exceed applicable rates in statute. Specifies that a district may levy of up to $0.25 per $1,000 of assessed value that is outside of levy limits established in statute.
