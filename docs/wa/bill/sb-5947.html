What the bill does (WA Engrossed Senate Bill Report (Summary of Bill))

The Washington Health Care Board (Board) is established and directed to design and implement the Washington health plan to provide for all medically necessary health care services for all residents of the state. The plan may not be implemented until the state obtains a federal waiver for a state-based universal health care plan and receives federal funding to support the implementation of the plan and any necessary implementing legislation has been adopted by the Legislature. The Board shall consist of the following 19 members appointed by the Governor: five members representing employers; six members who are practicing health care providers in the state as follows: two physicians, at least one of whom must be a primary care physician; one registered nurse; one mental health provider; one dentist; and one naturopath; one health care facility director; one member with expertise in health care financing; one member with expertise in prescription drug purchasing and economics; one tribal representative; one member representing organized labor; and three members representing health-related state agencies.
