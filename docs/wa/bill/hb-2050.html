HB 2050 · K-12 savings & efficiencies

Reduces the enrollment counts used for LEA funding calculations for districts where over one-third of students take alternative learning experience courses.

What the bill does (WA House Bill Report (Summary of Bill))

Student Enrollment for LEA Calculations . The student enrollment used to calculate the LEA is reduced for school districts having more than 33 percent of the AAFTE enrolled in ALE courses. The reduction is equal to the school district's full-time equivalent students enrolled in ALE courses, minus 33 percent of the AAFTE. To illustrate, if a school district has a total enrollment of 100 AAFTE students, and 50 of those students are enrolled in an ALE, then the total enrollment would be reduced to 83 students for purposes of LEA calculations.
