SB 5182 · Incarcerated parents

Allows the Department of Corrections to contract with nonprofits or volunteers for midwifery and doula services for incarcerated individuals, and updates gendered and outdated terminology.

What the bill does (WA Substitute House Bill Report (Summary of Bill))

Statutory language specifying that nothing requires the Department of Corrections (DOC) to establish or provide funding for midwifery or doula services to incarcerated individuals is removed. Statutory language is added to specify that nothing in the provisions of state law related to requiring the DOC to make reasonable accommodations for midwifery or doula services prevents the DOC from contracting with a nonprofit organization or partnering with volunteers to deliver such services. Related references to "inmates" are replaced with "incarcerated individuals" and references to an incarcerated individual's gender are replaced with gender neutral terms.
