What the bill does (WA Engrossed Substitute Senate Bill Report (Orig.) (Summary of Bill))

Foster Care Licensing. When high-potency synthetic opioids or illicit substances are present in the home, DCYF is to immediately terminate a child-specific probationary license. DCYF may issue a child-specific license to a relative or close family friend for the placement of a specific child in the care and custody of another state who is to be placed in Washington State through the Interstate Compact on the Placement of Children. Kinship caregivers do not need to complete blood-borne pathogen training. The licensing exemption for physicians and lawyers is struck. DCYF is authorized to terminate a license when a license has been inactive. DCYF is to develop rules in collaboration with impacted groups, which will include a definition of inactive, a process to assess, and exceptions. Foster home closure due to inactivity is considered an enforcement action and is subject to appeal. Foster home licenses specify the category of care the agency is authorized to provide and the ages and number of persons to be served. Crisis Residential Centers. CRCs are to have one staff member to every four children during working hours and one staff member to every six children during sleeping hours. Child Care Licensing . To qualify for a non-expiring full license, licensees accepting state subsidy must utilize an electronic attendance system that captures the daily check-in and check-out time of each child, verified by a parent authorized guardian's electronic signature or biometric identifier. A license may be denied, or a license may be suspended, revoked, modified, or not renewed by the secretary upon proof that the agency has exceeded its licensed capacity. If DCYF has evidence that an agency has exceeded its licensed capacity, DCYF is to conduct an investigation that may result in the revocation of the agency's license. Any child care licensee who is found to have committed child care subsidy fraud is permanently disqualified from child care licensure.
