HB 1596 · Speeding

Requires intelligent speed assistance devices as a license condition for certain drivers, limiting vehicle speed to the posted limit during probation or by court order.

What the bill does (WA Engrossed Substitute Senate Bill Report (Summary of Bill))

(As Amended by Senate) Requires the use of an intelligent speed assistance device, as a condition to a temporary restricted or occupational driver's license under certain circumstances, during a probationary period following the lifting of a driver's license suspension for certain traffic-related offenses, or by order of a court, to limit the operation of a vehicle to no more than the speed limit, with certain exceptions.
