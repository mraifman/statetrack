HB 2114 · Defective license plates

Lets owners get free replacement plates for defective plates within 24 months of issuance, and allows DOL to waive fees up to five years.

What the bill does (WA Substitute House Bill Report (Summary of Bill))

Replacement License Plates . A person who has a defective license plate may request a replacement license plate or plates for use on the same vehicle at no charge, if the request for replacement occurs on or before the last day of the twenty-fourth month from the date of the license plate issuance. The DOL may waive all fees for a replacement license plate or plates if the request occurs after the last day of the twenty-fourth month from the date of issuance through and including the five-year anniversary of the date of the issuance. To qualify to be replaced free of charge, a license plate must have defects such as, but not limited to, numbers that are no longer legible, or paint that is peeling or bubbling. The DOL may require the defective license plate to be submitted with the request for a replacement. A defective license plate is eligible to be replaced as a set, unless only one license plate is required by law.
