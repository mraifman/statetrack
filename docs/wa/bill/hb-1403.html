HB 1403 · Condominium construction

Revises condo implied warranties, creates a 2-10 warranty alternative for small ADU condos, and exempts those ADUs from building enclosure design and inspection requirements.

What the bill does (WA Engrossed House Bill Report (Summary of Bill))

Implied Warranties under the Washington Uniform Common Interest Ownership Act . The WUCIOA provisions related to implied warranties of quality are revised and apply to condominiums in common interest communities created on or after the effective date of the bill. For such condominium units, a declarant or dealer impliedly warrants to a purchaser that the unit will be constructed in accordance with the plans, specifications approved by the applicable jurisdiction for the construction of the condominium, manufacturer installation guidelines, applicable building code in effect at the time of permit approval, and any published industry standards specifically incorporated into the applicable building codes at the time of permit approval, instead of in accordance with engineering and construction standards and applicable building codes generally accepted in Washington at the time of construction. Additionally, the term "workmanlike manner" is defined to mean the degree of care that a reasonably prudent licensed contractor would exercise under the same or similar circumstances. An additional exclusion from implied warranties of quality is created for any condominium unit that is an accessory dwelling unit organized as a condominium under the WUCIOA and that is located in a new or a conversion building containing 12 or fewer units. For such units, a declarant or dealer is not subject to the implied warranties of quality if the declarant or dealer provides an express warranty of quality (2-10 warranty) and express warranty insurance coverage that: requires acknowledgement by the purchaser that the express warranty of quality applies; allows for recovery of defects under the express warranty; applies to all units and common areas within the building; and provides minimum coverage periods for: defective workmanship and materials (one year); defective plumbing, electrical, and ductwork distribution systems (two years); and structural defects to load-bearing structural members (10 years). A proceeding for the breach of an express 2-10 warranty of quality and insurance coverage must be commenced pursuant to the WUCIOA provisions that govern breach of warranties of quality and actions for construction defect claims. Building Enclosure Requirements for Multiunit Residential Buildings . The building enclosure design and inspection requirements do not apply to any accessory dwelling unit organized under the WUCIOA as a condominium unit in a common interest community created on or after the effective date of the bill. If a building with 12 or fewer units and no more than three stories is subject to an express 2-10 warranty (instead of the implied warranty of quality under the WUCIOA), the building is not excluded from the definition of "multiunit residential building," and, as a result, the building is subject to building enclosure design and inspection requirements.
