What the bill does (WA House Bill Report (Summary of Bill))

Inventory Grant Opportunities . The Department of Commerce's (Commerce) requirement to develop an inventory of economic development grants, including regularly reviewing the federal register, maintaining an inventory of private grants, and consulting with certain federal agencies is made mandatory, rather than discretionary, but subject to appropriated funds. Commerce's obligations to provide a resource guide for grant applicants and facilitate the attraction of major events are removed. Assist Federal Grant Applicants . Commerce is required to assist applicants for federal grants in the following ways: Commerce is required to expand the FundHubWA website to include all federal funding opportunities related to economic development by July 1, 2028. After Commerce develops a review process and develops scoring criteria, it must provide a letter of support to an applicant for federal funds upon request. Beginning in 2027, and every two years thereafter, Commerce must solicit input from certain economic development organizations to identify federal funds anticipated in the next two-year period and prioritize applications for the funds that are most likely to have a significant impact in the state. By June 30, 2027, if funds have been appropriated for this purpose, Commerce must develop scoring criteria to prioritize the award of matching funds, such as the economic impact of a successful application and whether the project is identified as a priority for the state. Award Matching Funds . Commerce is required to award matching funds through a competitive process. The awards of matching funds are limited based on the type of applicant and whether other funding is available: Entities eligible for an award of 100 percent of the matching funds include: political subdivisions, federally recognized Indian tribes, higher education institutions, consumer-owned utilities, small businesses, regional planning organizations, economic development organizations, and nonprofits.
