HB 2521 · Firearms background checks

Lets the Washington State Patrol set firearms background check fees at a level that covers program administration costs.

What the bill does (WA House Bill Report (Summary of Bill))

The Washington State Patrol may set the fee for firearms background checks to an amount calculated to cover the total costs incurred in administering the Firearms Background Check Program.
