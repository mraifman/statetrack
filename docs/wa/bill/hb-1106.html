HB 1106 · Disabled veterans/prop. tax

Lowers the service-connected disability rating needed for disabled veterans' property tax exemption from 80 percent to 40 percent, starting in 2027.

What the bill does (WA Engrossed House Bill Report (Summary of Bill))

Beginning January 1, 2027, the disabled veteran combined service-connected evaluation rating for the SPTE is decreased from 80 percent or higher to 40 percent or higher for taxes levied for collection in calendar year 2027 and thereafter. An exemption from a TPPS, JLARC review, and the 10-year expiration is included.
