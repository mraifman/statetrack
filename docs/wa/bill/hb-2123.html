What the bill does (WA Substitute Senate Bill Report (Summary of Bill))

The requirement to obtain a certification stating that foreign nationals were not involved in the financing or decision making regarding a contribution is removed for candidates and political committees organized for the purpose of supporting or opposing candidates. EFFECT OF STATE GOVERNMENT, TRIBAL AFFAIRS & ELECTIONS COMMITTEE AMENDMENT(S): Removes the $6,000 monetary threshold for certifications stating that no foreign national financed or was in involved in making decisions regarding a contribution or expenditure. Removes the certification requirement for contributions to candidates and political committees organized for the purpose of supporting or opposing candidates.
