SB 5837 · Guardianship, etc.

Revises adult guardianship and conservatorship procedures, requiring appointed attorneys for objecting respondents at public expense and expanding supported decision-making provisions.

What the bill does (WA Engrossed Substitute House Bill Report (Summary of Bill))

Service of Court Visitor's Statement . If a court visitor serves the court visitor's statement by mail, service must be by first-class mail instead of certified mail with return receipt. Appointment of an Attorney for a Respondent . Provisions authorizing the court to appoint an attorney are modified to require the court to appoint an attorney, regardless of the respondent's ability to pay, when: the respondent objects to the petition; the respondent requests appointment of an attorney to the court visitor; or in the opinion of the court, the rights and interests of the respondent cannot otherwise be adequately protected and represented. It is clarified that if an attorney is appointed under the above-listed circumstances, the attorney must be at public expense if the respondent is unable to afford an attorney, the expense of an attorney would result in substantial hardship, or if the respondent does not have practical access to funds with which to pay an attorney. Similarly to a guardianship or conservatorship petition, if the respondent objects to the petition for a protective arrangement instead of guardianship or requests appointment of an attorney to the court visitor, the court visitor must petition the court to have an attorney appointed within five days of meeting the respondent. Confidentiality of Court Visitor's Report and Professional Evaluation . The report of a court visitor or the evaluation report of a medical professional appointed to examine the respondent is additionally available to proposed guardians, conservators, and special agents, as well as their attorneys, for purposes of guardianship, conservatorship, or other protective arrangement proceedings. Contents of an Order of Appointment of a Guardian for an Adult or a Conservator . The requirement for the court order appointing a guardian or conservator to include the date by which the court will review the guardian's or conservator's plan is removed. All orders establishing guardianship or conservatorship for an adult must include the date on which the original letters of office will expire, which must be 180 days after the anniversary date of appointment. The mistaken use of the term "guardian's plan" when referring to a guardian's report is corrected. Respondent's Attendance at Hearings . To align with the standard applicable to a hearing on a guardianship petition, the court no longer has to make the additional finding that the respondent's refusal to attend is consistent and repeated in order to proceed with a hearing on a conservatorship or other protective arrangement petition without the respondent's attendance. Notice of Order of Appointment of a Guardian for an Adult or Conservator . To align with the timeline applicable in guardianship cases, a conservator must provide to the person subject to conservatorship a copy of the order of the conservator's appointment no later than 14 days, rather than 30 days, after the appointment. Supported Decision-Making Agreements . An employer or employee of an adult with a disability may act as a supporter for the adult if the employer or employee is a certified professional guardian and conservator or certified professional guardian and conservator agency. Provisions addressing acceptance and reliance on a supported decision-making agreement are expanded.
