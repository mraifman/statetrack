HB 1503 · Digital equity

Shifts digital equity planning, outreach and reporting duties from the broadband office to the Office of Equity, adding broadband adoption goals and a biennial report.

What the bill does (WA Second Substitute Senate Bill Report (Summary of Bill))

Washington State Broadband Office. Broadband adoption and digital equity are added as purposes for WSBO. Changes to the responsibilities for WSBO include the addition of coordination with relevant state agencies and community anchor institutions as well as the promotion of broadband adoption. WSBO must provide data to the Office of Equity regarding broadband adoption and digital equity programs to assist the Office of Equity in monitoring the implementation of a state digital equity plan. It is a goal of Washington that by 2028 broadband speed goals for the state are reviewed and updated with input provided by entities, such as the Digital Equity Forum, communities, tribes, nonprofit organizations, local governments, consumer-owned and investor-owned utilities, relevant state agencies, and public and private entities. Office of Equity. The Office of Equity must coordinate with WSBO to encourage, foster, develop, and improve digital equity through the provision of digital devices and services to individuals and communities. Responsibility for outreach efforts regarding broadband and digital equity programs for unserved and underserved communities are transferred from WSBO to the Office of Equity. The outreach effort must include providing information about the federal Lifeline Program and other low-income broadband programs as well as facilitating the amplification of local efforts. The Office of Equity must annually review the outreach effort and coordinate with state boards and commissions that support the participation of underrepresented populations. Digital Equity Plan. The Office of Equity, in consultation with WSBO, the Department of Social and Health Services, and other state agencies, is responsible for the development of the digital equity plan. The plan must build upon WSBO's work in developing a state digital equity plan. By December 1, 2027, and every two years after initial publication of the digital equity plan, the Office of Equity must submit a report to the Governor and appropriate committees of the Legislature describing the digital equity plan, collaboration with partners, and an evaluation of existing state digital equity initiatives, policies, programs, funding needs, and public and private investments. The report must include a recommended model for future infrastructure and outreach investments. The Office of Equity must make publicly available, on an ongoing basis, an overview of the progress made in promoting digital equity among underserved communities. The overview must include a description of successes and challenges in promoting digital equity. As part of this requirement, the Office of Equity is required to have oversight over and direct future improvements to WSBO's public data resources used to track digital equity. The Office of Equity must review, update, and submit to the Governor and the appropriate committees of the Legislature a revised state digital equity plan every four years. Digital Equity Forum. The Office of Equity must provide staff support for the forum. Members of the the Digital Equity Forum are not prohibited from receiving compensation. The Office of Equity is responsible for transmitting the recommendations of the Digital Equity Forum. Reporting dates for the Digital Equity Forum may be aligned with the reporting requirements for the digital equity plan. Statutes relating to the digital equity plan and the Digital Equity Forum are recodified under the Office of Equity.
