HB 2523 · Community reinvestment prg.

Creates a Community Reinvestment Program with a participatory plan, a work group on joint Equity-Commerce administration, and a WSIPP study, using existing appropriations.

What the bill does (WA Engrossed Second Substitute House Bill Report (Summary of Bill))

The Washington State Office of Equity (Equity), the Department of Commerce (Commerce), and "by and for community organizations," must update the Community Reinvestment Plan (Plan) as needed, but at least every five years, beginning in 2032. The Plan must be developed using a community-based participatory action research approach, and include funding recommendations, programmatic guidelines, funding distribution, long-term economic benefits, monitoring, and evaluation. At least 15 percent, and no more than 60 percent of the funds must be distributed to each of the five strategies, unless Equity, Commerce, and the Plan recommend an alternative allocation to avoid unexpended funds or limit access to an eligible committee. Certain organizations are eligible for grants statewide, provided that specified criteria are met, including a limited number of organizations providing services, submission of annual reports, and obtaining a letter of support. Commerce is required to prohibit organizations that receive a grant from distributing the funds to an officer, family member of an officer, or a business or nonprofit owned or managed by an officer. A Community Reinvestment Program Work Group (Work Group) is created to prepare a report, legislative recommendations, and a transition plan for joint administration of the Community Reinvestment Program by Equity and Commerce by July 1, 2027, including guidance for an administrative framework. The work group will include legislative members, Equity, Commerce, and up to six optional members, and be chaired by the Equity member. The report is due to the Legislature, including the legislative caucus representing a minority community, by November 1, 2026. Commerce is required to use existing appropriations to fund the Work Group and Equity's participation in the Work Group. The Washington State Institute for Public Policy is required to study Commerce's distribution and regional impact of, and the recipient organizations' use of, the funds allocated under the Plan by June 30, 2027. Commerce is required to use existing appropriations to fund the preparation of the report.
