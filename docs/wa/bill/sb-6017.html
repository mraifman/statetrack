What the bill does (WA Senate Bill Report (Summary of Bill))

Victim Witness Questioning. Upon the motion of a prosecuting attorney or the victim in any criminal proceeding involving sexual assault or domestic violence in which the defendant is proceeding pro se, the court may conduct, appoint standby counsel to conduct, or appoint an investigator to conduct the questioning of the victim witness on behalf of the defendant if certain conditions are met. The court must make particularized findings on the record articulating the the factors which the court based its decision. Nonfatal Strangulation. A minor age 13 years or older may consent to a forensic examination for the purpose of gathering evidence for possible prosecution for domestic violence assault involving nonfatal strangulation. Rights of Sexual Assault Survivors. The term sexual assault survivor is changed to sexual violence survivor. Victims of female genital mutilation are afforded the same statutory rights as sexual violence survivors. Sexual Assault Forensic Examination Best Practices Advisory Group. The SAFE Advisory Group is redesignated and reauthorized until July 1, 2028. The purpose of the SAFE Advisory Group is shifted to recommending actionable, trauma-informed, victim-centered policies related to preventing and addressing sexual violence. EFFECT OF CHANGES MADE BY WAYS & MEANS COMMITTEE (Second Substitute): Allows the victim witness to move for the court to either conduct or appoint standby counsel to conduct all examination of the victim witness on behalf of the defendant. Adds a member representing children's advocacy centers to the SAFE Advisory Group.
