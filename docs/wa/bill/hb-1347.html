HB 1347 · Cannabis testing labs

Shifts cannabis testing lab oversight from the Liquor and Cannabis Board to WSDA accreditation, ending LCB certification and limiting its enforcement role.

What the bill does (WA Senate Bill Report (Summary of Bill))

The bill requires LCB to accept the accreditation of a cannabis testing laboratory by WSDA. Any authority previously exercised by LCB to certify, approve, or otherwise authorize cannabis testing laboratories is terminated. Nothing in the bill authorizes LCB to impose accreditation requirements beyond those administered by WSDA. The bill repeals provisions establishing the interagency coordination team for cannabis laboratory quality standards. LCB may only take enforcement action related to laboratory accreditation upon formal notice from WSDA that a laboratory is no longer in good standing. LCB may request WSDA take enforcement action if LCB identifies evidence of unethical, deceptive, or fraudulent laboratory practices relating to the generation or reporting of analytical data. Laboratories accredited by WSDA are authorized to conduct quality assurance and product standards testing. EFFECT OF LABOR & COMMERCE COMMITTEE AMENDMENT(S): Requires LCB to accept the accreditation of testing laboratories by WSDA, rather than requiring LCB to accept accreditation from WSDA as the sole basis for establishing the initial certification and any subsequent renewals if the laboratories do not violate requirements established by LCB. Allows laboratories accredited by WSDA to conduct quality assurance and product standards testing. Repeals provisions establishing the interagency coordination team for cannabis laboratory quality standards. Terminates any authority previously exercised by LCB to certify, approve, or otherwise authorize cannabis testing laboratories. Provides that LCB may only take enforcement action related to laboratory accreditation upon formal notice from WSDA that a laboratory is no longer in good standing. Allows LCB to request that WSDA take enforcement action if LCB identifies evidence of unethical, deceptive, or fraudulent laboratory practices relating to the generation or reporting of analytical data. Provides that nothing in the act authorizes LCB to impose accreditation requirements beyond those administered by WSDA. Makes conforming changes to related provisions.
