What the bill does (WA Senate Bill Report (Orig.) (Summary of Bill))

Mobile Driver's License and Identicard. By July 1, 2028, DOL must establish a program that allows every qualifying applicant for a driver's license or identicard the ability to acquire a mobile credential in addition to the physical version of these documents. By January 1, 2028, DOL must adopt rules to implement this requirement, which include, but are not limited to, issuance and verification requirements, expiration date, security and privacy protections, and the uses associated with the mobile credential. If a law requires a person to provide proof of identification, the requirement may be satisfied with a mobile credential so long as the mobile credential is electronically verified. A licensee must have their physical credential while operating a motor vehicle. Each governmental agency of the state must determine whether, and the extent to which, a governmental agency will accept a mobile credential. Any federal, state, local, or private entity allowing the use of the mobile credential must only retain data for which they explicitly obtained consent from the credential holder. These entities must inform the credential holder of the use and retention period of the mobile credential data. Any data collected by DOL and displayed on a mobile credential, will be handled the same as data collected for the issuance of a physical credential. These include: exempt from public disclosure; being private and confidential except as otherwise provided in federal and state law; shared only with specified use restrictions with contractual agreements; not being used in any federal registration or enforcement programs targeting Washington residents solely based on their race, religion, ethnicity, immigration, or citizenship status; and not being provided to any individual, agency, or department from another state, or to the extent permitted by federal law, to a federal law enforcement agency, for the purpose of enforcing another state's law that asserts criminal or civil liability related to the provision or receipt of protected health services. DOL's existing authorization to use a facial recognition matching system is expanded to include the verification of the identity for purposes of application and issuance of a mobile credential. Mobile Driver's License and Identicard Fee. Beginning October 1, 2026, an additional $1 fee is imposed on each issuance or renewal of a driver's license or identicard. Bus and Bus Facilities Grant Program. The bus and bus facilities grant program is expanded to include providing grants to transit agencies for safety or security enhancements for transit rolling stock or transit facilities. Statewide School-Based Bicycle Education Grant Program. The statewide school-based bicycle education grant program account is created in the state treasury to be used only for program activities. Beginning September 2029, the state treasurer must transfer on a quarterly basis $3.375 million from the Multimodal Transportation Account to the Statewide School-Based Bicycle Education Grant Program Account. Fish Passage Barrier Correction Project Permits. An existing Fish Habitat Enhancement Project Permit Streamlining Program is modified by adding eligibility for WSDOT culvert fixes where adjoining property owner's barriers remain in place. Clean Energy Facilities. WSDOT is directed to establish a process for issuing highway right-of-way leases to clean energy facility developers.
