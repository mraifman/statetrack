HB 2442 · Local government fund use

Expands local governments' authority to use housing, real estate and rental car tax revenues, and to extend property tax levy lid lifts.

What the bill does (WA Engrossed Substitute House Bill Report (Summary of Bill))

Use of Local Real Estate Excise Tax Revenues . A local government may use REET 2 revenues for the abatement of nuisance properties. Local Sales and Use Tax to Fund Services for Children and Families . The use of the revenue from the housing sales and use tax is expanded to include funding the rehabilitation, operations, and maintenance of existing units of affordable housing and facilities, which may include emergency, transitional, or supportive housing, as well as new units. The portion of the revenue that is not required to be used on affordable housing, behavioral health related facilities, evaluation and treatment centers, and facilities used for housing-related programs may additionally be used for rental assistance. Counties and cities may enter into interlocal agreements with one or more other counties, cities, or public housing authorities related to this housing tax. The agreement may include pooling tax receipts from the tax or pledging those receipts for bonds issued by one of the parties to the agreement. The sales and use tax for affordable housing that is a credit against the state's sales and use tax may be used for funding operations and maintenance costs of existing units of affordable or supportive housing, as well as for new units of such housing. Local Sales and Use Tax for Housing . The use of the revenue from the housing sales and use tax is expanded to include funding the rehabilitation, operations, and maintenance of existing units of affordable housing and facilities, as well as new units. Additionally, a portion of the revenue may be used for rental assistance. The sales and use tax for affordable housing that is a credit against the state's sales and use tax may be used for funding operations and maintenance costs of existing units of affordable or supportive housing, as well as for new units of such housing. Exceeding Regular Property Tax Levy Limitations . With voter approval, a levy lid lift for a single-year may be authorized for two consecutive years, and a multi-year annual levy lid lift may be authorized for up to 10 consecutive years. Sales Tax on Rental Cars . The use of funds generated by a county from the imposition of a sales tax on rental cars is extended to criminal justice purposes. Criminal justice purposes means activities that substantially assist the criminal justice system, which may include circumstances where ancillary benefit to the civil justice system occurs, such as: domestic violence services such as those provided by domestic violence programs, community advocates, and legal advocates; staffing adequate public defenders to provide appropriate defense for individuals; diversion programs; reentry work for inmates; local government programs that have reasonable relationship to reducing the numbers of people interacting with the criminal justice system; community placements for juveniles; and community outreach and assistance programs, alternative response programs, and mental health crisis response. Flood Control Zone Districts Recovery Assistance . In addition to their other authority, flood control zone districts may provide funds for county-administered flood recovery assistance to households and businesses damaged in a flood that is the subject of an emergency proclamation by the Governor.
