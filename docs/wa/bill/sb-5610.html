What the bill does (WA Senate Bill Report (Summary of Bill))

The Commission is authorized to impose fees to pay federal fees and be in compliance with the Horseracing Integrity and Safety Act of 2020. Beginning July 1, 2027, and July 1st of each year thereafter, the Office of the State Treasurer must transfer an amount not to exceed $1.5 annually into the newly created Washington Equine Industry Federal Regulatory Account. Expenditures from the account may be used to pay fees charged by HISA or direct required costs as determined by the commission pursuant to HISA. The amount deposited into the Washington Equine Industry Federal Regulatory Account will be funded by the imposition of state sales and use taxes charged or levied on: purchases made by or purchases sold and tax collected by a class 1 racing organization; taxes submitted to the Commission; and fees submitted to the Commission. EFFECT OF CHANGES MADE BY BUSINESS, FINANCIAL SERVICES & TRADE COMMITTEE (First Substitute): The tax credit is removed. The emergency clause is removed. The first date of the transfer from the general fund is delayed to July 1, 2027.
