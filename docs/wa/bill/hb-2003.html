HB 2003 · Columbia river endorsement

Creates a fee-based Columbia River salmon and steelhead fishing endorsement, with revenue dedicated to a special account, and sunsets it in 2028.

What the bill does (WA House Bill Report (Summary of Bill))

A Columbia River Salmon and Steelhead Endorsement (Endorsement) is required for any person 15 years or older to fish recreationally for salmon or steelhead in the Columbia River and its tributaries. The cost of the Endorsement is $7.50 for residents and nonresidents, and $6 for youth and seniors. The Columbia River Recreational Salmon and Steelhead Endorsement Program Account (Account) is created, and receives revenue from the Endorsement. Expenditures from the Account may only be used to facilitate recreational salmon and steelhead selective fishing opportunities on the Columbia River and its tributaries, including monitoring, hatchery production, pinniped removal, and enforcement. Only the Director of the Department of Fish and Wildlife (DFW) or the Director's designee may authorize expenditures, and appropriations are not required for expenditures. The DFW, working with relevant stakeholders, is required to review the Endorsement, prepare a brief summary of Endorsement activities, and provide this summary and a recommendation on whether the program should be continued to the appropriate committees of the Legislature by December 1, 2026. The Endorsement and Account expire on January 1, 2028.
