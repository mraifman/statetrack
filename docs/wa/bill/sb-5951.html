What the bill does (WA Senate Bill Report (Summary of Bill))

Authorization for a School Supply of Albuterol. Beginning in the 2027-28 school year, school districts, charter schools, state-tribal education compact schools, and private schools are authorized to maintain a supply of albuterol at each school in a designated location. Albuterol may be acquired from donation sources, but it must be accompanied by a prescription. Albuterol from the school or school district supply authorized must be accessed and administered in accordance with a uniform procedure for the management of a student's respiratory symptoms, including breathing difficulties caused by conditions such as asthma, as follows: if the student has a prescription for albuterol on file, a school nurse or designated trained school personnel may administer albuterol; or if the student does not have albuterol or a prescription for albuterol on file, only a school nurse may administer albuterol. Albuterol may be maintained, accessed, and administered on private school or school district property, including the school building, playground, and school bus, as well as during field trips or sanctioned excursions away from school property. The school nurse or designated trained school personnel may transport and carry a reasonable quantity of the school or school district supply of albuterol on field trips or excursions. School employees who are not licensed for nursing care and who have not agreed in writing to the use of albuterol as a specific part of their job description, may file a written letter of refusal to use albuterol. This letter may not serve as grounds for discharge, nonrenewal of an employment contract, or other action adversely affecting the employee's contract status. Student Asthma Policy. By January 1, 2027, the Superintendent of Public Instruction and Secretary of Health must update the student asthma policy to include a uniform procedure for when school nurses and designated trained school personnel access and administer the school or school district supply of albuterol. School districts may adopt policies regarding the use of albuterol from a school or school district supply. If a school district chooses to adopt a policy, it must include the uniform procedure. Liability protections are included for a licensed health professional prescribing albuterol and a pharmacist dispensing it; and a school nurse or other school employee or contractor administering albuterol in substantial compliance with a prescription and in accordance with policy. EFFECT OF CHANGES MADE BY EARLY LEARNING & K-12 EDUCATION COMMITTEE (First Substitute): Removes the section directing the Secretary of Health to issue a statewide standing order prescribing albuterol for school supplies and removes corresponding references.
