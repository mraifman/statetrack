What the bill does (WA House Bill Report (Summary of Bill))

Washington Businesses Response to Legal Process . Any Washington business that provides electronic communication services that is served with a subpoena, warrant, court order, or other civil or criminal legal process related to the provision or receipt of protected health care services must notify the Attorney General's Office of the receipt of such legal process if the required attestation is absent or incomplete unless the business is prohibited by law or court order from providing such notice. Limitations on Public Officers and Employees . The limits on public officers and employees related to cooperation with, or the provision of information to, any individual or entity related to protected health care services are extended to agents of state or local agencies, commissions, boards, or departments.
