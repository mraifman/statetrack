SB 5901 · On-base school construction

Raises state school construction funding assistance by 15 percent for projects on military bases and excludes on-base facilities from district space inventories.

What the bill does (WA Substitute House Bill Report (Summary of Bill))

When determining eligibility for state funding assistance under the School Construction Assistance Program, school district instructional facilities located on military bases must be excluded from a district's inventory of available instructional space. The computed state funding assistance percentage must be increased by an additional 15 percent for any project located on a military base.
