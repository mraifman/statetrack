What the bill does (WA Engrossed Senate Bill Report (Summary of Bill))

(As Amended by Senate) Requires that registered apprentices at petroleum refining and petrochemical manufacturing facilities receive written notification from their employer identifying their applicable occupation and wage rates, and prohibits apprentices from performing work outside of their applicable occupations. Requires workers in apprenticeable occupations at petroleum refining and petrochemical manufacturing facilities that are neither registered apprentices nor skilled journeypersons to be paid at least the rate required for skilled journeypersons.
