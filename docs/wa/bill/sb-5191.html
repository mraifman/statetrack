SB 5191 · Dockworkers/PFML premiums

Extends the PFML employer definition to cover employer representatives of dockworkers, who take on premium and reporting duties for those workers.

What the bill does (WA Substitute House Bill Report (Summary of Bill))

The definition of "employer" in the PFML Program is expanded to include representatives for employers of dockworkers who normally work for several employers in the same industry interchangeably through a collectively bargained agreement, thereby imposing PFML Program employer responsibilities and obligations onto those representatives. However, other than for their own employees, employer representatives are not obligated to report dockworkers who are not covered by the collective bargaining agreement.
