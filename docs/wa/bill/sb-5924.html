What the bill does (WA Senate Bill Report (Summary of Bill))

The bill as referred to committee not considered. Summary of Bill (First Substitute): Beginning December 1, 2027, a pharmacist may prescribe the following: immunizations; opioid antagonists and treatments for addiction; epinephrine autoinjectors; antihistamine agents; tobacco cessation products; medications to prevent HIV; products used to test for tuberculosis; hormonal contraception; medications to treat or prevent disease related to travel; and prescribe drugs, drug categories, or devices that are limited to conditions that: do not require a new diagnosis; are minor and generally self-limiting; have a test that is used to guide diagnosis or clinical decision making, and are waived under the federal Clinical Laboratory Improvement Amendments (CLIA) program; involve devices waived under the federal CLIA program; or are prescribed in team-based practices with a shared medical record. The definition of “practice of pharmacy” is expanded to include limited diagnosing and prescribing or ordering of certain drugs and devices. PQAC is authorized to adopt rules to administer and implement the expanded prescribing authority of pharmacists.
