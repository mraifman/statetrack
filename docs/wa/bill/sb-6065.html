SB 6065 · School vehicle funds

Lets financially distressed school districts borrow interest-free from, or petition OSPI to transfer, their transportation vehicle fund money to other school funds.

What the bill does (WA Senate Bill Report (Summary of Bill))

Interest-Free Interfund Loans. A school district that is in binding conditions or under enhanced financial oversight may take a temporary, interest-free interfund loan from its transportation vehicle fund, subject to the same conditions as temporary, interest-free loans authorized from its capital projects fund. Interfund Transfers. A school district that is in binding conditions or under enhanced financial oversight may petition OSPI for permission to transfer money from its transportation vehicle fund to another school fund. If a school district has taken a temporary interfund loan from its transportation vehicle fund, the petition may include a request to convert the temporary interfund loan to a permanent transfer with no requirement for repayment. OSPI may only approve the petition if the transfer is not detrimental to any function or project for which the transportation vehicle fund was established. OSPI rulemaking authority is modified to allow for such transfers to take place.
