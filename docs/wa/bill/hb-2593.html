What the bill does (WA House Bill Report (Summary of Bill))

Minimum Balances for General Funds . Each school district with 2,000 or more average annual full-time equivalent (FTE) students must have a general fund year ending balance of no less than 6 percent of their prior year's state apportionment. For other school districts, their general fund year ending balance must be no less than 8 percent of their prior year's state apportionment. The required minimum amounts—or fund balance thresholds—must be calculated by Office of the Superintendent of Public Instruction (OSPI) for each school district for review and adoption by its board of directors. Formal Financial Monitoring . Beginning in the 2027-28 school year, the OSPI must place school districts with general fund balances that are below required minimums at the end of the year on formal financial monitoring. School districts placed under formal financial monitoring, beginning in the 2028-29 school year, must submit monthly financial data to the OSPI. The data must include the following information for each fund of the school district: monthly expenditures; monthly revenue; end-of-month cash balance; interfund loan activity; and any other debt or borrowing transactions. For each school district placed under formal financial monitoring, the OSPI must: provide technical assistance to support the school district and avoid the imposition of binding conditions; and specify whether the school district has failed to meet the minimum general fund balance threshold in the district's financial health indicators. Substitute Bill Compared to Original Bill: In comparison to the original bill, the substitute bill: removes provisions requiring school districts to implement minimum general fund balance requirements by the 2030-31 school year, and instead requires the Office of the Superintendent of Public Instruction (OSPI), beginning in the 2027-28 school year, to place school districts with general fund balances that are below required minimum amounts at the end of the year on formal financial monitoring; removes provisions establishing maximum allowable amounts for general fund balances; removes provisions establishing allowable uses of restricted fund balance resources, including requirements for resolutions by the school district's board of directors and fund repayment plans approved by the OSPI; requires school districts that have been placed under formal financial monitoring to meet specified monthly financial reporting requirements; removes provisions authorizing the OSPI to withhold monthly apportionment payments if a school district under formal financial monitoring fails to submit required monthly financial data within 45 days of the end of a reporting month; requires the OSPI to provide technical assistance to each school district placed under formal financial monitoring to support the school district and avoid the imposition of binding conditions, and to specify in the school district's financial health indicators whether a school district has failed to meet minimum general fund balance requirements; and makes nonsubstantive technical and grammar edits, such as changing "2,000 average annual full-time equivalent students or greater" to "2,000 or more average annual full-time equivalent students," and permitting agency rules to be adopted by the "Office of" the Superintendent of Public Instruction.
