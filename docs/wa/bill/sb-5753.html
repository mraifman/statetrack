What the bill does (WA Senate Bill Report (Orig.) (Summary of Bill))

A public housing task force is established to look at: using a public housing paradigm as means of meeting housing demand for the future; and leveraging publicly constructed, owned, and operated mixed-income housing. The task force must provide a preliminary report to the Legislature by July 1, 2026. The report must, at minimum, review and consider the following: the extent to which funding is being used or could be used towards development of mixed-income housing; alternative funding strategies for publicly owned mixed-use housing; review of the land inventory conducted by state agencies and other available public land to determine if the land could be used for publicly owned housing development and whether a state land bank or similar structure could facilitate development of affordable housing; review of the United Nations Human Rights Office of the high commissioner's definition of housing adequacy and develop a strategic plan to create livable communities; and structural governance and financing changes that may be necessary to the state's housing strategy to address the housing crisis on a statewide basis. The task force must provide a final report to the Governor and Legislature by November 1, 2026 that includes recommendations regarding: how to restructure public housing development to address affordability at all income levels; strategies to implement public housing to increase affordability of units; and strategies to create whole livable communities using public housing as the anchor strategies with all necessary amenities required for healthy communities.
