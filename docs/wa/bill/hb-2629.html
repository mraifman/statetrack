What the bill does (WA House Bill Analysis 2026 (Summary of Bill))

Scrap Metal Businesses . It is unlawful to sell or offer for sale transaction records involving nonferrous metal property. Transactions involving nonferrous metal property may not be made with cash and may only be paid with a stored value device, electronic funds transfer, other electronic payment method, or a nontransferable check. A scrap metal business may pay up to a maximum of $30 in a stored value device or electronic funds transfer for nonferrous metal property. The limitations in transaction methods do not apply to transactions between commercial accounts. Civil penalties are established for a scrap metal business who engages in a pledge for, purchase of, or the trade of stolen copper that was used in telecommunications cable. A first violation is up to $10,000, a second violation is up to $20,000, and a third violation must result in loss of licensure. A scrap metal business that commits three violations must immediately surrender their license. Criminal Offense Established for Destruction of Critical Communications Infrastructure . Destruction of critical communications infrastructure is established as criminal profiteering as a class C felony ranked at a seriousness level II. A person is guilty of destruction of critical communications infrastructure if, with intent to cause damage, commit theft, or cause harm to communications infrastructure, including both physical and cyber components that deliver voice, video, and data services, the person causes damage to or renders inoperable such voice, video, or data services. In addition, a person who sustains injury to their person, business, or property by a person committing destruction of critical communications infrastructure may file an action against the perpetrator for the recovery of damages and reasonable attorney's fees and costs.
