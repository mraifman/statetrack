What the bill does (WA Substitute Senate Bill Report (Summary of Bill))

Targeted Underdeveloped Urban Areas Deferral. The bill clarifies that a recipient of a conditional certificate for the deferral must apply to DOR for a tax deferral certificate before the initiation of the construction. Besides other required documentation, the applications must also include a copy of the conditional certificate issued by the city that detailing specifics of the investment project conditionally approved and clarifying any portions of the project not approved for a tax deferral. The bill also requires conditional recipients of the deferral to provide a copy of the city's determination to DOR when notifying DOR of the project's completion to schedule an audit of the deferred taxes. Local Tax Rate Changes. For local REET tax change, local jurisdictions must notify DOR and the county treasurer at least 60 days in advance and provide a copy of the signed ordinance, resolution, or other action authorizing the tax change. Tax changes can only be effective on January 1st, April 1st, July 1st, or October 1st of the year. For lodging tax changes that are not a state-shared lodging tax, local jurisdictions must notify DOR at least 75 days for a tax change and provide certain documentation. Tax changes can only be effective on January 1st, April 1st, or July 1st of the year. If the lodging tax change is for a state-shared lodging tax, DOR must be notified of the change at least 30 days in advance, and the tax change must be effective on the first day of any calendar month.
