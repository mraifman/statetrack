SB 6231 · Data center tax exemptions

Ends sales tax exemptions for refurbished rural data centers and removes replacement server equipment from the exemption for all eligible data centers.

What the bill does (WA Engrossed Substitute House Bill Report (Summary of Bill))

New sales tax exemption certificates may not be issued on or after July 1, 2026, for rural county data centers that qualify through refurbishment. The exemption granted to rural county data centers that qualify through refurbishment expires July 1, 2026. Data centers refurbished on or after July 1, 2026, do not qualify as eligible data centers for the purpose of the urban county data center sales tax exemption. For both rural and urban county data centers, replacement server equipment no longer qualifies as eligible server equipment for the purpose of data center sales tax exemption. The definition of replacement server equipment is removed.
