What the bill does (WA Senate Bill Report (Orig.) (Summary of Bill))

An officer or family member of an officer of an organization receiving funding must not, directly or indirectly, have a beneficial interest in, or receive any compensation, gratuity, or reward in connection with, a loan or grant distributed under the Account, Affordable Housing Program or CHP. Community Reinvestment Account. Commerce must distribute grants in collaboration with by and for community organizations as defined by Commerce and the Office of Equity. Priority must be given to grant recipients who demonstrate greater need based on: income; family size; cost, condition, and energy efficiency of available residential housing; availability of decent, safe, and sanitary housing; age or infirmity; and applicable federal, state and local requirements. Commerce must evaluate the distribution of the grants biannually and make evaluations available to the Legislature upon request. The evaluation must contain the eligibility requirements, the number of participants and their corresponding eligibility categories, and metrics for determining success and success rates. Affordable Housing Program. The organizations that Commerce must prioritize allocating at least 10 percent of the money to are updated to include organizations that serve and are substantially governed by individuals disproportionately impacted by homelessness. This may include, but is not limited to black, indigenous, and other people of color and, lesbian, gay, bisexual, queer, transgender, and other gender-diverse individuals. Commerce must evaluate the distribution of the loan or grant projects biannually and make evaluations available to the Legislature upon request. The evaluation must contain the eligibility requirements, the number of participants and their corresponding eligibility categories, and metrics for determining success and success rates.
