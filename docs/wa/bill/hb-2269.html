HB 2269 · Middle housing/uninc. areas

Requires middle housing in rural and nonrural LAMIRDs to be served by public sewer or, in rural counties, a large on-site sewage system.

What the bill does (WA Substitute House Bill Report (Summary of Bill))

Middle housing in limited areas of more intensive rural development (LAMIRDs) in rural counties must be served either by a publicly owned sanitary sewer system or a large on-site sewage system, which is defined as an on-site sewage system with a design flow between 3,500 gallons per day and 100,000 gallons per day. A rural county is defined as a county with a population density of fewer than 100 persons per square mile or a county smaller than 225 square miles. Middle housing in LAMIRDs in nonrural counties may be served only by a publicly owned sanitary sewer system.
