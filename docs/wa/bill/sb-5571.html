SB 5571 · Exterior cladding materials

Prohibits cities and counties from requiring or excluding exterior cladding materials that comply with the state building code.

What the bill does (WA Senate Bill Report (Summary of Bill))

A city, code city, and county are prohibited from requiring or excluding exterior cladding materials that are in compliance with the Code. Exterior cladding is defined as a nonload-bearing material attached to the exterior of a building. The limitation in this section does not apply to homeowners' associations and plat communities, common interest communities in which units have been created by subdivision, or short subdivision.
