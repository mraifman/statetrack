HB 2296 · Distributed energy resources

Lets utilities invest in efficiency upgrades for rental housing, recover costs through tenant bills, and requires owners to give tenants 30 days' notice before work.

What the bill does (WA Substitute House Bill Report (Summary of Bill))

Utility Company Rental Housing Energy Investments . The Utilities and Transportation Commission (UTC) must allow an electric or natural gas company (utility company) to invest in programs that achieve energy conservation and improve the efficiency of energy end use of single-family and multifamily rental housing (rental housing energy investments), instead of requiring the owner to finance cost-effective measures. The UTC may allow the utility company to earn a return on cost-effective rental housing energy investments over a time period that: reduces the customer's energy burden and minimizes the investment's impact of the customer's bill; and provides a return on equity that incentivizes the utility to make the investment. The utility company must prioritize rental housing energy investments to reduce the energy burden of low-income customers, vulnerable populations, and customers in highly impacted communities while meeting the customer's comfort and productivity needs. Rental housing energy investments must be secured through the meter and recovered through the regular billing paid by the tenant, successor tenant, or owner of the premises. If the bill is paid by the owner, the utility company's rental housing energy investment must be recovered pursuant to a site-specific services agreement. The rental housing energy investment must be: recovered as any other energy charge until the authorized return on the investment has been fully recovered; and designated as an energy savings charge as a line-item on the customer's bill. The utility company must provide the owner sufficient information for the owner to provide to their tenant who is responsible for paying the electric or gas bill. The owner must notify a tenant at least 30 days prior to the commencement of work to install conservation measures at the premises. The notice must include a description of the work to be performed and expected benefits of the conservation measure.
