HB 2041 · Postpartum coverage

Requires the Health Care Authority to keep pregnant and postpartum people continuously eligible for postpartum Medicaid coverage for six months, regardless of income changes.

What the bill does (WA Substitute Senate Bill Report (Orig.) (Summary of Bill))

HCA must ensure that all persons approved for pregnancy or postpartum coverage at any time are continuously eligible for postpartum coverage for six months after the pregnancy ends regardless of whether they experience a change in income during the period of eligibility.
