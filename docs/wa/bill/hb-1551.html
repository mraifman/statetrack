HB 1551 · Cannabis social equity prg.

Extends the cannabis social equity licensing program to 2034, requires an evaluation report, and removes a grant eligibility restriction.

What the bill does (WA Engrossed Substitute Senate Bill Report (Summary of Bill))

The program's expiration date is extended from July 1, 2032, to July 1, 2034. By December 1, 2025, LCB must, in consultation with Commerce, evaluate the program and submit a report to the Governor and appropriate committees of the Legislature with findings and policy options. In conducting the evaluation, LCB must provide opportunities for public comment on the program from communities throughout Washington. The evaluation must include the following components: a review of feedback received by LCB in public comments from individuals the program is intended to benefit, the public, and the cannabis industry; an examination of the issuance and reissuance of cannabis retailer licenses that began before January 1, 2025, including a comparative analysis of the applicants who successfully secured a location and were issued a cannabis retailer license relative to applicants who remain pending but were issued a preliminary letter of approval by LCB; an examination, in consultation with Commerce, of the awarding of grants and the provision of mentorship and opportunities for the alignment of LCB's implementation of the program with Commerce's implementation of the Cannabis Social Equity Technical Assistance Grant Program; the demographic information about owners of licensed cannabis businesses who became licensed under the program to the extent the information is available or obtainable by LCB; the identification of any provisions of law or rule and any economic, market, or practical factors that effectively prevent or hinder the successful opening, operation, and business success of cannabis businesses licensed under the program; and an examination of the impact of provisions in laws and rules on cannabis licensees in the program with respect to: permissible locations for the siting of licensed cannabis businesses including specified distance restrictions, zoning or other location restrictions in local government ordinances, and specified local written objections; the mobility of cannabis licenses to or between jurisdictions; the ability of persons holding an existing cannabis retailer license or title certificate for a cannabis retailer business in a local jurisdiction subject to a ban or moratorium on cannabis retail businesses to apply for a cannabis license under the program; prioritizing license applications through use of a third-party contractor using a scoring rubric developed by LCB; restrictions on the transfer or assumption of a cannabis license issued through the program other than to individuals or groups of individuals who comply with the requirements for initial licensure as a social equity applicant for a period of at least five years from the date of initial licensure; and the definition of a social equity applicant. The grant eligibility restriction related to the Cannabis Social Equity Technical Assistance Grant Program that requires cannabis licensees who meet the social equity applicant criteria to be licensed after April 1, 2023, and before July 1, 2024, is removed.
