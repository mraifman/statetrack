SB 5346 · Student mobile device use

Revises digital citizenship to include mobile device norms, directs OSPI to post related research and best practices, and requires a 2026 report on school phone-limit policies.

What the bill does (WA Substitute Senate Bill Report (Summary of Bill))

Digital Citizenship, Media Literacy, and Internet Safety. The definition of digital citizenship is revised to include that the norms of appropriate, responsible, and healthy behavior related to current technology use includes the use of mobile devices. "Mobile device" means any personal mobile telephone or other portable electronic communication device with which a user engages in a call or writes or sends a message, or any device in which the user plays a game or watches a video. Mobile device does not include school-issued devices. In its web-based location to support digital citizenship, media literacy, and Internet safety, OSPI must include research on student use of mobile devices and recommended best practice strategies for teaching students how to use their mobile devices responsibly. OSPI must also distribute information to public schools on how to access the web-based location. Report on Student Use of Mobile Devices. By December 15, 2026, OSPI must submit an initial report with: a summary of the policies and procedures that have been adopted by public schools in Washington and in other states limiting student use of mobile devices during instructional hours; and a summary of the barriers that public schools have experienced when implementing these policies and procedures.
