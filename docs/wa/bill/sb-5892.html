SB 5892 · Voter registration database

Bars county elections offices from releasing voter database records, requires requests go to the Secretary of State, and makes knowing unauthorized disclosure a class C felony.

What the bill does (WA Senate Bill Report (Summary of Bill))

If a county elections office receives a request for records from or any existing reports generated by the statewide voter registration database, the county elections office is prohibited from producing any records in response to the request and must direct the requestor to submit their request to the Secretary of State. A knowing violation of this prohibition is a class C felony. Any state or local election officer, or a designee, who has access to any county or statewide voter registration database who knowingly discloses information in the database inconsistent with the performance of their duties is guilty of a class C felony. The Washington driver's license number, Washington identification card number, social security number, and full birthdate, shown on an affidavit of voter registration or included in a voter registration file are confidential and must not be disclosed to any person except to state or local election officers and election officials unless authorized by the Office of the Secretary of State. Any state officer or election officer, or designee, who knowingly violates this prohibition is guilty of a class C felony.
