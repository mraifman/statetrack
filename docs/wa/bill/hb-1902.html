What the bill does (WA Engrossed Substitute Senate Bill Report (Summary of Bill))

(As Amended by Senate) Requires the Washington State Department of Transportation, in consultation with the Department of Ecology, to convene a work group of state, local, and tribal representatives to develop recommendations to streamline the permitting of transportation projects.
