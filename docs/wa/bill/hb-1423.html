HB 1423 · Vehicle noise cameras

What the bill does (WA Engrossed Substitute Senate Bill Report (Summary of Bill))

Between January 1, 2026, and July 1, 2028, WTSC must oversee a pilot program, for up to three cities with a population of at least 2000, that implements the use of automated vehicle noise enforcement cameras (noise enforcement cameras) in zones designated by ordinance as vehicle-racing camera enforcement zones. Under the pilot program, noise enforcement cameras may be used to detect violations of noise standards that exceed the maximum permissible vehicle sound levels established by Ecology in rule. Noise enforcement cameras may only record audio of the vehicle immediately before, during, and after a violation. Amplified music that exceeds maximum permissible vehicle sound levels and any other audio not related to such sound levels recorded by vehicle noise enforcement cameras may not be used as evidence that vehicle noise standards have been exceeded. Any video or audio recording by a noise enforcement camera must be deleted within 30 days of either a decision to not issue an infraction or final adjudication or payment of an issued infraction. Enforcement of noise standard violations detected through the use of noise enforcement cameras are exempt from testing and inspection procedures adopted in rule by the WSP. WTSC must provide a report on the pilot program to the transportation committees of the Legislature by January 1, 2028, which includes information on the number of noise enforcement cameras in use by type and location, with an analysis of camera placement in the context of area demographics and household incomes. The report must also include the number of violations that occurred at each location. Cities using noise enforcement cameras must provide WTSC with the data requested for this report. Noise enforcement cameras are defined as a type of automated traffic safety camera that uses a combination of camera and microphone technologies to capture audio and visual data on a device in an encrypted manner and used to detect violations of certain noise standards, and are subject to the restrictions applicable to local automated traffic safety cameras. EFFECT OF TRANSPORTATION COMMITTEE AMENDMENT(S): Limits the Noise Enforcement Camera Pilot Program to no more than three cities above a population of 2000. Clarifies that the maximum permissible vehicle sound levels used to detect violations with noise enforcement cameras are those adopted in state Department of Ecology rule and not within a local ordinance option. Prohibits any other recorded audio not related to maximum permissible vehicle sound levels from being used as evidence for establishing a noise violation. Requires the deletion of any video or audio recording by noise enforcement cameras within 30 days of either a decision to not issue an infraction or final adjudication or payment of an issued infraction.
