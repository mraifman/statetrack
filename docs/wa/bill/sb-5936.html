What the bill does (WA Substitute House Bill Report (Summary of Bill))

Business Liability for Trafficking or Coercion . A business may only be prosecuted for Trafficking or Coercion of Involuntary Servitude when the business knowingly engages in Trafficking, or when an employee or agent engages in Trafficking, the conduct is a pattern of activity that meets one of the offenses, the conduct directly benefits the business, and the entity knew the conduct was occurring and failed to take effective action to stop it. When a business is prosecuted for Trafficking or Coercion of Involuntary Servitude, the court may consider the severity of the business's conduct, and may order additional penalties. The additional penalties may include: a fine up to $1 million per offense; profit disgorgement from the conduct; and debarment from state and local government contracts. Victim Confidentiality . Law enforcement officers and prosecuting agencies are required to keep the identity, pictures, and images of the alleged victim and the victim's family confidential for investigations and prosecutions of Trafficking or Coercion of Involuntary Servitude. Disclosure is permitted when it is necessary for the investigation or prosecution, required by law or a court order, or necessary to ensure services or benefits are provided for the victim or victim's family. Public Records Exemption . An exemption is added to the Public Records Act for the identity, pictures, and images of the alleged victim or victim's family in an investigation or prosecution for Trafficking or Coercion of Involuntary Servitude. Alien Crime Victims . If a state or local law enforcement agency, prosecutor, administrative judge, hearing office, or other authority determines that an individual does not meet the requirements for the certifying agency's determination regarding helpfulness to the detection, investigation, or prosecution, then the certifying agency is required to inform the individual of the reason for its determination. The individual may make another request for a determination and submit additional evidence.
