HB 1952 · Special license plates

Creates a task force to review special license plate processes and costs, and pauses new special plate applications through June 30, 2029.

What the bill does (WA House Bill Report (Summary of Bill))

The bill creates a legislative task force consisting of two members from the DOL, two members from each of the two largest caucuses of the Senate, and two members from each of the two largest caucuses of the House of Representatives. At least one member from each caucus must be a member of either one of the transportation committees of the Legislature. The task force is directed to review the following: (1) application requirements and evaluation of special license plate requests; (2) cost of implementing new special license plates and a funding policy for future requests; (3) identifying metrics the DOL must use to recommend the discontinuation of special license plates; (4) the state's ability to recoup the cost of new special license plates; and (5) efficiency in processing special license plate legislation. The task force must report its findings and recommendations to the Legislature by December 1, 2025. The bill pauses the application process for new special license plates through June 30, 2029, with exception to the special license plates created in House Bill 1368, if the bill is enacted. Substitute Bill Compared to Original Bill: The substitute bill adds an additional requirement for the task force to identify metrics for discontinuation of special license plates.
