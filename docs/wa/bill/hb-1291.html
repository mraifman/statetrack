What the bill does (WA Engrossed Substitute Senate Bill Report (Summary of Bill))

For health plans, including health plans offered to state and school employees, issued or renewed on or after January 1, 2026, that provide coverage for labor and delivery services, labor and delivery services are not subject to the enrollee's deductible. Any cost sharing for labor and delivery services paid by an enrollee must be applied toward the enrollee's deductible obligation. For a health plan that provides coverage for labor and delivery services and is offered as a qualifying health plan for a health savings account (HSA), the health carrier must establish the plan's deductible for the coverage of the labor and delivery services at the minimum level necessary to preserve the enrollee's ability to claim tax-exempt contributions from their HSA under internal revenue service laws and regulations. OIC may adopt rules necessary to implement this act as it relates to OIC regulated health plans. EFFECT OF HEALTH & LONG-TERM CARE COMMITTEE AMENDMENT(S): Clarifies that OIC has rule-making authority for section 1 of this act.
