SB 6260 · Public education funding

Cuts Running Start funded enrollment to 1.2 FTEs, lengthens school bus depreciation lifetimes, and lets Transition to Kindergarten programs charge tuition or sliding-scale fees.

What the bill does (WA Engrossed Substitute Senate Bill Report (Summary of Bill))

School Bus Purchasing and Reimbursement. Beginning with school buses that are scheduled for depreciation payments as of September 2025, and those qualifying for payments after that date, OSPI must use a minimum anticipated lifetime of 120 months—10 years—for category A buses, and 180 months—15 years—for category C and D buses. If a school district is awarded a federal grant or rebate to purchase a student transportation vehicle, OSPI must adjust the district's reimbursement payments generated by that purchase by reducing the quote for that category of vehicles by the amount of the grant or rebate. Running Start Enrollment. After the conclusion of the 2025-26 school year, students participating in RS programs may be funded up to a combined maximum enrollment of 1.2 FTEs, rather than 1.4 FTEs. Transition to Kindergarten Programs. OSPI must develop rules for the allocation of state funding that prioritizes existing TTK programs and areas that lack early learning providers. Funding for the TTK Program is limited to the amount of funding specified in the omnibus appropriations act and must be allocated based on the prioritized criteria developed by OSPI. TTK children must be counted as kindergarten students for the purposes of calculating special education funding. OSPI rules must authorize school districts, charter schools, and state-tribal compact schools to charge tuition or sliding scale fees for enrollment in a TTK program, except to children with disabilities entitled to a free appropriate public education.
