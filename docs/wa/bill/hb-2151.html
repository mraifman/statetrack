HB 2151 · Factory built structures

Revises factory-built structure rules: removes the agency contract requirement, allows third-party inspections, and exempts utility energy-storage and electrical enclosures from L&I approval and inspection.

What the bill does (WA Substitute House Bill Report (Summary of Bill))

When adopting rules for factory-built housing and commercial structures, L&I must consider the national consensus standards published by the International Code Council for off-site construction planning, design, fabrication, and assembly, the national consensus standards published by the International Code Council for inspections and regulatory compliance, and the standards and specifications contained in the International Building, Uniform Plumbing, and International Mechanical Codes, among others. The requirement for an agency to have a contract with L&I is removed. Instead, L&I rules may include options for inspections to be conducted by a qualified inspection agency. A prefabricated enclosure is exempt from the L&I approval and inspection process if: the enclosure is for energy storage systems or electrical equipment associated with the generation, control, transformation, transmission, or distribution of energy installations under the ownership and exclusive control of an electric utility; and entry to the enclosure is limited to qualified service personnel for the maintenance, repair, or occasional monitoring of energy storage system units or electrical equipment.
