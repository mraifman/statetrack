HB 2422 · Private security guards

Removes security guard license fees for applicants, requires employers to pay employee licensing fees, and bars deducting those costs from wages.

What the bill does (WA House Bill Report (Summary of Bill))

The requirement that an applicant for a security guard license pay a fee to be licensed is removed. An applicant is instead required to include their employer's billing pin as part of their application. A private security company is directed to pay the application, license renewal, and endorsement fee for all private security guards employed by or who have received an offer of employment from the company. The private security company must provide its billing pin to the employee. A private security company may not require a private security guard to reimburse the application, license renewal, or endorsement fees, including deducting such costs from the employee's wages. A private security company that violates these requirements is subject to a $500 penalty per violation. A private security company that has had subsequent violations may be subject to a license suspension or revocation at the determination of the Director of the Department of Licensing. The fee for transferring a license from one employer to another is eliminated. Substitute Bill Compared to Original Bill: The substitute bill extends the effective date to November 1, 2026.
