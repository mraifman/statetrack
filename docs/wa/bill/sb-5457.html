SB 5457 · Broadcasters

Moves the radio and television broadcasting B&O tax into a new section, defines broadcasting, and exempts it from tax preference review and its 10-year expiration.

What the bill does (WA House Bill Report (Summary of Bill))

The B&O tax for radio and television broadcasting business activities is moved into a new section of the B&O tax code. Radio and television broadcasting activities are defined as the delivery of audio, video, and written information by a person operating as a radio or television broadcasting station licensed, regulated, and issued a call sign by the Federal Communications Commission, including, but not limited to, delivery by wire, satellite, or any other means. An exemption from a TPPS, JLARC review, and the 10-year expiration is included.
