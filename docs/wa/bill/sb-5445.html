SB 5445 · Distributed energy resources

Lets utilities meet clean energy targets with conservation and demand response, quadruples credit for certain distributed generation, exempts distributed solar from SEPA review, and keeps agrivoltaics on farmland from triggering added tax.

What the bill does (WA Engrossed Substitute House Bill Report (Summary of Bill))

(As Amended by House) Specifies the addition of an agrivoltaics facility to land classified as farm and agricultural land under the Open Space Taxation Act does not constitute a reclassification, withdrawal, or removal from farm and agricultural land classification and is not subject to additional tax. Creates a categorical exemption for certain distributed solar energy generation actions under the State Environmental Policy Act. Allows qualifying electric utilities to be in compliance with Energy Independence Act (EIA) targets through any combination of eligible renewable resources and certain accelerated conservation and demand response actions. Provides a quadruple multiplier towards EIA targets for the value of certain new distributed energy priority generation.
