HB 2588 · County ferry districts

Lets county ferry districts operate ferries generally, requires county advisory committees, and caps property tax levies for new non-King County districts absent voter approval.

What the bill does (WA Engrossed House Bill Report (Summary of Bill))

A county that forms a ferry district must include the whole of the county within the district. Promptly after the formation of a ferry district, if the county does not already have a ferry advisory committee, the county legislative authority must appoint a five-member County Ferry District Advisory Committee. The members must be residents of the county. At least three of the members must be frequent users of the ferry system, while the other two members must represent other areas of the county. County legislative authorities are encouraged to include at least one member of a federally recognized Indian tribe on the committee. The initial appointments must be made in two groups, with one group appointed for two years and the other group appointed for four years. Thereafter, all members are appointed for four year terms. If the county is transitioning from a county ferry system to a ferry district system, and the county had an advisory committee for the ferry system, then the members of the existing committee must be appointed as the County Ferry District Advisory Committee. Members of an advisory committee must serve without fee or compensation. A ferry district's authority to operate or take other actions related to ferries, including powers related to the use of tax revenue and bond financing, is not limited to activities involving solely passenger-only ferries, but extends to ferries in general. Ferry districts created after June 10, 2026, outside of King County may not impose a property tax levy to support the district's activities at a rate greater than 10 cents per $1,000 of assessed value unless voters within the ferry district first approve the levy at a general or special election. The prohibition on operating a ferry crossing or bridge within 10 miles of a ferry crossing or toll bridge operated by the WSDOT does not apply to the operation of ferry service by a ferry district.
