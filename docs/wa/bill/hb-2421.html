What the bill does (WA House Bill Report (Summary of Bill))

Beginning January 1, 2035, the manufacture, sale, and distribution of new vehicle tires containing 6PPD or regrettable 6PPD substitutes identified by Ecology by rule is prohibited. These restrictions do not apply to products that are certified, regulated, or specified by the following federal agencies: the United States Federal Aviation Administration, the United States Department of Defense, the United States Department of Homeland Security, or the National Aeronautics and Space Administration. In adopting rules, Ecology must consider information generated by other states or nations to identify regrettable 6PPD substitutes, and must, at minimum, identify as a regrettable 6PPD substitute any alternative that is evaluated under Safer Products and determined not to be a safer alternative to 6PPD. Ecology may adopt rules to implement, administer, and enforce requirements related to 6PPD and regrettable 6PPD substitutes in vehicle tires. Violations are subject to civil penalties of up to $5,000 for a first offense and $10,000 for repeat offenses. Penalties are appealable to the Pollution Control Hearings Board and must be deposited in the Model Toxics Control Operating Account. The restrictions on 6PPD and regrettable 6PPD substitutes in vehicle tires do not limit the authority of Ecology under Safer Products. Substitute Bill Compared to Original Bill: Substitute House Bill 2421 as compared to the original bill: eliminates the new 6PPD mitigation fee on motor vehicle tires that contain 6PPD; specifies that the 2035 restriction on 6PPD and regrettable 6PPD substitutes in tires applies only to vehicle tires; and excludes, from the new 2035 restrictions on 6PPD in vehicle tires, products that are certified, regulated, or specified by the following federal agencies: (1) the United States Federal Aviation Administration; (2) the United States Department of Defense; (3) the United States Department of Homeland Security; or (4) the National Aeronautics and Space Administration.
