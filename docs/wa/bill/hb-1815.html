HB 1815 · Prison riot offenses

Narrows the prison riot offense to exclude juvenile facilities, vacates related convictions, requires resentencing, and moves riot behavior in DCYF facilities to internal infractions.

What the bill does (WA Engrossed Substitute House Bill Report (Summary of Bill))

The term "correctional institution" is limited, for purposes of the Prison Riot offense, to apply to places designated by law primarily for keeping a person age 18 or older in custody and specifically excludes facilities operated by the Department of Children, Youth, and Families (DCYF) and county juvenile detention facilities. This modification to the Prison Riot offense applies retroactively to all prison riot convictions or adjudications and all prison riot offenses that have been charged within five years before the effective date of the bill. Any person who was convicted of a Prison Riot offense who was incarcerated in a facility operated by the DCYF or a county juvenile detention facility at the time of the offense may apply to the sentencing court for vacation of the applicant's record of adjudication or conviction of the offense. If the applicant qualifies for vacation, the court must grant the request. Prosecutors are required to make a motion for relief from sentence for cases in which an offender has been sentenced or adjudicated for an offense where an adjudication or conviction for Prison Riot that occurred in a DCYF facility or a county juvenile detention facility was used as the basis for the offender's sentence or disposition. The court that imposed the sentence or disposition must grant the motion if it finds that the current or past adjudication or conviction for a Prison Riot offense that occurred in a facility operated by the DCYF or juvenile detention facility was used as a basis for the person's disposition or sentence and set an expedited date for resentencing. The DCYF must establish rules for including prison riot behavior as an infraction that is managed through the internal behavioral management infraction system. By August 1, 2025, the DCYF must respond to prison riot behavior that occurs in an institution using the internal behavioral management infraction system. The DCYF may impose an infraction using the internal behavioral management infraction system for offenses that were vacated when appropriate.
