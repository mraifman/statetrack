What the bill does (WA Engrossed Substitute Senate Bill Report (Summary of Bill))

Taxation of Nicotine Products. Beginning January 1, 2027, the various OTP tax rates and the vapor products tax are each repealed and replaced with a single tax rate of 95 percent of the taxable selling price for all nicotine products. "Nicotine product" means any product that contains nicotine or a nicotine analogue, whether derived from tobacco or created synthetically, and is intended for human consumption or placement in the oral or nasal cavity or absorption into the human body by any other means. The term does not include cigarettes or therapeutic nicotine cessation products approved by the United States Food and Drug Administration. "Nicotine analogue" means a substance that has a chemical structure substantially similar to the chemical structure of nicotine or which has, or is represented to have, an effect on the central nervous system that is similar to or greater than the effect on the central nervous system of nicotine. Vapor Product Tax Revenue Distributions. Revenue from the tax on vapor products must be deposited as follows: 50 percent to the match transfer account and 50 percent into the FPHS account until distributions to the match transfer account reach $10 million each fiscal year; any additional revenue collected from the tax on vapor products must be deposited to the FPHS account until the total amount of distributions to the account reach $20 million each fiscal year; and the remainder of revenue collected from the tax on vapor products each fiscal year must be deposited into the state general fund. Beginning January 1, 2029, DOR must annually adjust for inflation distribution amounts to the match transfer account and the FPHS account using the consumer price index for the Seattle area as calculated by the United States Bureau of Labor Statistics or its successor agency. The inflationary adjustment may not result in a distribution amount that is less than the distribution amount for the prior year. Preexisting Inventories. The nicotine products tax established in the bill does not apply to any nicotine product for which the OTP tax was paid by the distributor prior to July 1, 2027. Cigarette Tax. Beginning January 1, 2028, an additional tax is imposed on the sale, use, consumption, handling, possession, or distribution of cigarettes at a rate of $0.09875 per cigarette. The additional tax on cigarettes results in a total tax rate of $5 per package of 20 cigarettes. Beginning January 1, 2032, the additional tax on cigarettes must be adjusted for inflation once every four years using the consumer price index for the Seattle area as calculated by the United States Bureau of Labor Statistics or its successor agency. DOR may not adjust the tax rate if the adjustment would result in a tax rate that is lower than the applicable tax rate for the prior year. All revenue from the additional taxes on cigarettes must be deposited to the state general fund. Tribal Compacting. The Governor may seek government-to-government consultations with federally recognized Indian tribes to renegotiate cigarette and vapor product tax compacts to include the taxation of flavored nicotine products and flavored cigarettes. Any tribal tax imposed on flavored nicotine products or flavored cigarettes must be equal to the state rate imposed on such products.
