What the bill does (WA Substitute House Bill Report (Summary of Bill))

The WEIAO Board members must elect cochairs biennially, and those cochairs serve for two-year terms. The WEIAO Board must meet at least four times a year, and report recommendations to the Legislature by December 31 of each year. In addition to consulting with the Workforce Training and Education Coordinating Board, the WEIAO Board must also consult with the WSAC when reviewing and determining whether workforce education investments funded from the Workforce Education Investment Account are effectively increasing student success and career readiness. The requirement that the WEIAO Board maintain a public data dashboard is eliminated.
