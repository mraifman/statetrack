SB 5995 · Zero emission cargo handling

Removes the 2031 sunset on port authority to buy zero-emission cargo equipment and on the ban on buying fully automated cargo equipment, making both permanent.

What the bill does (WA House Bill Report (Summary of Bill))

The December 31, 2031, expiration date on the authorization for port districts and port development authorities to purchase zero and near zero emissions cargo handling equipment, and on the prohibition on the districts and authorities purchasing fully automated marine cargo handling equipment, is repealed.
