SB 5467 · Water-sewer district surplus

Raises the dollar thresholds for water-sewer districts to sell surplus personal property without notice and real property privately.

What the bill does (WA House Bill Report (Summary of Bill))

The value of personal property that may be sold by a water-sewer district without notice is increased to $5,400. The value of real property that may be sold privately by a water-sewer district is increased to $7,500.
