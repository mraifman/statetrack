What the bill does (WA Engrossed Substitute Senate Bill Report (Summary of Bill))

Creates an additional 0.5 percent B&O surcharge on taxpayers with Washington taxable income over $250 million. Increases the tax rate for several existing B&O surcharges. Increases the annual cap for the Advanced Computing Surcharge. Modifies the B&O investment income deduction.
