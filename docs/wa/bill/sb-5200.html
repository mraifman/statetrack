SB 5200 · Veteran medical foster homes

Exempts veteran medical foster homes from adult family home requirements while applying long-term care worker rules, vulnerable adult status, and caregiver disqualification standards.

What the bill does (WA Engrossed Substitute House Bill Report (Summary of Bill))

An MFH is exempt from requirements regarding adult family homes if the MFH is under the oversight of and annually reviewed by the United States Department of Veterans Affairs, care is provided exclusively to three or fewer veterans, and its caregivers are in compliance with applicable state laws, including laws regarding required training, certification, and background checks. Requirements regarding long-term care workers, including requirements regarding certification and training, are applied to MFH caregivers. An MFH is added to the list of facilities where the person admitted to one of those facilities is considered a vulnerable adult. An MFH caregiver is considered to be a type of provider who may not be employed in the care of and have unsupervised access to vulnerable adults if certain circumstances are met.
