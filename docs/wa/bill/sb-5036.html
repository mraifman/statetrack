SB 5036 · Statewide emissions data

Requires Ecology and Commerce to annually report statewide greenhouse gas emissions totals to the Governor and Legislature, starting in 2026.

What the bill does (WA Senate Bill Report (Summary of Bill))

By December 31st of each year, beginning in 2026, Ecology and Commerce must annually report to the Governor and Legislature the total emissions of GHGs for the preceding year, subject to certain exceptions in existing law.
