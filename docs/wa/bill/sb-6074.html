What the bill does (WA Senate Bill Report (Summary of Bill))

Parole Implementation Work Group. The parole reinstitution work group is established. The work group's membership is enumerated. Members shall be jointly appointed by the President of the Senate and the Speaker of the House of Representatives. Staff support will be provided by the William D. Ruckelshaus Center. The work group shall make recommendations on a coordinated and comprehensive response for reinstituting parole in Washington, develop criteria for parole eligibility, and report its findings and recommendations to the Legislature by October 1, 2027 The work group expires December 31, 2027. EFFECT OF CHANGES MADE BY LAW & JUSTICE COMMITTEE (First Substitute): Strikes most of the bill except for the establishment of the parole implementation work group and renames it the parole reinstitution work group. Shifts membership appointment responsibilities from DOC to the President of the Senate and Speaker of the House of Representatives. Shifts staffing responsibilities from DOC to the William D. Ruckelshaus Center. Requires the work group to make recommendations on reinstituting parole, to develop criteria for parole eligibility, and report its findings and recommendations to the Legislature by October 1, 2027. Changes the work group expiration date to December 31, 2027. Adds two more members representing victim advocates to the work group.
