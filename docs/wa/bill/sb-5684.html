What the bill does (WA Senate Bill Report (Summary of Bill))

Prohibiting the Influence or Interference of Voters within Buildings Containing County Election Divisions and Adjacent Parking Lots. The locations where a person may not influence or interfere with a voter by means prohibited by law is expanded to include within the building that contains the division of elections for a county auditor's office, including any county owned and operated parking lot adjacent to the building and routinely used for parking at that building and any routes of access between the building and the parking lot. Declaring Findings. Legislative findings are made regarding the importance of preventing voter intimidation and election fraud, the need for reasonable buffer zones around voting locations, the fact that parking lots are extensions of election offices, and that the prohibition is narrowly tailored to protect both the first amendment rights of political campaigns and the rights of voters to be free of intimidation or influence while they attempt to vote. EFFECT OF CHANGES MADE BY STATE GOVERNMENT, TRIBAL AFFAIRS & ELECTIONS COMMITTEE (First Substitute): Clarifies the parking lot adjacent to the building containing the division of elections must be county owned and operated and routinely used for parking at that building.
