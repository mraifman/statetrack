What the bill does (WA Engrossed Third Substitute House Bill Report (Summary of Bill))

Renewable Energy Excise Tax . Personal Property Tax Exemption. All personal property used exclusively for the generation of renewable energy in a qualified energy facility that becomes operational on or after January 1, 2028, or is repowered on or after January 1, 2028, is exempt from property tax beginning in calendar year 2028. In addition, all personal property used exclusively for a battery electric storage system is exempt from property tax beginning in calendar year 2029. A qualified renewable energy facility is a solar or wind facility with nameplate capacity sufficient to generate at least 10 MW of alternating current power. It does not include renewable energy facilities owned or operated by centrally assessed utilities or by joint operating agencies that make payments in lieu of taxes. "Battery electric storage systems" means commercially available technology that is capable of retaining electricity, storing the energy for a period of time, and delivering the electricity after storage by chemical, thermal, mechanical, or other means that has at least 10 megawatt-hours of storage. It does not include any form of hydroelectric power. "Battery electric storage systems" does not include systems owned or operated by centrally assessed utilities or by joint operating agencies that make payments in lieu of taxes. Repowered means the cumulative rebuild or refurbishment of a majority, or more than half the nameplate capacity of the wind or solar energy facility due to the facility reaching the end of its useful life or useful reasonable economic life. The rebuild or refurbishment does not constitute repowering if it is part of routine major maintenance or the maintenance of or replacement of equipment that does not materially affect the expected physical or economic life of the facility. Repowering, rebuilds, and refurbishments must be reported to the county assessor as they occur. If a facility or system has undertaken repowering, rebuilds, or refurbishment prior to the enactment of this bill, then this must be reported to the county assessor within 90 days of the effective date of the act. Beginning January 1, 2028, the property tax levy calculation for all taxing districts will not include an increase in assessed value due to the construction of wind turbine and solar facilities. Each qualified renewable energy facility must file an annual report with the DOR by March 15 that provides the location by tax code area and nameplate capacity of the exempt personal property. The DOR must provide a copy of the report to the appropriate county treasurers and county assessors. An exemption to prohibitions on disclosure of taxpayer information is provided to allow the DOR to share with local taxing officials, including county treasurers and assessors, the identity and tax information of taxpayers subject to the renewable energy (RE) tax. New Renewable Energy Tax Rates. Beginning January 1, 2028, a state RE tax is imposed on qualified renewable energy systems that become operational on or after January 1, 2028, or are repowered on or after January 1, 2028, or a battery electric storage system that is operational or repowered on or after January 1, 2028. These rates apply for the life of the facility or system.
