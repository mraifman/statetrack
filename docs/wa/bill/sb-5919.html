What the bill does (WA House Bill Report (Summary of Bill))

Fire districts and insurance providers should collaborate to develop optional incentives that promote wildfire mitigation best practices related to agricultural activities. Examples of potential best practices include maintaining specific response resources such as tractors and water tanks, creating defensible spaces, and notification for local red flag warnings.
