What the bill does (WA House Bill Report (Summary of Bill))

The House Joint Memorial requests that the Major Richard Star Act be scheduled for a floor vote in Congress. The House Joint Memorial will be transmitted to the President of the United States (US), the Secretary of the US Department of Defense, the Secretary of the Department of Veterans Affairs, the President of the US Senate, the Speaker of the House of Representatives, and the two Washington Senators and 10 Representatives: Maria Cantwell, Patty Murray, Suzan DelBene, Rick Larsen, Marie Gluesenkamp Perez, Dan Newhouse, Michael Baumgartner, Emily Randall, Pramila Jayapal, Kim Schrier, Adam Smith, and Marilyn Strickland.
