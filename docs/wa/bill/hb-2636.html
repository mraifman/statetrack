What the bill does (WA Engrossed Second Substitute Senate Bill Report (Summary of Bill))

Directs the Joint Legislative Audit and Review Committee to assess policy and funding provisions identified by the Public Education Review Steering Committee, as resources allow, and report to the Governor and the Legislature.
