What the bill does (WA House Bill Analysis 2026 (Summary of Bill))

Beginning in the 2028-29 academic year, compensation must equal or exceed 75 percent of the compensation provided to full-time and tenured faculty. Beginning in the 2029-30 academic year, compensation must equal or exceed 80 percent of the compensation provided to full-time and tenured faculty. Beginning in the 2030-31 academic year, compensation must equal or exceed 85 percent of the compensation provided to full-time and tenured faculty. By January 1, 2030, the State Board for Community and Technical Colleges (SBCTC) must submit a report with an update on progress both at the system and college level toward meeting the 85 percent pay equity goal to the Governor and the higher education committees of the Legislature. By December 1, 2031, the SBCTC must report on the implementation status of pay equity plans. At minimum, the report must include: the percentage of full-time pay attained by part-time and adjunct faculty; data that includes the race, ethnicity, and other demographic characteristics of faculty not meeting pay equity plans; data that includes the academic department of faculty not meeting pay equity plans; and progress at the college level toward meeting pay equity plans. The standards used to determine pay equity must be based on the instructional work of the faculty, including direct student support, time in class, preparation for class, grading and assessment, and office hours equivalent to those required for full-time tenure-track faculty under the relevant collective bargaining agreement.
