What the bill does (WA Engrossed Substitute Senate Bill Report (Orig.) (Summary of Bill))

The abandonment by WSDOT of portions of state highways longer than two miles or including a bridge must occur pursuant to an agreement between WSDOT and the relevant city or county or through a determination by the Legislature in duly enacted legislation. The Legislature is required to make the final determination regarding the status of an existing state highway portion that exceeds two miles in length or includes a bridge, if it is planned for decommissioning and an agreement regarding its disposition between WSDOT and the relevant city or county cannot be reached. The RJT process at the Commission is expanded to include jurisdiction over WSDOT abandonments that exceed two miles in length. A prerequest conference with the requesting parties is added to the Commission's RJT process requirements to ensure the eligibility of transfer requests. The Commission must provide analysis regarding potential costs, risks, and impacts of route transfers and abandonments in its annual report to the Legislature required by the RJT process. The criteria for changes to the state highway system are updated to require strong connectivity, continuity, and functional characteristics when compared to routes already part of the state highway system. The criteria for rural state highways are modified to include connections to or between United States Census Bureau designated places or cities. The criteria for urban state highways are modified to include highways in a United States Census Bureau designated place or city. WSDOT's existing authority regarding state highway designations for city streets and county roads is made subject to the abandonment process created in the bill regarding the abandonment of portions of state highways longer than two miles or including a bridge.
