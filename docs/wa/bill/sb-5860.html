What the bill does (WA Senate Bill Report (Summary of Bill))

School Board Director Compensation and Reimbursement. The amount each director may receive as compensation is raised from $50 per day to $100 per day, and the annual limit is increased from $4,800 per year to $13,750 per year. Compensation in excess of $3,000 per year must be authorized by the board of directors at a regularly scheduled meeting. School board director compensation may only be paid from locally collected excess levy funds available for the purpose, and may not cause the state to incur any funding obligation. Adjustments in compensation apply to sitting directors at the time the adjustment becomes effective. The Office of the Superintendent of Public Instruction must review and adjust the compensation provided to directors at least every five years to reflect cumulative changes in the cost of living. Child care costs are added as an expense to be paid when a director attends a school board meeting or other meeting held pursuant to statute. School Board Director Training. Beginning January 1, 2027, WSSDA is directed to develop and provide a training program on school district funding and finance for all directors. Directors may not be charged a fee for attendance and must be compensated for participating. The training program must enhance directors' understanding of state and local funding structures, budgeting practices, and fiscal accountability requirements applicable to school districts. All currently serving directors must complete the training within one year of the requirement taking effect, while all newly elected or appointed directors must complete the training at least once during the first year of their term. Directors are strongly encouraged to renew or retake the training at least once during each term of office to remain current with changes in state and local funding laws and fiscal accountability standards. If funds are not appropriated, this requirement is null and void. EFFECT OF CHANGES MADE BY EARLY LEARNING & K-12 EDUCATION COMMITTEE (First Substitute): Specifies that all compensation provided to a school director must be paid from locally collected excess levy funds available for that purpose and may not cause the state to incur any present or future funding obligation. Directs the Office of the Superintendent of Public Instruction, rather than the Office of Financial Management, to review and adjust compensation limits for school directors.
