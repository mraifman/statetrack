SB 5820 · Freight rail dependent use

Repeals local authority to allow freight rail dependent uses on resource lands near short line railroads and in rural lands.

What the bill does (WA House Bill Report (Summary of Bill))

The authority for Clark County, and the cities in the county, to adopt development regulations to assure that natural resource lands adjacent to short line railroads can be developed for freight rail dependent uses, to include the development of freight rail dependent uses on such land in the transportation element of the county's or city's comprehensive plan, and to modify development regulations to include the development of freight rail dependent uses in rural lands, is repealed.
