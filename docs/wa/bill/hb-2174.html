HB 2174 · Accident risk zones

Lets local governments or WSDOT create crash prevention zones with increased enforcement and a fixed $73 penalty for speed and collision infractions, with penalty revenue funding safety work.

What the bill does (WA House Bill Report (Summary of Bill))

The legislative body of a county, city, or town, or the Secretary of the Washington State Department of Transportation (WSDOT), may create a Crash Prevention Zone (CPZ) identifying public roads where there have been a multitude of collisions that have caused serious injuries or fatalities to provide time for authorities to investigate and implement safety improvements in the designated areas. With the approval of a city or town, a county may designate a CPZ that includes roadways that include public roads within the county, city, or town. A county may establish roadways managed by the WSDOT within a CPZ. The WSDOT may establish a CPZ without the approval of a county, city, or town. Prior to establishing a CPZ, the WSDOT, county, city, or town must hold a public hearing where members of the public may view and comment on a map of the designated CPZ. Once a CPZ is established, the establishing jurisdiction or the WSDOT must conduct an engineering and traffic investigation of the public roads in the CPZ to identify safety improvements. The Washington State Patrol and local law enforcement agencies must coordinate increased enforcement of traffic laws within the CPZ in a manner intended to reduce accident risks. A person who is found to have committed any traffic infraction relating to speed restrictions or an infraction associated with a vehicle collision within the CPZ must receive a monetary penalty of $73, provided that signs stating that penalties are increased within the CPZ are posted. The $73 penalty received must be deposited into an account created for the CPZ. Funds in the account may be used for engineering and traffic investigations, creation and installation of road signs, safety improvements, and increased law enforcement presence within the CPZ. The CPZ must be dissolved once safety improvements have been implemented. The CPZ may also be dissolved at the discretion of the city, county, or the WSDOT at the recommendation of the WSDOT, or by a petition of 10 percent of the property owners, residents, or business owners within the CPZ or an abutting property. Substitute Bill Compared to Original Bill: The substitute bill authorizes the creation of crash prevention zones (CPZ) rather than accident risk zones. A county, city, town, or the Secretary of the Washington State Department of Transportation (WSDOT) may create a CPZ within their jurisdiction by identifying public roads where there have been a multitude of collisions that have caused serious injuries or fatalities, rather than accidents. A county may establish roadways managed by the WSDOT within a CPZ. The WSDOT may establish a CPZ without the approval of a county, city, or town. The monetary penalty assessed for traffic infractions within CPZs is $73, rather than double the normal penalty assessed, provided that signs are posted within the CPZ stating that penalties are increased. The $73 must be deposited into the CPZ account, rather than half of the monetary penalty assessed. The CPZ account must be administered and maintained by the jurisdiction in which the infraction occurred.
