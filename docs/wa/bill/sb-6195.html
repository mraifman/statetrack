What the bill does (WA Senate Bill Report (Summary of Bill))

Revenue Thresholds and Tier Conversions. At the time of license renewal, a tier three producer must demonstrate minimum gross sales reported to the Department of Revenue of $24,000 per month or $288,000 annually throughout the two-year period prior to renewal. If a tier three producer fails to meet this revenue threshold throughout the two-year period prior to renewal, the license is converted to a tier two producer license and the producer’s maximum amount of space for cannabis production is limited to 10,000 square feet. At the time of license renewal, a tier two producer must demonstrate minimum gross sales reported to the Department of Revenue of $8,000 per month or $96,000 annually throughout the two-year period prior to renewal. If a tier two producer fails to meet this revenue threshold throughout the two-year period prior to renewal, the license is converted to a tier one producer license and the producer’s maximum amount of space for cannabis production is limited to 4000 square feet. Tier one producers are not subject to the revenue threshold requirements and tier conversions. The bill defines tier one, tier two, and tier three producers based on the licensed production area. Two-Year Lookback Period. When determining the two-year period prior to renewal, time prior to the effective date of the new section must be included if the time is within two years of a license renewal occurring on or after the effective date of the new section. Exemptions. A tier three producer or tier two producer may request, and the LCB in its discretion may grant, a one-year exemption from the application of the new section if extenuating circumstances exist and the request is submitted to the LCB within 30 days of license renewal. Extenuating circumstances include, but are not limited to, natural disasters or other extraordinary events beyond the producer’s control. Conforming Changes. Cannabis producer licenses are subject to annual renewal and the revenue threshold and tier conversion requirements established in this bill. Renewal of a cannabis producer license is subject to those requirements. EFFECT OF CHANGES MADE BY LABOR & COMMERCE COMMITTEE (First Substitute): Revises the revenue-threshold framework for cannabis producer tiers by replacing the bill’s two-year, monthly-or-annual gross sales structure with cultivation-method-specific annual thresholds measured over a one-year evaluation period. Adds cultivation method categories used to determine which annual revenue threshold applies, including indoor cultivation, mixed-light cultivation, light deprivation, and outdoor cultivation. Applies specified annual revenue thresholds by cultivation method. Defines the one-year evaluation period as the 12 months immediately preceding the month in which the producer license renewal is due, and provides a limited lookback for certain renewals. Requires LCB to determine a producer’s cultivation method designation based on the predominant cultivation method reflected in the producer’s approved floor plan, and allows a producer to request reclassification by submitting an amended floor plan. Treats multiple cannabis producer licenses operated under a shared UBI as a single entity for purposes of meeting the applicable revenue thresholds by aggregating gross sales across those licenses, and directs LCB to verify UBI information through DOR records.
