What the bill does (WA Engrossed Substitute House Bill Report (Summary of Bill))

Increases the aircraft fuel tax and annual aircraft registration fees, indexing both to 2 percent annual inflation and directing the increases to two sustainable aviation fuel-related accounts. Aggregates and distributes fuel taxes received attributable to inflation in the same manner as other fuel tax increases made in 2025. Modifies the use tax base for the use tax component of the luxury vehicle tax and the recreational vessel tax. Exempts motor homes from the luxury vehicle tax for the period from July 1, 2026, to December 31, 2026. Delays the program to reimburse registered tow truck operators for the costs of towing indigent persons' vehicles to July 1, 2027. Expands the bus and bus facilities grant program to include safety or security enhancements for transit rolling stock or transit facilities and allows grant funds to be used for safety or security enhancements. Establishes a program to provide $5 reduced-fee identicards to individuals age 70 or older who voluntarily replace their driver's license with an identicard. Authorizes the Washington State Department of Transportation to send toll bills and civil penalty notices by email, in addition to by paper mail. Incorporates the authorization for a 3 percent debit card surcharge into the existing 3 percent ferry fare payment credit card surcharge authorization.
