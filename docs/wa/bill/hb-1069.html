HB 1069 · Supp. retirement bargaining

Makes DOC employees' supplemental retirement contributions, including medical plans, a mandatory bargaining subject but excludes them from interest arbitration.

What the bill does (WA House Bill Report (Summary of Bill))

For Department of Corrections (DOC) employees, contributions for supplemental retirement benefits, including medical plans, administered by or on behalf of an employee organization is a mandatory subject of bargaining, but is not subject to interest arbitration. Contributions for supplemental retirement benefits are not included in the requirement to bargain over the dollar amount expended on behalf of each employee for health care benefits.
