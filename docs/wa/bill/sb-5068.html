Sets citizenship or federal work authorization as the eligibility standard for certain public safety and civil service jobs and deputy prosecutors, including current employees.

What the bill does (WA Engrossed Senate Bill Report (Summary of Bill))

Citizens of the United States and persons legally authorized to work in the United States under federal law are qualified to apply for positions as peace officers with general authority Washington law enforcement agencies and limited authority Washington law enforcement agencies, civil service positions such as firefighters, city police, and county sheriffs, and deputy prosecuting attorneys. Currently employed law enforcement officers, firefighters, and prosecuting attorneys must have citizenship or legal authorization to work in the United States.
