What the bill does (WA House Bill Report (Summary of Bill))

The Joint Legislative Audit and Review Committee (JLARC) must conduct a review of safety and stability outcomes across the Department of Social and Health Services (DSHS) developmental disabilities residential services continuum for the time period of January 1, 2023, through December 31, 2026. Residential settings included in the review must include residential habilitation centers, state-operated living alternatives, and contracted community-based residential settings, including group homes, group training homes, residential supported living, companion homes, and other similar settings. For each setting, the review must include the following information, per calendar year, for clients assessed by the DSHS as having a high behavior acuity level, a high medical acuity level, or both, according to the developmental disabilities assessment: the number of calls to 911 due to client behavioral management needs or crisis intervention; the number of emergency room visits where the client was medically cleared for discharge but remained in the hospital due to lack of an available provider acceptance based fully or partially on behavioral or safety concerns; and the number of instances where a client's residential placement was terminated due to the client's behavioral challenges, resulting in a transfer to state custody, incarceration, or homelessness. The review must also include the annual census of high behavioral and medical acuity clients in each setting, and the percentage of the overall client census that these clients represent for each setting. Additionally, the review must compare staff retention rates across each setting. The JLARC must compile its review findings into a report to the Legislature by December 1, 2027. As part of its report, JLARC must recommend a methodology for collecting and analyzing the information included in the review on an ongoing basis, including recommended minimum viable measures, data standardization techniques, and other methods for accurately comparing and portraying data across included settings. The DSHS must produce an ongoing annual report to the Legislature of the data measures included in the JLARC review. The report must also be posted to the DSHS website in order to allow families, guardians, and other members of the public to view aggregate data on emergency service utilization and placement stability for contracted residential settings as compared to state-operated residential settings for DSHS clients with developmental disabilities. In producing the annual reports, the DSHS must collect and organize the data in a manner that is informed by the methodology recommended by JLARC. The first report must be completed by September 1, 2028, and must include updated data starting from January 1, 2027. The report must be updated on an annual basis with updates completed by September 1 of each year.
