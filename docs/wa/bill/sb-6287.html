SB 6287 · Kratom products

What the bill does (WA Senate Bill Report (Summary of Bill))

Product Standards and Labeling. A kratom processor or retailer is prohibited from preparing, distributing, selling, or exposing for sale, a kratom product that is mixed or packed with a nonkratom substance in a manner that affects the product’s quality or strength to a degree that is likely to injure a consumer. A kratom processor or retailer is also prohibited from preparing, distributing, selling, or exposing for sale, a kratom product that contains a poisonous or otherwise harmful nonkratom substance, including a controlled substance. The bill prohibits kratom products that contains more than 2 percent 7-hydroxymitragynine in the alkaloid fraction, and prohibits products containing any synthetic alkaloid, including synthetic mitragynine or synthetic 7-hydroxymitragynine. A kratom product may not be prepared, distributed, sold, or exposed for sale, unless its package or label includes the full list of ingredients, the percentages of mitragynine and 7-hydroxymitragynine, and the identity and address of the kratom processor. Age Restriction. A kratom processor or retailer may not distribute, sell, or expose for sale a kratom product to an individual under 21 years old. Penalties and Enforcement. Any violation of these provisions is a gross misdemeanor. A person aggrieved by a kratom processor or retailer may bring a civil action for damages against the processor, the retailer, or both. A kratom processor or retailer is not liable for a violation related to the required labeling information if the processor or retailer relied in good faith on the representations of the manufacturer, packer, or distributor regarding the product’s ingredients or the percentages of mitragynine and 7-hydroxymitragynine. Local Ordinances. A city or county may adopt ordinances governing kratom products that are more restrictive than those provided in state law. Definitions. The bill includes a June 30, 2027 expiration date and a June 30, 2027 effective date related to amendments to definitions used in the Uniform Controlled Substances Act. EFFECT OF CHANGES MADE BY LABOR & COMMERCE COMMITTEE (First Substitute): Revises the definition of kratom products to include those that contain kratom extract, as defined. Provides that kratom extract may not contain controlled substances or residual solvents above certain standards. Replaces the prohibition on products containing synthetic alkaloids with a prohibition on products containing synthesized kratom material, alkaloids, or other plant constituents that have been isolated or manipulated to increase potency. Prohibits kratom products that are mixed with compounds known to inhibit certain enzymes. Prohibits a kratom processor or retailer from processing certain kratom products, in addition to preparing, distributing, and selling the products.
