SB 5957 · Homeless youth committee

Expands the Homeless Youth Advisory Committee's membership to add people with lived experience of youth homelessness or publicly funded youth care systems.

What the bill does (WA House Bill Report (Summary of Bill))

Brief Summary of Bill Expands the membership of the Homeless Youth Advisory Committee to include: (1) at least two additional individuals over age 25 who experienced homelessness or involvement with a publicly funded system of care as a youth; and (2) at least two individuals who represent populations that are disproportionately homeless or have experience in a publicly funded system of care in the youth and young adult population.
