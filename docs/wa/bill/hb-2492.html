HB 2492 · Apprenticeships/wellness

Requires building-trade apprenticeships to include behavioral health and wellness training starting 2027, and lets electricians and plumbers count up to four such hours toward continuing education.

What the bill does (WA Substitute House Bill Report (Summary of Bill))

Apprenticeship Programs . Beginning July 1, 2027, an apprenticeship program for a building or construction trade occupation approved by the WSATC must provide apprentices with behavioral health and wellness training and must require apprentices to successfully complete the training. The behavioral health and wellness training must include content on destigmatizing behavioral health, supporting wellness, recognizing signs of distress, suicide prevention, substance abuse disorder awareness and prevention, peer support, and connection to resources. The training must include at least two hours of instruction. These hours are in addition to the minimum related/supplemental instruction hours. Continuing Education Requirements for Electricians and Plumbers . An electrician or plumber may count up to four hours from a behavioral health and wellness training toward continuing education requirements, so long as the training is approved by L&I and covers the same subjects as those required for apprentices.
