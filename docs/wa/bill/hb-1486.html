What the bill does (WA Substitute House Bill Report (Summary of Bill))

A student member is added to the SBCTC. The student must be at least 18 years old at the time of appointment, enrolled at a CTC, and must serve for one year. The student member must excuse themself from participating or voting on matters relating to hiring, discipline, personnel, or issues pertaining to pending litigation. The SBCTC is authorized to adopt rules for this purpose.
