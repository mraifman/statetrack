Creates a new offense for intentionally blocking access to or disrupting religious services at places of worship, with civil remedies for those harmed.

What the bill does (WA Engrossed Substitute House Bill Report (Summary of Bill))

(As Amended by Committee) Creates a new offense of intentionally preventing or substantially hindering ingress to or egress from a place of worship or intentionally disrupting the normal functioning of religious services. Provides civil remedies for persons aggrieved by the prohibited actions.
