SB 5965 · Retail bags

Requires retailers to collect rising passthrough charges on plastic carryout bags from 2028, drops the thicker-bag requirement, and preempts local bag ordinances.

What the bill does (WA Senate Bill Report (Summary of Bill))

Beginning in 2028, a retail establishment must collect a passthrough charge of $0.15 for reusable film plastic carryout bags. The passthrough charge for reausable film plastic carryout bags increases to $0.18 in 2028. Restaurants are not required to collect the passthrough charge for carryout bags provided for off-site consumption. A retail establishment that uses a third-party platform must list the applicable passthrough charges associated with each customer order. The requirement that reusable film plastic carryout bags increase to 4 mils in thickness beginning in 2028 is eliminated. Retail establishments are prohibited from collecting passthrough charges and the establishments customary fee for paper carryout bags for any customer using WIC, cash, or food assistance program benefits. Violations of the chapter are civil penalties of up to $250 for a retail establishment. Any carryout bag ordinance by a local government enacted as of April 1, 2020 is preempted by this chapter. EFFECT OF CHANGES MADE BY WAYS & MEANS COMMITTEE (Second Substitute): Retains current law allowing the sale of reusable plastic film bags and specifying postconsumer recycling requirements. Increases the passtrhough charge for reusable plastic film bags to $0.15 in 2028 and $0.18 in 2030. Strikes the increase in thickness of reusable plastic bags beginning in 2028. EFFECT OF CHANGES MADE BY ENVIRONMENT, ENERGY & TECHNOLOGY COMMITTEE (First Substitute): Maintains the passthrough charge for paper carryout bags at $0.08. Specifies that carryout bags provided by restaurants for off-site consumption are exempt. Eliminates new civil penalties for manufacturers and distributors. Allows a compostable film bag to be made from potato starch. Makes technical changes to the definition of carryout bags.
