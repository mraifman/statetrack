HB 1080 · Lodging fee disclosure

Requires hotels and short-term rentals to include all fees and taxes in advertised prices, with civil penalties for violations, capped at $1,000 for small owners.

What the bill does (WA Substitute Senate Bill Report (Summary of Bill))

Disclosure Requirements. Hotels and short-term rentals must disclose all fees and charges in the advertised, displayed, or offered rate, except for taxes and fees imposed by governments and assessment fees. The fee and charges disclosure is applicable to advertising, displays, or offers that are public in this state or from this state before the public in any state. Prior to a stay at a hotel or a short-term rental, all taxes and assessment fees must be included in the price to be paid. Civil Penalties. Hotels and short-term rentals that do not comply with the fee and charges disclosures are subject to a civil penalty not exceeding $10,000 for each violation. The maximum civil penalty for a person that owns three or fewer short term rentals is not to exceed $1,000. City attorneys, county prosecuting attorneys, or the Office of the Attorney General are permitted to enforce these requirements. EFFECT OF BUSINESS, FINANCIAL SERVICES & TRADE COMMITTEE AMENDMENT(S): The maximum penalty for a civil violation by a person that owns three or fewer short term rentals is reduced from $10,000 to $1,000
