SB 6011 · Court of appeals bailiffs

Authorizes Court of Appeals bailiffs to assess threats and receive criminal history, including non-conviction data, solely for threat investigations, with limits on other uses.

What the bill does (WA Senate Bill Report (Summary of Bill))

Bailiffs of the Court of Appeals are authorized to conduct threat assessments on behalf of Court of Appeals judicial officers and staff members, and to receive criminal history record information that includes non-conviction data for purposes exclusively related to the investigation of any person making a threat against a Court of Appeals judicial officer or staff member. The dissemination or use of criminal history records or non-conviction data for purposes other than authorized by law is prohibited. Any threats investigated under this section and found to be credible must be referred to local law enforcement for further action. The Court of Appeals must ensure that the bailiffs are qualified by training and experience.
