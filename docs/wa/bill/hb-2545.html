What the bill does (WA House Bill Report (Summary of Bill))

The DOH is required to adopt rules establishing criteria for the issuance of a certificate of need for the performance of elective percutaneous coronary interventions at ambulatory surgical facilities. The DOH must adopt these rules by July 1, 2027.
