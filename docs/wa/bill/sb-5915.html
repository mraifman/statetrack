SB 5915 · Health technology assessment

Changes Washington's health technology assessment priorities and evidence standards, including accepting expert input in place of randomized trials for rare or life-threatening diseases.

What the bill does (WA House Bill Report (Summary of Bill))

In selecting health technologies to be reviewed under the Health Technology Assessment Program, the Health Care Authority (Authority) must give priority to an additional category of health technologies. The new priority category relates to health technologies that have patient access established or recommended for a patient population under Medicare or in nationally recognized expert treatment guidelines, including guidelines adopted by specialty physician organizations or patient advocacy organizations. When the health technology pertains to a life-threatening or rare disease, the Health Technology Clinical Committee (Committee) must: evaluate all applicable clinical trials regarding the technology, as published in peer-reviewed clinical literature, including randomized control trials; and consider information submitted by clinical experts indicating that a randomized control trial or other specific trial design would be unethical, impractical, or impossible for the technology within a specific patient population. Re-reviews of health technologies are not limited to evidence made available since the previous determination. Re-reviews must consider new evidence in combination with, and within the context of, the clinical evidence the Committee had previously considered. The Authority must publish on its website the receipt of submissions for new technology assessments and re-review assessments with 30 days of receipt. The Committee must review the submissions and communicate its decision to review or re-review within 180 days. If the Committee issues an adverse determination, it must provide the submitting party with a written, substantive explanation of the rationale for the adverse determination within 180 days from the date of submission.
