HB 1589 · Health carriers & providers

Requires health carriers to give providers advance notice and contract information before contract changes, including 90 days' notice of significant payment modifications, starting in 2027.

What the bill does (WA Engrossed Second Substitute House Bill Report (Summary of Bill))

Contract negotiations and renewals . For health plans issued or renewed on or after January 1, 2027, prior to entering into or renewing a contract with a health care provider, a health carrier must provide the following: the contact information for the carrier's primary contact for contract negotiations; a copy of the new contract with all changes clearly indicated and a clean copy of the revised contract that incorporates amendments into the body of the contract and into any relevant exhibit or addendum; and the carrier's payment methodology no less than 60 days in advance of the execution of the contract. For new contracts, a health carrier must provide contract and payment policy updates in a manner that does not require access to a secure website or portal. These requirements do not apply to negotiations between a health carrier and a provider who is: an employee of the health carrier or affiliate; employed by a hospital or any affiliate of a hospital or health system; or employed by an entity that owns or operates multistate provider clinics. Significant Payer Contract Modification . For health plans, including health plans offered to public and school employees, issued or renewed on or after January 1, 2027, health carriers must provide notice of significant payer contract modifications (modifications) to the relevant health care provider or facility contract representative 90 days before the modification's effective date. The notice must: specify the provider contract to which the modification applies and be conspicuously labeled as a modification; include the modification language and contain sufficient information about the change to allow the health care provider or facility to assess the financial impact, if any, including a detailed description of any change in methodology, payment, or administrative procedure; impacted current procedural terminology (CPT) codes, health care common procedure coding system codes, modifiers, or any other data elements; and the scope of services and service locations that the significant payer contract modification applies to; and be sent to the person designated to receive notice under the provider contract. Carriers must include a summary of all modification notices in a single location on the carrier's website and in the carrier's newsletters 90 days before the modification's effective date. "Significant payer contract modification" means an action by a carrier that results in any of the following changes for a health care provider or facility that has a provider contract with the carrier: a decrease in fees, payments, or reimbursement to a participating provider or facility; a change in the payment methodology for determining fees, payments, or reimbursement to a participating provider; a restriction on the site of service for specific services or changes to payment or coverage based on site of service; a new requirement that services be separate or bundled; a change to prior authorization, inpatient notification, or claims submission requirements or processes; a restriction or exclusion from the contracted components of service or a requirement that the contracted component be provided by or purchased from another entity; or a change of procedures that may reasonably be expected to significantly increase a health care provider's administrative expenses.
