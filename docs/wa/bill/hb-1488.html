HB 1488 · Conservation district limits

Caps the annual per-parcel rate for conservation district RACs systems at $25 and adjusts it by CPI every three years starting in 2029.

What the bill does (WA Substitute House Bill Report (Summary of Bill))

The annual maximum per-parcel rate for RACs systems in CDs is $25. Beginning March 1, 2029, the Department of Revenue must adjust the per-parcel rate every three years based on the consumer price index.
