HB 1941 · Agricultural coops/cannabis

Lets licensed cannabis producers form agricultural associations or cooperatives and treats cannabis as an agricultural product for that purpose, but caps each group at three producer licenses.

What the bill does (WA Engrossed House Bill Report (Summary of Bill))

Licensed cannabis producers may form agricultural associations, corporate or otherwise, to engage in certain business activities including collectively processing, handling, or marketing their products. For the purposes of licensed cannabis producers forming agricultural associations only, cannabis is considered an agricultural product. No single agricultural association or cooperative composed of licensed cannabis producers may represent more than three producer licenses issued by by the Liquor and Cannabis Board.
