HB 1795 · Student restraint, isolation

Bans mechanical, chemical and breathing-restricting restraints on students, limits planned restraint and isolation, and bars new isolation rooms in schools.

What the bill does (WA Engrossed Substitute House Bill Report (Summary of Bill))

Brief Summary of Engrossed Substitute Bill Prohibits staff use of the following interventions on students: mechanical or chemical restraint and restraint that restricts breathing or blood flow to the brain. Specifies that isolation may not be used as a planned behavioral intervention, and that restraint may be planned only under specified circumstances. Prohibits construction, repurposing, or otherwise establishing any new room or other enclosed area for the primary purpose of student isolation. Applies student restraint and isolation requirements to all providers of public educational services, except inpatient facility licensed or certified health professionals.
