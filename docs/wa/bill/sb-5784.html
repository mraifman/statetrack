SB 5784 · Agency demographic data

Requires state agencies to collect voluntary demographic data under OMB SPD 15 standards by 2030, with public reporting and OFM exemptions and variances.

What the bill does (WA Senate Bill Report (Summary of Bill))

By June 30, 2026 at the latest, agencies shall collect voluntary self-identification demographic data using the United States Office of Management and Budget SPD 15 as published on March 29, 2024. Each agency must consider and base data collection on the appropriate demographic data set necessary with regard to appropriate target demographic categories or subcategories of a program or agency being evaluated. This data must be included in every demographic report on race or ethnic origin published or released after June 30, 2025. Reports are required to state the reasoning and process used to determine the appropriate dataset. If the appropriate data cannot be collected, the report should state the reasons for unavailability. This data must be made available to the public, in accordance with law, except for personal information. Agencies may consult the state ethnic commissions or the Governor's Office of Indian Affairs for advice. Agencies may submit a request to the Office of Financial Management (OFM) for an exemption to the requirement to collect more detailed data beyond the minimum categories if the agency determines the potential benefit of the detailed data would not justify the additional burden to the agency and the public, or the additional risk to privacy or confidentiality. Agencies may submit a request to OFM for a variance to the detailed categories if the agency determines that collecting different detailed categories than the ones listed in SPD 15 provides more useful or accurate data for the collection's specific context and intended uses. Any variances in detailed data must be able to be aggregated up to the minimum required categories. OFM and agencies must consult with the Washington State ethnic commissions and the Governor's Office of Indian Affairs regarding which demographic breakdown is most important and relevant to the program being analyzed. The superintendent of public instruction must collect all student-level data using SPD 15 as soon as practicable. EFFECT OF CHANGES MADE BY STATE GOVERNMENT, TRIBAL AFFAIRS & ELECTIONS COMMITTEE (First Substitute): Clarifies that the last four digits of a social security number is considered personal information. Clarifies that the minimum categories and subcategories are those used by the US Census Bureau for Washington state population estimates. Permits agencies to collect data using other, more granular demographic data as deemed appropriate by the agency. Details specific state and federal law that must be followed when making data available to the public. Requires agencies to comply with the requirements of the bill by June 30, 2030. Requires OFM to convene a multiagency committee, which shall be responsible for reviewing requests for exceptions or variances to the requirements of the bill. Sets forth qualification factors for exemptions and variances.
