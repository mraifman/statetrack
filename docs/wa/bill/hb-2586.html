What the bill does (WA House Bill Report (Summary of Bill))

Beginning in the 2026-27 academic year, students eligible for Passport to Careers are automatically income-eligible for the Washington College Grant. The Passport to Careers formula for determining financial need is aligned with the federal formula by replacing total family contribution with total student aid index, redefining financial need as the difference between a student's cost of attendance and the student's total student aid index, as determined by the United States Department of Education. The Office must deposit all money received for Passport to Careers into the State Financial Aid Account.
