HB 1438 · Housing permit approval

Sets criteria for residential projects in urban growth areas to gain permit approval unless local governments identify serious health or safety concerns in writing.

What the bill does (WA House Bill Analysis 2025 (Summary of Bill))

The project is located within an urban growth area. The project contains at least one residential unit. The application included plans, computations, or specifications that were prepared, stamped, and signed by a professional engineer or architect who is licensed in Washington. The local government did not identify, in writing, serious public health or safety concerns that prevent approval of the project.
