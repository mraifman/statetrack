SB 5463 · Industrial insurance/duties

Extends the good-faith duty and certification-withdrawal penalty for repeat violations to all workers' compensation self-insurers and third-party administrators.

What the bill does (WA Senate Bill Report (Summary of Bill))

The duty of good faith and fair dealing applies to all workers' compensation self-insurers and third-party administrators, rather than only self-insured municipal employers and self-insured private sector firefighter employers and their third-party administrators. L&I's authority to withdraw a self-insurer's certification when an employer has been found to have violated the self-insurer's duty of good faith and fair dealing three times within a three-year period applies to all self-insurers.
