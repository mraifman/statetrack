What the bill does (WA Substitute House Bill Report (Summary of Bill))

(As Amended by House) Allows school districts in binding conditions or under enhanced financial oversight to take a temporary, interest free interfund loan from its capital projects fund, subject to certain conditions. Allows boards of directors of school districts in binding conditions to request from the Superintendent of Public Instruction (SPI) authorization to sell real property of the district for purposes of maintaining financial solvency. Modifies provisions governing the sale of school district real property when directed by a financial oversight committee convened by the SPI.
