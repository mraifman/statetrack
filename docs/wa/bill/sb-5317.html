What the bill does (WA House Bill Report (Summary of Bill))

(As Amended by House) Specifies that actions taken by a city or county under certain agreements with the Energy Facility Site Evaluation Council (EFSEC) are not subject to appeal on the basis that the action is inconsistent with a code that has been preempted by the EFSEC's statutes.
