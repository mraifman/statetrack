SB 6058 · Wage enforcement discretion

Lets L&I choose which wage complaints to investigate, sets a 60-day decision deadline, and starts the civil statute of limitations when a complaint is filed.

What the bill does (WA Engrossed Senate Bill Report (Summary of Bill))

Wage Complaint Investigations. The bill authorizes L&I to investigate a wage complaint filed by an employee, rather than requiring an investigation in all cases. When L&I accepts a complaint, it must issue either a citation and notice of assessment or a determination of compliance within 60 days of acceptance, unless an extension is allowed for good cause. L&I must establish a written process that prioritizes wage complaints based on certain factors including the severity of the complaint and the probability of retaliation. L&I's enforcement priorities must be made publicly available. Statute of Limitations. The toll on the statute of limitations for civil actions for an employee’s wage complaint begins once the complaint is filed with L&I.
