What the bill does (WA Engrossed Substitute Senate Bill Report (Summary of Bill))

Appropriations are provided for certain state transportation agencies and programs for the 2025-27 fiscal biennium and are adjusted for the 2023-2025 fiscal biennium. Budget summary materials can be found in the SCS tab in the committee materials for the public hearing.
