SB 5221 · Personal property distraint

Lets county treasurers cancel small personal property taxes when collection costs exceed the tax, and requires owners to request excess sale proceeds, which expire after three years.

What the bill does (WA Substitute House Bill Report (Summary of Bill))

County treasurers are authorized to cancel personal property taxes, including taxes for a titled manufactured or mobile home, if the cost for the county to collect and distrain is more than the tax amount due. The requirement that the county treasurer automatically pays the owner of the personal property any excess money from the distraint sale is removed. In the case of excess money from any personal property or improvements on real property, the owner must request a refund. The owner of the property is the person who held the title on the date of issuance of the notice of distraint. Assignments of interests, deeds, or other documents executed or recorded after filing the notice of distraint do not affect the payment of excess funds to the title owner of record. If the owner does not request a refund within three years of the sale, all claims by any owner to the excess funds are extinguished, and the treasurer must deposit the excess funds in the current expense fund of the county. A person may have a new certificate of title created by the DOL if the mobile home, manufactured home, or park model is purchased in a county treasurer's foreclosure or distraint sale for nonpayment of taxes where no title can be found.
