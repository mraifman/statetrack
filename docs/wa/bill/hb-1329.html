HB 1329 · Wholesale power purchases

Exempts short-term and BPA wholesale power purchases from CETA's coal-resource definition and requires consumer-owned utilities to report certain unspecified-electricity contracts.

What the bill does (WA Engrossed House Bill Report (Summary of Bill))

Washington Clean Energy Transformation Act Standards . The definition of a "coal-fired resource" does not include unspecified electricity that is included as part of a limited duration wholesale power purchase contract for a term of up to three months, or for a term of up to six months if the purchase is used to demonstrate compliance with the electric utility's seasonal resource adequacy requirements under a regional resource adequacy program. Such limited duration wholesale power purchases may not be used for the purpose of avoiding the restrictions on coal-fired resources. Electricity purchased from the Bonneville Power Administration (BPA) under a long-term power purchase agreement or exchange agreement, including any portion of the federal system supplied by unspecified electricity, is exempt from the compliance requirement that each electric utility eliminate coal-fired resources from its allocation of electricity, except for any portion of the federal system supplied by a transaction for electricity where the BPA knows the source of electricity is from a coal-fired generating unit. Reporting Requirements . Each interim performance or compliance report submitted by consumer-owned utilities must include the number of unspecified electricity contracts that are used to serve Washington retail customers with terms greater than 31 days, as well as the duration and the purpose of such contracts.
