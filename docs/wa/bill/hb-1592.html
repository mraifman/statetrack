What the bill does (WA House Bill Report (Summary of Bill))

Reallocation of Funding Responsibility for Public Defense Services . Beginning in fiscal year (FY) 2026, funding responsibility for public defense services is reallocated between the state and local governments based on an average of the actual expenditures for public defense services paid for by eligible cities and counties for the five years looking back from FY 2024 (five-year average).
