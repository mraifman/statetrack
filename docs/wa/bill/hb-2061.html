What the bill does (WA Engrossed Substitute House Bill Report (Summary of Bill))

The state is permitted to impose concession fees on duty-free sales enterprises operating within the state. The concession fee levied upon every duty-free sales enterprise operating within the state and collected shall be equal to 10 percent of the gross proceeds of sales of merchandise by duty-free enterprises. This fee is separate and in addition to business and occupation taxes and all other taxes and fees applicable to duty-free sales enterprises operating in the state, including concession fees paid to a port authority or other governmental authority. The Department of Revenue (DOR) must establish procedures and requirements for the payment of concession fees by duty-free sales enterprises, and for the collection of such concession fees. A portion of the moneys collected by the DOR may be retained to cover implementation and administration costs related to the provisions of the bill. All remaining funds collected are directed to be split equally between the Statewide Tourism Marking Account and the newly created Sustainable Aviation Fuel Account. Airports and other air navigation facilities controlled and operated by any municipality, or jointly controlled and operated pursuant to RCW 14.08, are now made subject to concession fees on duty-free sale enterprises, where applicable.
