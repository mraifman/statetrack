HB 1390 · Community protection program

Repeals statute references to DSHS's Community Protection Program effective 2027 and requires a plan to move participants into other services.

What the bill does (WA Substitute House Bill Report (Summary of Bill))

References to the Community Protection Program (CPP) are removed from statute effective January 1, 2027. The legislative intent of the bill is expressed as ending the CPP to allow clients of the Home and Community Living Administration of the Department of Social and Health Services (DSHS) to obtain less restrictive service options and is not intended to reduce the supportive services that those clients receive. The DSHS must develop and implement a plan to transition all individuals participating in the CPP into other services or programs by December 31, 2026.
