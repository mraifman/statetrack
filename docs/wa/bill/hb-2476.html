HB 2476 · Theater licenses/alcohol

Creates a spirits, beer, and wine license for small theaters, conditioned on alcohol control plans, ID scanners, and staff training.

What the bill does (WA Engrossed Substitute House Bill Report (Summary of Bill))

A theater may hold a spirits, beer, and wine theater license if the theater has no more than 200 seats per screen. Theaters holding a spirits, beer, and wine theater license must have alcohol control plan measures that include: regular monitoring by staff who have completed an alcohol server training program; electronic identification scanners used at all points of sale for alcoholic beverage purchases; and identification checks for all alcoholic beverage purchases regardless of the age of the consumer.
