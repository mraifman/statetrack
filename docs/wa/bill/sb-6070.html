What the bill does (WA Engrossed Second Substitute House Bill Report (Summary of Bill))

Pen Registers, Trap and Trace Devices, and Cell Site Simulator Devices . The circumstances where a law enforcement officer may be authorized by court order to install and use a pen register, trap and trace device, or cell site simulator device are expanded to include when the device is necessary to fulfill community caretaking functions, subject to certain conditions. The court must authorize the installation and use of the device if the court finds that the information likely to be obtained is necessary for law enforcement to fulfill community caretaking functions and there is probable cause to believe that the device may aid in locating a missing endangered person. The court may consider the following nonexaustive list of factors when determining whether there is probable cause for a law enforcement officer to perform community caretaking functions to help locate a missing endangered person: the person has a recent history of suicidal ideation; the person has an apparent lack of access to prescribed medications; the person left behind personal items that one would expect the person to take if the disappearance was voluntary; the disappearance involves an unusual and unexplained absence from work, school, or other regularly scheduled activity; the person is a minor who lacks the skills for long-term self-care and safety; there is evidence to suggest the person is suffering a significant mental health crisis leaving them unable to manage the person's care and safety; the person was the primary caregiver for another person or animal and made no effort to arrange for a continuation of care; the location and conditions in the area where the person is suspected to be located, combined with their available resources, are not consistent with long-term survival; the person has a developmental disability; the person is a vulnerable adult; the person is a minor for whom law enforcement can identify multiple risk factors that are indicative that the minor is a current victim of human trafficking or commercial sexual exploitation; or the person is believed to have Alzheimer's disease or other age-related dementia that inhibits the person's ability to manage the person's care and safety. The existing gross misdemeanor for a law enforcement officer's knowing and unauthorized installation or use of a pen register, trap and trace device, or cell site simulator device is limited to circumstances where the device was installed or used in an ongoing criminal investigation. A law enforcement agency must take all steps necessary to permanently delete any information or metadata collected from the unauthorized use of a cell site simulator device, and may not transmit, use, or retain such information or metadata for any purpose whatsoever, if: the agency has not obtained a court order approving the use of the device within 48 hours; or the application for the court order is denied. Search Warrants . The statutory provision authorizing magistrates to issue search warrants is expanded. Any magistrate with power to issue a warrant for the arrest of a person charged with a crime may, when satisfied that there is probable cause and upon application supported by oath or affirmation, issue a search warrant to search for and seize any evidence that may aid in locating a missing endangered person.
