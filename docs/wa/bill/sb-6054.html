Bars common interest community governing documents from banning fire-hardened building materials, while allowing reasonable rules on their design, placement, and appearance.

What the bill does (WA Substitute House Bill Report (Summary of Bill))

(As Amended by House) Prohibits common interest community (CIC) governing documents from prohibiting the installation, use, or maintenance of fire-hardened building materials that meet certain health and safety standards. Authorizes CIC governing documents to include reasonable rules regarding the design, dimensions, placement, or exterior appearance of fire-hardened building materials if the rules meet certain criteria.
