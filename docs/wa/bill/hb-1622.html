What the bill does (WA Engrossed Substitute House Bill Report (Summary of Bill))

Public employers covered by the Personnel System Reform Act (PSRA) or the Public Employees' Collective Bargaining Act must bargain with covered employees over the decision to adopt or modify the use of artificial intelligence unless it is implemented or modified by a third party. A third party is an individual or entity that provides services to the employer but does not have an employment or coemployment relationship with the employer. Some services a third party might provide include technology services, digital services, equipment, or software. Artificial intelligence means an engineered or machine-based system that can, for a given set of objectives, generate outputs such as predictions, recommendations, or decisions influencing real or virtual environments. The is act null and void if specific funding is not provided for it in the omnibus appropriations act by June 30, 2026. Second Substitute Bill Compared to Engrossed Substitute Bill: The definition of "artificial intelligence" is changed and the definition for "machine learning" is removed. The second substitute bill also permits an employer to implement or modify artificial intelligence technology without bargaining if it is part of an update made by a third party to technology that employees are already using, regardless of whether it impacts wages or performance evaluations. The date on the null and void clause is moved forward by one year to June 30, 2026.
