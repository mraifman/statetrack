HB 2668 · Bob Oke game farm relocation

Directs WDFW to plan relocation of the Bob Oke Game Farm away from groundwater contamination risk and to pursue capital funding for relocation and site remediation.

What the bill does (WA House Bill Report (Summary of Bill))

Relocation Plan . The Department of Fish and Wildlife (WDFW) must identify one or more alternate locations suitable for the relocation of the WDFW-owned and operated pheasant rearing and release facility located in Lewis County commonly known as the Bob Oke Game Farm (Game Farm) that do not pose a material risk of contaminating municipal or residential drinking water supplies, first determining if property the WDFW currently owns or manages is suitable for relocation. The WDFW must submit the list of properties to the committees of the House and Senate with jurisdiction over the capital budget and the legislators representing the legislative district in which the Game Farm is located. The WDFW must request funding in the 2027-29 Capital Budget for predesign to: evaluate each candidate site's proximity to certain water sources and the potential operational impacts on water supplies; consider access, infrastructure needs, land availability, zoning and land use compatibility, and the ability to maintain wildlife management and release functions; consult with affected local governments, water utilities, tribes, and conservation districts; and estimate costs, timelines, and logistical requirements for relocation. At the conclusion of the predesign, the WDFW must submit a relocation report to the Governor and appropriate committees of the Legislature that includes a recommendation for a preferred site and certain other information related to a schedule and cost estimates for relocation. Once a preferred location has been identified, the WDFW must submit a request for funding needed to relocate the Game Farm the in 2029-31 Capital Budget, and must continue to seek funding until the Game Farm has been relocated. Remediation Plan . The WDFW must request funding in the 2027-29 Capital Budget to complete design work to remediate the current Game Farm site. The remediation plan must address continued groundwater and soil monitoring, prevention of further nitrate or nutrient leaching to groundwater, evaluation of restoration techniques to reduce existing nutrient concentrations. Once the design work is complete, the WDFW must submit a request for funding in the 2029-31 Capital Budget to fully remediate the current site, and must continue to seek funding until the site is fully remediated.
