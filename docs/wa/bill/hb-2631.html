What the bill does (WA House Bill Analysis 2026 (Summary of Bill))

The Office of the Secretary of State (OSOS), the Department of Fish and Wildlife (WDFW), and the Office of the Insurance Commissioner (OIC) must implement a pilot project with Your Washington. These agencies must, in collaboration with Your Washington, develop and track metrics focused on the customer service experience and identify opportunities for improvement. These agencies must work in partnership with Washington Technology Solutions (WaTech) and Your Washington to ensure that customer service delivery channels meet enterprise accessibility, usability, and customer experience best practices and prioritize digital-first services. As soon as practicable, these agencies must begin partnering with WaTech to move towards a "one front door" vision of state digital services accessible in a single place. The OSOS, the WDFW, and the OIC websites and digital applications should prominently display a standardized navigational component that provides a clear pathway to WA.gov, and the agencies must collaborate with WaTech to develop a schedule for integrating their digital applications with WA.gov. By July 10, 2026, the OSOS, the WDFW, and the OIC must designate a Your Washington liaison. The liaison is granted decision-making authority and accountability for: partnering with Your Washington to ensure enterprise-wide coordination; aligning agency service improvements with enterprise customer experience and accessibility priorities; submitting quarterly customer experience performance data to Your Washington; and ensuring customer feedback is incorporated into service improvements and digital accessibility enhancements. By September 8, 2026, these agencies must provide Your Washington with a timeline of each step of the customer experience for the key services provided by the agency and a documented process and workflow map. Reporting Requirements. By October 8, 2026, the OSOS, the WDFW, and the OIC must provide Your Washington with a report detailing: customer experience metrics, including data related to timelines, communications, and outcomes for delivering public services; information about customer feedback and complaints and a summary of the most common customer complaints; a description of the agency's delivery of digital and in-person services, and identification of areas for improvement; and policies to strengthen plain language writing and design principles, including the extent to which the agency's policies or practices align with Executive Order 23-02. Beginning April 1, 2026, the agencies must notify Your Washington about any pilot projects expected to improve customer experience, including expected outcomes and actual results. Customer Experience Improvement Plans. By December 7, 2026, the OSOS, the WDFW, and the OIC must develop and begin implementing Customer Experience Improvement Plans, following a format mutually agreed upon with Your Washington. The Customer Experience Improvement Plans must include: metrics for measuring customer experience and customer satisfaction, and timelines for regularly tracking and reporting on these; staff training plans for customer service and the use of plain language; and a proposal, including a timeline for implementation, for improving the customer experience for key services provided by the agency.
