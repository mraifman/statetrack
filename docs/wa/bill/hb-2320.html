HB 2320 · Firearm manufacturing

Bans 3D-printed or CNC-made manufacture of machine guns, undetectable and untraceable firearms, and frames or receivers, and restricts sharing digital gun-making code.

What the bill does (WA Engrossed Substitute House Bill Report (Summary of Bill))

Restrictions on manufacturing various firearms and related components are modified to expressly reference the use of three-dimensional (3D) printers, computer numerical control (CNC) milling machines, and digital firearm manufacturing code. Restrictions are established regarding: (1) the sale, transfer, and distribution of digital firearm manufacturing code; and (2) manufacturing frames and receivers and unfinished frames and receivers through the use of a 3D printer, CNC milling machine, or by other means, subject to various exceptions. Restrictions on Manufacturing Undetectable Firearms, Other Firearms, and Components . No person may manufacture, including by use of a 3D printer, CNC milling machine, or other means, any machine gun, bump-fire stock, undetectable firearm, short-barreled shotgun, or short-barreled rifle; or any part designed and intended solely and exclusively for use in the same, or in converting a weapon into a machine gun, short-barreled shotgun, or short-barreled rifle; subject to various exceptions. No person may manufacture, including by use of a 3D printer, CNC milling machine, or other means, an untraceable firearm with intent to sell the untraceable firearm. A violation of these restrictions is a class C felony. Restrictions on Manufacturing Untraceable Firearms . No person may manufacture, cause to be manufactured, assemble, or cause to be assembled an untraceable firearm, including by use of a 3D printer, CNC milling machine, or other means. A violation of this restriction is a civil infraction. A second violation is a misdemeanor. A third or subsequent violation is a gross misdemeanor. No person may knowingly or recklessly allow, facilitate, aid, or abet the manufacture or assembly of an undetectable firearm or untraceable firearm, including by distribution of digital firearm manufacturing code, by certain prohibited persons. A violation of this restriction is a gross misdemeanor. Restrictions on Manufacturing a Frame or Receiver or Unfinished Frame or Receiver . No person may knowingly or recklessly manufacture a frame or receiver or an unfinished frame or receiver by use of a 3D printer, CNC milling machine, or by other means, unless that person is a federal firearms manufacturer or federal firearms dealer for the sole purpose of repairing existing firearms. A violation of this restriction is a civil infraction. A second violation is a misdemeanor. A third or subsequent violation is a gross misdemeanor. A violation involving three or more frames or receivers or unfinished frames or receivers is a gross misdemeanor. A person commits a separate violation for each frame or receiver, or unfinished frame or receiver. Restrictions on Digital Firearm Manufacturing Code . No person may sell, transfer, distribute, or offer to sell digital firearm manufacturing code for a firearm or unfinished frame or receiver to a person who is not licensed to manufacture firearms under federal law. No person may possess digital firearm manufacturing code for a firearm or unfinished frame or receiver with an intent to distribute the code to a person who is not a federal firearms dealer for the sole purpose of repairing existing firearms, or with an intent to manufacture a firearm or unfinished frame or receiver using a 3D printer or CNC milling machine.
