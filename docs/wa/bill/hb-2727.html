What the bill does (WA House Bill Report (Summary of Bill))

The WSDOT is required to establish an Educational Transit Access Grant Program, to provide funds to public transit agencies for pilot programs with local community or technical colleges. The pilot programs must provide free or reduced transit fares for, at minimum, students enrolled in a degree- or certificate-seeking program at these institutions. To be eligible to receive a grant, the transit agency must submit a written proposal to the WSDOT that provides, at minimum: the pilot program's goals and objectives, with measurable metrics; a description of how the pilot program will lower the cost of public transit for students; a written commitment to participate in the pilot program from the partnering community or technical college; and a preliminary plan, in collaboration with the partnering community or technical college, detailing how the program will be sustained, once the pilot program is complete. The WSDOT is required to conduct a call for proposals no later than 60 days after the effective date of the bill. The WSDOT must also submit a report, in partnership with the relevant transit agency, to the Transportation Committees of the Legislature, within six months of the completion of a pilot project funded under this section, detailing the outcomes of the particular pilot project, including methods by which future pilot projects could be improved.
