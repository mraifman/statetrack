HB 2428 · Life insurance policy lapses

Requires insurers to send written lapse-for-nonpayment notices to policyholders and designated third parties, with proof of delivery, for individual life policies issued after the effective date.

What the bill does (WA Substitute House Bill Report (Summary of Bill))

Life Insurance Policy—Written Notice of Possible Termination for Nonpayment of Premium . Subject to three exclusions, at least 30 days before the day on which coverage under an individual life insurance policy will lapse based on nonpayment of the premium, the insurer must send a written notice of possible termination of coverage to the policyholder at the policyholder's last known address and a third party designated on the account as authorized under the bill. An insurer must obtain and, upon request, demonstrate proof of delivery for each notice the insurer sends under this requirement. Proof of delivery may include records sufficient to show that notices are sent first-class mail using an intelligent mail barcode, or a certified mail receipt, read-receipt for electronic delivery, or tracking confirmation from a shipping service. The requirement to send the written notice does not apply to: (1) group life insurance policies; (2) life insurance policies for which payment of premium is due monthly or more frequently; or (3) term life insurance policies that have a term of one year or less. Life Insurance Policy—Applicant Designation of Third Party to Receive Notice . Subject to the same three exclusions, an insurer that delivers or issues for delivery an individual life insurance policy in Washington must notify the applicant for the policy, in writing at the time of application for the policy, of an applicant's right to designate a third party to receive notice of lapse or termination of the policy based on nonpayment of the premium. An applicant may make a designation at the time of application for the policy, or at any time the policy is in force, by submitting a written notice to the insurer containing the name and address of the third-party designee. The designation of a third party does not create or constitute acceptance of any duty or liability on the part of the third party related to: (1) informing or not informing the policyholder or any other person about a notice received by the third party; (2) making a payment of any premium on behalf of the policyholder; (3) compensating the policyholder, beneficiary, or any other person in relation to a lapse or cancellation of a life insurance policy; or (4) taking any lawful action upon receiving a notice. The designation of a third party also does not create or constitute acceptance of any liability on the part of the insurer as to the third party. Prospective Application of Bill . The bill applies prospectively only and not retroactively. It applies to life insurance policies issued on or after the effective date of the bill.
