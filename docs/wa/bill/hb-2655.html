What the bill does (WA House Bill Analysis 2026 (Summary of Bill))

An exemption from sales and use tax is established for sales of eligible server equipment to be installed, without intervening use, in eligible computer data centers, and to charges for labor and services to install such equipment, subject to certain requirements. This exemption also applies to sales of eligible power infrastructure, and related labor and services to construct, install, repair, alter, or improve such infrastructure at an eligible data center, subject to certain requirements. This exemption expires July 1, 2048. Eligible Computer Data Centers . "Eligible computer data center" means a computer data center: (1) located in a county east of the Cascades that borders another state and has a population of at least 500,000 persons; (2) having at least 20,000 square feet dedicated to housing working servers; and (3) for which the commencement of construction occurs after June 30, 2026, and before July 1, 2035. "Computer data center" means a facility comprised of one or more buildings, which may be comprised of multiple businesses constructed specifically and used primarily to house working servers, where the facility has the following characteristics: (1) uninterruptible power supplies, generator backup power, or both; (2) sophisticated fire suppression and prevention systems; and (3) enhanced physical security, such as: access to the facility restricted to selected personnel; permanent security guards; video camera surveillance; an electronic system requiring passcodes, keycards, or biometric scans, such as hand scans and retinal or fingerprint recognition; or similar security features. A facility comprised of one building or more than one building must have a combined square footage of at least 100,000 square feet. Exemption Certificates . This exemption applies to eligible data centers for which an exemption certificate has been issued by the Department of Revenue (DOR). Exemption certificates expire two years after the date of issuance, unless construction has been commenced. No new exemption certificates may be issued on or after July 1, 2036. Construction Requirement . To qualify for the sales tax exemption under this act, a qualifying business or tenant must certify to the DOR that, for new construction work to be performed on the site of the computer data center, the computer data center receiving an exemption will be constructed by the prime contractor and its subcontractors in a way that includes community workforce agreements or project labor agreements and the payment of area standard prevailing wages and apprenticeship utilization requirements, subject to certain conditions. Net Employment Requirement . Within six years of issuance of an exemption certificate, the qualifying business or tenant must establish that net employment assigned to an eligible computer data center has increased by a minimum of 35 family wage employment positions or, if lower, 3 family wage employment positions for each 20,000 square feet of space or less that is newly dedicated to housing working servers at the eligible computer data center. The minimum number of family wage employment positions must be maintained while the exemption certificate is valid. "Family wage employment positions" must meet the following standards.
