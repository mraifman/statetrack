What the bill does (WA Senate Bill Report (Summary of Bill))

Categorical Exemption for Growth Management Act Activities. Beginning July 1, 2029, a local government must have adopted an ordinance protecting archaeological and cultural resources that was developed in consultation with affected federally recognized Indian tribes as a condition for using the categorical exemption from SEPA for planning activities under the growth management act (GMA). The adopted ordinance must be consistent with the model ordinance developed by the Department of Archaeology and Historic Preservation (DAHP). Local Ordinance . DAHP must develop minimum standards for preservation in consultation with federally recognized Indian tribes. The minimum standards must include an information and data sharing agreement between the local government and DAHP as well as a written consultation agreement between the local government and affected federally recognized Indian tribes. The minimum standards must be included in the local ordinances protecting archaeological and cultural resources. By no later than July 1, 2029, a county, city, or town planning under the GMA must have adopted a local ordinance protecting archaeological and cultural resources. Archaeological and Cultural Resource Task Force . A task force is established to assess archaeological and cultural resource protections. Membership of the task force is appointed by the Governor and consists of: representatives of federally recognized Indian tribes; a representative of DAHP; local government representatives; a representation of the Department of Ecology; a representative of the Department of Commerce; a representative of a state association representing cities; a representative of a state association representing counties; and one member from each of the two largest caucuses of the house and senate. The task force must review existing archaeological and cultural resource protections including state laws, administrative rules, and protections adopted by local governments. The task force must submit findings and recommendations to the Legislature by December 1, 2028. EFFECT OF CHANGES MADE BY WAYS & MEANS COMMITTEE (Second Substitute): Adds and intent section. Specifies minimum requirements for a local ordinance protecting archaeological and cultural resources including a data sharing agreement and written consultation agreement. EFFECT OF CHANGES MADE BY ENVIRONMENT, ENERGY & TECHNOLOGY COMMITTEE (First Substitute): Strikes requirements related to data sharing, cultural resource management plans, and consultation agreements as conditions for categorical exemptions for infill housing, project review under the GMA, projects that are categorically exempt by rule, and future categorical exemptions. Specifies as a condition of using the categorical exemption for project review under the GMA, beginning July 1, 2029 a local government must have adopted a local ordinance related to archaeological and cultural resource protection. Requires that not later than July 1, 2029, a county, city, or town planning under the GMA must adopt a local ordinance protecting archaeological and cultural resources that was developed in consultation with affected federally recognized Indian tribes. Requires DAHP to adopt a model ordinance for archaeological and cultural resource protection in consultation with tribes.
