HB 2607 · Child care rate regions

Requires DCYF to review and rebase child care subsidy rate regions every four years using cost-of-living data, and bars rate cuts caused solely by the rebase.

What the bill does (WA House Bill Analysis 2026 (Summary of Bill))

Beginning August 1, 2026, and every four years thereafter, the DCYF must review and rebase the child care rate regions to include regional adjustments in the cost of living and economic differences between regions based on the most recent available MRS. The DCYF must take into consideration factors like area median income, cost of living by zip code, and grouping by categories such as rural, suburban, and urban. The DCYF is encouraged to designate regions on a basis more localized than on the county level. No provider may receive a lower child care subsidy base rate as compared to its prior year only because of adjustments that reflect the updated child care rate regions. The DCYF must report each review and rebase to the Legislature.
