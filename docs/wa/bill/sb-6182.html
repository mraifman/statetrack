SB 6182 · Abortion savings program

Creates an Abortion Savings Program funded by an annual assessment on health carriers, with grants to abortion care providers for patients without sufficient resources.

What the bill does (WA Second Substitute House Bill Report (Summary of Bill))

Assessment on Health Carriers . Each health carrier offering health plans on the Health Benefit Exchange is subject to an annual coverage assessment which is initially due and payable on or before March 1, 2027. This does not include carriers offering dental only or vision only plans. The assessment is set at 82 cents per coverage month in the prior calendar year for the first year of the assessment, and annually thereafter, 16.5 cents per coverage month in the prior calendar year. Coverage month means each month of coverage under an insurance policy or each month of prepayment for health care services where premiums or prepayments are subject to the premium tax, not including coverage for Medicaid services rendered by licensed providers on a prepaid capitated basis that meets the federal requirements for Medicaid managed care organizations. Each health carrier must file an annual coverage month assessment statement with the OIC indicating the number of coverage months associated with premiums or prepayments for health care services subject to the premium tax. Any health carrier that fails to file their annual coverage month assessment statement or pay the assessment: by March 31st, will be assessed a total penalty of 5 percent of the assessment amount; within 45 days after the due date, will be assessed a total penalty of 10 percent of the assessment amount; and within 60 days of the due date, will be assessed a total penalty of 20 percent of the assessment amount. The assessment must be borne solely by the health carrier and may not be passed through to enrollees in premiums, rates, plan design, or otherwise, unless the OIC determines that to do so would create a significant risk of carrier insolvency or consumer harm. Interest accrues on the amount of the unpaid assessment at the maximum legal rate of interest permitted by the state beginning 61 days after the assessment is due until paid. The OIC may revoke the certificate of authority or registration of any delinquent health carrier, and the certificate of authority or registration may not be reissued until all assessments, interest, and penalties imposed under this section have been fully paid and the health carrier has otherwise qualified for the certificate of authority or registration. If any health carrier pays the OIC any assessment in error or more than what it is lawfully obligated to pay, the OIC shall upon written request make a refund thereof. A health carrier may only request a refund of assessments within six years of the end of the calendar year for which the assessments are owed. Refunds may be made either by crediting the amount toward payment of taxes, assessments, or other charges due or to become due from such health carrier, or by making a cash refund. Abortion Savings Program . The Abortion Savings Program is established for the Department of Health (DOH) to provide operating grants to eligible organizations to support equitable access to direct patient abortion clinical care services across the state for individuals without sufficient resources. The DOH shall award at least 85 percent of the funds appropriated for the program as grants to eligible organizations.
