SB 5689 · Drivers' licenses/blood type

Allows applicants to optionally include verified blood type on driver's licenses and identicards, with a one-time fee of up to $2 and public education.

What the bill does (WA Engrossed House Bill Report (Summary of Bill))

At the option of the applicant, a driver's license and identicard may include the individual's blood type if the applicant has provided documentation verifying the blood type from a licensed physician, medical facility, or blood donation organization. The DOL is given rulemaking authority and direction to develop processes and documentation requirements for individuals to submit blood type information. The DOL is given authority to impose a one-time administrative fee not to exceed $2 for processing the initial blood type designation request. The DOL must coordinate with health care providers, emergency responders, and blood donation organizations to educate the public about the availability and potential benefits of including blood type information on state-issued identification.
