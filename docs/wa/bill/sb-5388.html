SB 5388 · DOC behavioral health cert.

Requires DOH and DOC to adopt behavioral health standards for correctional facilities, with annual DOH inspections and DOC reimbursement of inspection costs.

What the bill does (WA Substitute Senate Bill Report (Summary of Bill))

Instead of requiring SUD treatment services to be licensed by DOH, the SUD treatment services must be provided by individuals licensed by the state of Washington. DOH and DOC are directed to jointly adopt standards by July 1, 2026, for the provision of behavioral health to individuals in correctional facilities. Beginning July 1, 2027, DOC must fully implement the adopted standards, and DOH must also begin conducting annual inspections to determine compliance with the standards. DOH must issue a report detailing any instances of noncompliance, and DOC must submit a corrective plan of action within 45 days. DOH and DOC are directed to review and update standards, as needed, every four years beginning July 20, 2030. DOC and DOH must enter into an agreement by July 1, 2027, with processes to conduct annual inspections and for DOC to reimburse DOH for the cost of annual inspections and technical assistance. The agreement must be renewed biennially.
