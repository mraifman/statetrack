SB 6188 · Asbestos training rules

Removes a limit that confined L&I's asbestos certification rulemaking to rules federally required for state standards to match federal law.

What the bill does (WA Senate Bill Report (Summary of Bill))

The provision that limits L&I's rulemaking authority for asbestos certification requirements to rules that are specifically required for the state's standards to be as stringent as applicable federal laws is removed.
