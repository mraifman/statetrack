HB 2219 · Child care providers

Requires zero-tolerance drug rules and immediate license suspension for child care, while allowing more mixed-age group time and waiving some repeat orientation.

What the bill does (WA Engrossed Substitute House Bill Report (Summary of Bill))

Minimum licensing requirements for child care and preschool programs must include a zero tolerance policy for imminent physical harm, defined as the presence of high-potency synthetic opioids or drug manufacturing equipment in the licensed facility or program space, and must expressly require that the premises of all licensed settings are absent of high-potency synthetic opioids, drug manufacturing equipment, and drug paraphernalia. Upon violation of the zero tolerance policy, the DCYF must issue an immediate summary suspension of the licensee's child care license. Statutory guidelines are established for periods of mixed-ratio time at child care centers in which children of different age groups may be combined. Child care centers are authorized to have up to 2.5 hours per day of mixed-ratio time, or four hours per day if at least 90 minutes of that time is reserved for meal and rest breaks for staff. The mixed-ratio time may be used on a schedule established by the child care center. The staff-to-child ratio and group size for the youngest child in the mixed group must be maintained during mixed-ratio time. No private right of action is action is created by the statutory child care licensing standard requirements. The DCYF must waive the preservice orientation requirement when an individual has previously completed the applicable orientation, and: it has been less than five years since completion of the orientation; the orientation and licensing process have not substantially changed since the date of completion; and the individual has not had a break in service either as a licensed child care provider or working in a licensed child care facility.
