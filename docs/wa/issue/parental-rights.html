Bills

  • HB 1296 · Public education system Became Law
    Declares state policy that school policies prioritize student safety, nondiscrimination, basic education access and privacy, and expands parental rights, including language access notice and enrollment options.
    Last action 2025-05-20
  • HB 1620 · Parenting plan limitations Became Law
    Reorganizes parenting plan limits; mandates restraints for parents convicted of child sex offenses, raises rebuttal standards, and revises supervised visitation rules.
    Last action 2025-04-25
  • HB 2557 · Sp. ed. evaluation reports Became Law
    Gives parents access to special education evaluation reports for their children.
    Last action 2026-03-11
  • HB 1051 · IEP team meetings/recording Died
    Lets parents or guardians audio record their student's IEP team meetings and requires other team members to comply with the Washington Privacy Act.
    Author: Walsh, Jim · Last action 2026-01-12
  • HB 1753 · Child removal/opioid use Died
    Modifies the child removal standard where another person uses or possesses a high-potency synthetic opioid, addressing threats of harm to children.
    Author: Rule, Alicia · Last action 2026-01-12
  • HB 2511 · Imminent physical harm/child Died
    Defines 'imminent physical harm' for child removal decisions as substantial risk of serious harm, including conditions like substance use and emotional neglect.
    Author: Dent, Tom · Last action 2026-01-15
  • HB 2660 · Child shelter care orders Died
    Limits court-ordered conditions on children under 5 released to parents after shelter care, requires 30-day reviews, and bars using service participation as an admission.
    Author: Ortiz-Self, Lillian · Last action 2026-02-09
  • SB 5136 · Student given names Died
    Requires public schools to use students' given names, framed as protecting parents' and guardians' rights.
    Author: McCune, Jim · Last action 2026-01-12
  • SB 5137 · Sex ed./parent approval Died
    Requires parental or guardian approval before a child may take part in comprehensive sexual health education.
    Author: McCune, Jim · Last action 2026-01-12
  • SB 5180 · Schools/gender inclusive Died
    Requires school districts to adopt gender-inclusive policies on names, pronouns, athletics, restrooms and dress, bars staff from disclosing student gender identity without consent, and protects employees from retaliation.
    Author: Wilson, Claire · Last action 2026-01-12
  • SB 5181 · Parents rights in education Died
    Revises parental education rights: changes record access timelines and fees, removes medical treatment notice, narrows criminal-action and law-enforcement notices, and revises campus-removal notice.
    Last action 2026-01-12
  • SB 5979 · Dependency/substance abuse Died
    Changes dependency petition and initiation rules, directs DCYF to weigh substance use and prior referrals, and expands in-home dependency options.
    Author: Torres, Nikki · Last action 2026-02-26
  • SB 6222 · Students/surplus technology Died
    Exempts school districts from public notice for surplus tech given to students, and requires notifying parents before giving hardware to students under 18.
    Last action 2026-03-12
  • SB 6261 · School enroll. declaration Died
    Requires parents who do not enroll a six-year-old in school to file a signed education plan declaration with their school district by set deadlines.
    Author: Wellman, Lisa · Last action 2026-01-21
  • SB 6308 · Child shelter care orders Died
    Lets courts impose least-restrictive safety conditions on parents of young children released from shelter care, with DCYF referrals, 30-day reviews, and cultural-appropriateness limits.
    Last action 2026-02-26
