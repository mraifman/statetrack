What is the Governor vetoing?

Of 691 bills that reached the Governor in the 2025–26 session, 1 were vetoed (0.1%) and 690 were signed. This page shows which topics, directions and authors the vetoes fall on.

Direction compares each bill with current Washington law, as everywhere else on this site. The Governor’s veto messages (the stated reasons) are not part of this data; each bill shows its own summary and votes.

On each topic, the hollow circle is the average direction of the bills the Governor signed and the filled circle is the average of the bills the Governor vetoed, measured against current law.
