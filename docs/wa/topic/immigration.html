237 Washington bills carry at least one tag under this topic. A bill can appear under several sub-topics.

Every direction is measured against the status quo: current Washington law, as the bill summary describes it. Permissive means the bill moves the law toward more permission, access or fewer rules. Restrictive means it moves toward less permission or more rules. A bill that continues, studies or clarifies current law is neutral and is left out of the averages.

  • HB 1773 · Wage replacement Died Permissive
