Find my legislators

Enter your street address and city to see who represents you in the DC Legislature, then what each of them has authored and how they have voted.

Addresses are matched by the U.S. Census Bureau’s geocoder, called from your browser; StateTrack never sees them.

Demo for DC’s 2025–26 session. Bill data: Council of the District of Columbia (LIMS). Topics and directions are labelled by an AI model from the bill’s title and the legislation summary, and are approximate. How it works
