B26-0101 · Removing Barriers and Reducing Stigma to Encourage HIV Prevention Amendment Act of 2025

Prohibits insurers from considering pre-exposure prophylaxis (PrEP) use when issuing disability, life, or long-term care insurance; provisions were folded into Bill 26-159.

What the bill does (DC LIMS bill summary (additionalInformation))

BILL SUMMARY - As introduced, Bill 26-101 would prohibit insurance companies from factoring the use of pre-exposure prophylaxis in decisions related to the issuance of disability, life, or long-term care insurance policies. NOTE - Provisions of this bill have been incorporated into Bill 26-159, PrEP DC Act of 2026.
