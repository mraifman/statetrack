B26-0461 · Juvenile Curfew Amendment Act of 2025 (now known as "Youth Curfew Amendment Act of 2026")

Expands the District's juvenile curfew by starting it at 11 p.m. daily, applying it to 17-year-olds, and letting the Mayor and Police Chief extend curfew hours and zones.

What the bill does (DC LIMS bill summary (additionalInformation))

BILL SUMMARY - As introduced, Bill 26-461 would authorize the Mayor to extend the juvenile curfew hours in the District when appropriate to protect public safety of public or private property. It would authorize the Chief of Police to establish extended juvenile curfew hour zones in specified circumstances and it would start the juvenile curfew hours at 11 p.m. every day and to extend the applicability of the juvenile curfew to 17-year olds. RACIAL EQUITY ANALYSIS - The bill includes several provisions with negative racial equity impacts. Please see the Racial Equity Impact Assessment (REIA) for more information.
