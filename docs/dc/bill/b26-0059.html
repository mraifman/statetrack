What the bill does (DC LIMS bill summary (additionalInformation))

BILL SUMMARY - As introduced, bill 26-59 would change the definition of a Special Tree and increase the fee for removal of Special Trees. It establishes a fee for the removal of Heritage Trees, and limits the use of the Tree Fund for plantings on public property. FISCAL NOTE - This bill was passed Subject to Appropriations. Bill fully funded via Act 26-416. Please see Fiscal Impact Statement and Funding Certification Letter for more information.
