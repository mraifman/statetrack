What the bill does (DC LIMS bill summary (additionalInformation))

BILL SUMMARY - As introduced, Bill 26-189 would prohibit persons targeting a residence for purposes of a demonstration from using sound amplifying devices in a residential zone between 7:00 p.m. and 9:00 a.m. It would also prohibit a person from launching or throwing a projectile onto the residential property of another with the intent to cause fear, intimidate, or retaliate against the occupants of that residence. It also clarifies the term expiration date for a Council appointment to the Corrections Information Council. RACIAL EQUITY ANALYSIS - The bill includes provisions that have a negative racial impact. Please see the Racial Equity Impact Analysis (REIA) for more information.
