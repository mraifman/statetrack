What the bill does (DC LIMS bill summary (additionalInformation))

BILL SUMMARY - As introduced, Bill 26-596 would require the Public Service Commission to approve multiyear rate plans only if the plans are based on historic test years and do not include reconciliation. It would specify how excess return on equity will be refunded to customers and also requires the Public Service Commission to approve gas infrastructure projects only if the company demonstrates customer benefit and that the company analyzed cost-effective alternatives.
