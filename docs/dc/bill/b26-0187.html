What the bill does (DC LIMS bill summary (additionalInformation))

BILL SUMMARY - As introduced, Bill 26-187 would allow applicants for officers of the Metropolitan Police Department to count certain credit hours for completion of the initial training program towards the requirement that certain applicants complete 60-hours of post-secondary education at an accredited college or university. RACIAL EQUITY ANALYSIS - The bill includes several provisions with a mix of negative, positive, and inconclusive impacts. Please see the Racial Equity Impact Assessment (REIA) for more information. FISCAL NOTE - Section 5 is passed Subject to Appropriations. Please see the Fiscal Impact Statement for more information.
