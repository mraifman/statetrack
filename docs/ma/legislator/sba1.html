These come from what this legislator authored and how they voted on tagged bills, with the bills linked below. A co-author may sign on to a bill they have reservations about, and a vote reflects one amendment stage. Most bills that reach a floor vote pass with large majorities and almost every member votes aye, so aye counts mostly mirror what reached the floor; authorship is the more distinctive signal.
