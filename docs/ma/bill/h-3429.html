What the bill does (MA bill pinslip (official summary))

By Representative Vargas of Haverhill, a petition (accompanied by bill, House, No. 3429) of Andres X. Vargas and others for legislation to direct the State Treasurer to establish a baby bonds trust fund to assist certain designated beneficiaries in pursuing opportunities for education, housing, and entrepreneurship.
