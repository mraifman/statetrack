H 1423 · An Act relative to social work uplifting practices and exam removal

Removes the licensing exam for social workers and creates a Department of Education field placement grant program to support a stable, diverse licensed workforce.

What the bill does (MA bill pinslip (official summary))

By Representatives Barber of Somerville and Kushmerek of Fitchburg, a petition (accompanied by bill, House, No. 1423) of Christine P. Barber, Michael P. Kushmerek and others that the Department of Education create and administer a field placement grant program to ensure a stable, diverse workforce of licensed social workers. Higher Education.
