Bills

  • AB 56 · Social media: warning labels. Became Law
    Requires social media platforms to display black box warnings to users on initial daily access and periodically during active use, starting 2027.
    Author: Bauer-Kahan, Rebecca · Last action 2025-10-13
  • AB 621 · Deepfake pornography. Became Law
    Expands civil liability for sexually explicit deepfakes, including those of minors, raises statutory damages, and targets service providers that enable deepfake pornography sites.
    Author: Bauer-Kahan, Rebecca · Last action 2025-10-13
  • AB 656 · Account cancellation. Became Law
    Requires social media platforms to offer a clear account-deletion button, bars obstructing deletion, and treats account deletion requests as CCPA deletion requests.
    Author: Schiavo, Pilar · Last action 2025-10-08
  • AB 853 · California AI Transparency Act. Became Law
    Delays California AI Transparency Act to August 2026 and adds provenance detection duties for large platforms, capture device disclosure options, and GenAI hosting platform restrictions.
    Author: Wicks, Buffy · Last action 2025-10-13
  • AB 1043 · Age verification signals: software applications and online services. Became Law
    Requires operating system providers and app stores to collect age information at account setup and share age-bracket signals with developers starting 2027.
    Author: Wicks, Buffy · Last action 2025-10-13
  • AB 1609 · Customer service chatbots. Became Law
    Requires large businesses to offer human customer service agents, limit hold times, and disclose chatbots, with public prosecutors able to enforce penalties.
    Author: Zbur, Rick Chavez · Last action 2026-09-28
  • AB 1705 · Pornographic internet websites. Became Law
    Requires pornographic websites to take reasonable steps to keep depicted individuals out of uploaded content, and requires uploaders to certify criteria under penalty of perjury.
    Author: Bauer-Kahan, Rebecca · Last action 2026-09-30
  • AB 1709 · Covered platforms: age restriction: e-Safety Advisory Commission. Became Law
    Prohibits addictive features for users under 16 on covered platforms and creates an e-Safety Advisory Commission within the Department of Justice.
    Author: Lowenthal, Josh · Last action 2026-09-10
  • AB 1766 · Health curriculum framework: human trafficking and online safety. Became Law
    Requires the health curriculum framework to consider human trafficking and online safety content for K-12, and the Department of Education to publish related resources.
    Author: Krell, Maggy · Last action 2026-09-30
  • AB 1856 · Age verification signals: software applications. Became Law
    Expands age-signal requirements for operating systems and app stores to more devices, and bars requesting age signals unless legally required.
    Author: Wicks, Buffy · Last action 2026-09-10
  • AB 1946 · Reporting mechanism: child sexual abuse material. Became Law
    Expands the social media CSAM reporting duties, adds platform liability and civil penalties to a depicted-individual damages scheme, and removes the 4-month knowledge threshold.
    Author: Wicks, Buffy · Last action 2026-09-10
  • AB 2076 · The Parent’s Accountability and Child Protection Act: online marketplaces: nitrous oxide. Became Law
    Requires online sellers to verify buyers' age for nitrous oxide sales, bars gift card payments for age-restricted items, and raises civil penalties.
    Author: Lowenthal, Josh · Last action 2026-09-30
  • AB 2246 · Online service, product, or feature: access by children. Became Law
    Replaces the Age-Appropriate Design Code with stricter rules requiring online services likely accessed by children to prevent harms and limit data use, with civil penalties.
    Author: Wicks, Buffy · Last action 2026-09-10
  • AB 2713 · California AI Transparency Act: system provenance data. Became Law
    Revises large online platform duties on AI provenance data, exempting data and signatures that do not comply with standards-body specifications.
    Author: Wicks, Buffy · Last action 2026-09-30
  • SB 243 · Companion chatbots. Became Law
    Requires companion chatbot operators to disclose AI status, protect minors, maintain suicide-prevention protocols, and report annually to the Office of Suicide Prevention.
    Author: Padilla, Stephen C. · Last action 2025-10-13
  • SB 378 · Online marketplaces: illicit cannabis: reporting and liability. Became Law
    Requires online cannabis marketplaces to disclose unlicensed sellers, warn consumers, and provide a reporting mechanism for unlicensed listings.
    Author: Wiener, Scott D. · Last action 2025-10-06
  • SB 707 · Open meetings: meeting and teleconference requirements. Became Law
    Requires local legislative bodies to offer remote public attendance, adopt disruption policies, and meet expanded agenda and compensation-report requirements through 2030.
    Author: Durazo, Maria Elena · Last action 2025-10-03
  • SB 867 · Toys: companion chatbots. Became Law
    Temporarily bans manufacture and sale of toys that include companion chatbots until 2031, with civil liability for violations.
    Author: Padilla, Stephen C. · Last action 2026-09-10
  • SB 1119 · Companion chatbots: children’s safety. Became Law
    Requires companion chatbot operators to assess risks, undergo independent child safety audits, and face enforcement for minor users, starting July 2027.
    Author: Padilla, Stephen C. · Last action 2026-09-10
  • SB 1247 · Social media platforms: child influencers. Became Law
    Requires social media platforms to offer child influencers a way to request removal of paid content featuring them, and lets them sue vloggers who don't comply.
    Author: Padilla, Stephen C. · Last action 2026-09-30
  • AB 1064 · Leading Ethical AI Development (LEAD) for Kids Act. Vetoed
    Bars companion chatbots from children unless they cannot foreseeably encourage self-harm, violence, drug use, or disordered eating; adds AG penalties and private lawsuits.
    Author: Bauer-Kahan, Rebecca · Last action 2026-01-22
  • SB 771 · Personal rights: liability: social media platforms. Vetoed
    Makes social media platforms liable for civil penalties when their algorithms or conduct violate state personal-rights and hate-violence laws, operative 2027.
    Author: Stern, Henry I. · Last action 2026-03-02
  • AB 2023 · Companion chatbots: children’s safety. In Floor Process
    Requires companion chatbot operators to assess child safety risks, undergo independent audits, and face civil enforcement for children's protection.
    Author: Wicks, Buffy · Last action 2026-08-30
  • SB 300 · Companion chatbots. In Floor Process
    Tightens companion chatbot rules for minors and suicide protocols; adds a conflict-of-interest exception for nonprofit dues.
    Author: Padilla, Stephen C. · Last action 2026-08-30
  • AB 392 · Pornographic internet websites: consent. In Committee Process
    Requires pornographic websites to verify uploaders' email and consent and confirm depicted people are adults, with civil and infraction penalties for violations.
    Author: Dixon, Diane · Last action 2025-08-29
  • AB 1700 · e-Safety Commission: youth online protection. In Committee Process
    Creates an e-Safety Commission to develop minimum age compliance guidelines, review age verification technology, and investigate noncompliance for online platforms.
    Author: Lowenthal, Josh · Last action 2026-03-23
  • AB 1988 · Companion chatbots: crisis interruption pauses. In Committee Process
    Requires companion chatbot operators to detect credible crisis expressions, pause conversations, document incidents, and report annually to the Office of Suicide Prevention.
    Author: Pellerin, Gail · Last action 2026-06-26
  • AB 2169 · Social media platforms: artificial intelligence models. In Committee Process
    Requires social media companies and AI model deployers to let users export their data and open interoperability interfaces, enforced by the Attorney General.
    Author: Lowenthal, Josh · Last action 2026-05-14
  • AB 2426 · Online platforms: educational children’s content. In Committee Process
    Requires covered online platforms to maintain an ad-free, account-free section of exclusively educational children's content, with compliance reports and penalties.
    Author: Wallis, Greg · Last action 2026-04-09
  • AB 2617 · Protecting Kids from Online Gambling Act. In Committee Process
    Bars online gambling and prediction market wagering to minors, requires age assurance, and allows AG and city attorney enforcement.
    Author: Schiavo, Pilar · Last action 2026-03-17
  • SB 1074 · Covered provider: goods and services: self-preferencing conduct. In Committee Process
    Prohibits large covered platforms from self-preferencing their own products in rankings and from restricting interoperability or data portability.
    Author: Wiener, Scott D. · Last action 2026-04-20
  • SB 1142 · Digital Dignity Act. In Committee Process
    Creates liability for distributing AI digital replicas used for impersonation and requires large platforms with replica tools to offer reporting and honor court-ordered removals.
    Author: Becker, Josh · Last action 2026-05-14
  • SB 1217 · Nonconsensual Intimate Image Clearinghouse. In Committee Process
    Creates a DOJ clearinghouse so victims can seek removal of nonconsensual intimate images, requiring platforms to remove verified matches within 48 hours.
    Author: Grove, Shannon · Last action 2026-05-14
  • SB 1390 · Online dating services: background checks. In Committee Process
    Requires online dating providers to run criminal background checks on California users and flag profiles of registered sex offenders or people with specified convictions.
    Author: Menjivar, Caroline · Last action 2026-04-20
  • AB 796 · Social media platforms: advertising: tax. Died

  • AB 1137 · Reporting mechanism: child sexual abuse material. Died

    Author: Krell, Maggy · Last action 2026-02-02
  • SB 637 · Consumer review platforms. Died
    Requires consumer review platforms offered in California to let businesses opt out of being reviewed or featured in testimonials.
    Author: Grayson, Timothy S. · Last action 2026-02-02
  • SB 659 · The Parent’s Accountability and Child Protection Act: remedies. Died

  • SB 780 · Cyberbullying Protection Act: liability. Died
    Raises the per-violation liability for social media platforms that fail to address cyberbullying reporting duties from $10,000 to $50,000.
    Author: Stern, Henry I. · Last action 2026-02-02
