Bills

  • AB 1577 · Data centers: reporting. Became Law
    Requires data center operators to report energy data to the state and provide energy and sound information for local permits.
    Author: Bauer-Kahan, Rebecca · Last action 2026-09-21
  • AB 2383 · Electricity: data centers. Became Law
    Requires utilities and community choice aggregators to adopt tariffs for generation and transmission service, including for data centers.
    Author: Zbur, Rick Chavez · Last action 2026-09-21
  • AB 2469 · Data centers: water use disclosures. Became Law
    Bars local approval of data center construction or water-use expansions unless applicants provide water plans and pay infrastructure costs.
    Author: Papan, Diane · Last action 2026-09-21
  • AB 2619 · Water resources: data centers. Became Law
    Requires data centers to report water use under penalty of perjury when seeking local licenses; adds data centers to water supply assessments.
    Author: Papan, Diane · Last action 2026-09-21
  • SB 57 · Electrical corporations: data centers: report. Became Law
    Authorizes the PUC to assess whether data center electricity costs shift to other customers, and requires a public report by 2027.
    Author: Padilla, Stephen C. · Last action 2025-10-11
  • SB 886 · California Technology Innovation and Ratepayer Protection Act. Became Law
    Requires the PUC to set tariffs for data center interconnection and service by 2028, protecting nonparticipating ratepayers from stranded costs and cost shifts.
    Author: Padilla, Stephen C. · Last action 2026-09-21
  • SB 887 · California Environmental Quality Act: environmental leadership development projects: data centers: geothermal powerplant projects. Became Law
    Bars CEQA categorical exemptions for data centers while letting the Governor certify data center and geothermal projects for CEQA streamlining.
    Author: Padilla, Stephen C. · Last action 2026-09-21
  • SB 1168 · Data centers: rate structures. Became Law
    Directs the PUC to assess rate structures so data centers pay a fair share of grid costs and residential rates are protected.
    Author: McNerney, Jerry · Last action 2026-09-21
  • AB 93 · Water resources: data centers. Vetoed
    Requires data centers to estimate and report water use to their suppliers and self-certify this when applying for city or county business licenses.
    Author: Papan, Diane · Last action 2026-01-22
  • AB 222 · Data centers: power usage effectiveness: cost shifts. In Committee Process
    Requires data center energy reporting and PUC review to prevent utility costs from shifting onto other ratepayers.
    Author: Bauer-Kahan, Rebecca · Last action 2025-08-29
  • SB 978 · Data centers: labor: electricity rates. In Committee Process
    Requires PUC to create special electricity rate structure for large data centers, shifting infrastructure upgrade costs to them, plus construction labor rules.
    Author: Pérez, Sasha Renée · Last action 2026-05-14
  • AB 1095 · Data centers: waste heat energy. Died
    Allows data center waste heat capture projects to get I-Bank climate financing and qualify for renewable energy credits under the RPS.
    Author: Papan, Diane · Last action 2026-02-02
