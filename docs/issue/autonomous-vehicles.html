Bills

  • SB 480 · Autonomous vehicles. Became Law
    Authorizes autonomous vehicles to be equipped with ADS marker lamps that show when automated driving is engaged, under specified standards, starting 2026.
    Author: Archuleta, Bob · Last action 2025-10-06
  • SB 988 · California Motor Vehicle Glass Act. Became Law
    Requires glass repair shops to give ADAS calibration notices, written results and itemized invoices, and bars insureds from assigning auto glass policy benefits to shops.
    Author: Grayson, Timothy S. · Last action 2026-09-27
  • SB 1246 · Autonomous vehicles. Became Law
    Requires driverless vehicle makers to add remote-operator, communications, emergency response, and staffing requirements, with DMV guidelines on technician response times.
    Author: Cortese, Dave · Last action 2026-09-30
  • AB 33 · Autonomous vehicles. In Floor Process
    Bans driverless autonomous vehicles from delivering commercial goods on California highways, with fines and permit suspension for violations.
    Author: Aguiar-Curry, Cecilia M. · Last action 2025-09-09
  • SB 1315 · Advanced driver assistance systems. In Floor Process
    Requires manufacturers of driver-assistance systems to let drivers disable them where manual driving was previously available, with civil penalties up to $25,000 per violation.
    Author: Cabaldon, Christopher · Last action 2026-08-30
  • AB 2193 · Autonomous vehicles. In Committee Process
    Assigns traffic citations for autonomous vehicle violations to the manufacturer, registered owner, or driver, depending on whether a person is in the driver's seat and the technology is engaged.
    Author: Ta, Tri · Last action 2026-05-14
  • SB 572 · Vehicles: advanced driver assistance system: crash reports. In Committee Process
    Requires manufacturers to report Level 2 driver-assistance crashes to the DMV within 5 days, with DMV posting data and a civil penalty for noncompliance.
    Author: Gonzalez, Lena A. · Last action 2025-08-29
  • SB 511 · Pupil nutrition: nutrition assistance: community program information. Died
    Brings Level 2 driver-assistance systems under autonomous vehicle rules, moves zero-emission requirements to 2028, and adds cost-recovery fees.
    Author: Pérez, Sasha Renée · Last action 2026-02-02
