Bills

  • SB 480 · Autonomous vehicles. Chaptered
    Authorizes autonomous vehicles to be equipped with ADS marker lamps that signal when automated driving is engaged, under specified standards, starting 2026.
    Author: Archuleta, Bob · Last action 2025-10-06
  • SB 1246 · Autonomous vehicles. Chaptered
    Adds operational requirements for autonomous vehicle manufacturers, including remote driver licensing, communications capacity, and rapid local technician dispatch after crashes.
    Author: Cortese, Dave · Last action 2026-09-30
  • AB 2502 · Vehicles: driving under the influence: driving automation. Vetoed
    Clarifies that driving under the influence applies to an impaired driver using supervised driver support features, such as partial automation.
    Author: Pellerin, Gail · Last action 2026-09-27
  • AB 33 · Autonomous vehicles. In Floor Process
    Prohibits delivering commercial goods by driverless autonomous vehicles in California, with fines, possible permit suspension, and a required study of AV impacts.
    Author: Aguiar-Curry, Cecilia M. · Last action 2025-09-09
  • SB 1315 · Advanced driver assistance systems. In Floor Process
    Requires ADAS and autonomous-equipped vehicle manufacturers to let drivers disable automation, with up to $25,000 civil penalties; DMV considers related exam questions.
    Author: Cabaldon, Christopher · Last action 2026-08-30
  • AB 2193 · Autonomous vehicles. In Committee Process
    Assigns traffic citations for autonomous vehicle violations to the owner, driver, or manufacturer, depending on driver presence and automation level.
    Author: Ta, Tri · Last action 2026-05-14
  • SB 572 · Vehicles: advanced driver assistance system: crash reports. In Committee Process
    Requires Level 2 driver-assistance vehicle manufacturers to report crashes to DMV within five days, with public posting and civil penalties for noncompliance.
    Author: Gonzalez, Lena A. · Last action 2025-08-29
  • SB 511 · Pupil nutrition: nutrition assistance: community program information. Died
    Expands autonomous vehicle rules to Level 2 systems, moves the zero-emission requirement up to 2028, and requires DMV fees to cover AV regulatory costs.
    Author: Pérez, Sasha Renée · Last action 2026-02-02
