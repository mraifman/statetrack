Energy Costs and Clean Energy

Electricity and gasoline prices remain among the highest in the nation, and lawmakers have sent packages meant to bring them down.

Bills on this page: tagged under Clean Energy (Renewables, Storage, Nuclear), Utility Regulation and Rates, Fossil Fuels, Pipelines and Mining; whose title or digest mentions “ratepayer” or “gasoline” or “refiner” (in the title, or at least 2 times in the digest).

Every direction is measured against the status quo: current California law, as the Legislative Counsel’s digest describes it. Permissive means the bill moves the law toward more permission, access or fewer rules. Restrictive means it moves toward less permission or more rules. A bill that continues, studies or clarifies current law is neutral and is left out of the averages.
