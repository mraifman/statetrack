SB 800 · State bridges and overpasses: suicide prevention.

Requires the Department of Transportation to add suicide deterrent considerations to updated bridge and overpass guidance by July 2028.

What the bill does (Legislative Counsel’s digest)

Existing law requires the Department of Transportation to install screening on state freeway overpasses to prevent objects from being dropped or thrown upon vehicles passing underneath, as provided.. This bill would require, beginning on or before July 1, 2028, the Department of Transportation, in consultation with the State Department of Public Health and in collaboration with impacted local governments, to incorporate suicide deterrent considerations in the updates of applicable guidance documents, as provided.
