What the bill does (Legislative Counsel’s digest)

Existing law establishes the Department of Parks and Recreation in the Natural Resources Agency, and specifies that the department shall be conducted under the control of an executive officer known as the Director of Parks and Recreation. This bill would make nonsubstantive changes in that provision.
