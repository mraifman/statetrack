AB 711 · Civil Actions: shorthand reporters.

Requires civil motion meet-and-confer notices to state that parties discussed retaining a shorthand reporter, and allows those conferences electronically.

What the bill does (Legislative Counsel’s digest)

Existing law requires that meet and confer notices with specified information be filed in support of specified motions. This bill would require that the meet and confer notices specify that the parties have met and conferred regarding the retention of a certified shorthand reporter for the hearing on the motion. This bill also specifies that the meet and confer may be done through electronic communications.
