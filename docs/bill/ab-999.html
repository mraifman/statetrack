AB 999 · Skydiving or sport parachuting operations.

Makes a nonsubstantive wording change to existing skydiving operator duties regarding federal parachute safety and certification compliance.

What the bill does (Legislative Counsel’s digest)

Existing law requires owners and operators of a skydiving or sport parachuting operation to have a duty to ensure that the parachutist in command of a tandem jump and the parachute rigger responsible for packing the parachute are in compliance with all federal laws relating to parachute safety and certification, to the extent allowed by federal law. This bill would make a nonsubstantive change to that provision.
