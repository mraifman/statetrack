What the bill does (Legislative Counsel’s digest)

Existing law requires a person who traps fur-bearing mammals or nongame mammals to procure a trapping license from the Department of Fish and Wildlife, except as provided, and to pay a specified fee for that license that varies based on the age and residency of the applicant. This bill would make a nonsubstantive change to the latter provisions relating to the specified fees for the license.
