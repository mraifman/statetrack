What the bill does (Legislative Counsel’s digest)

Existing law generally prohibits the sale, installation, or replacement of vehicle parts for use, or intended use, on a vehicle that is not in conformity with the Vehicle Code. This bill would make a technical, nonsubstantive change to this provision.
