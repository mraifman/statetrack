SB 854 · Unemployment insurance.

Defines "mail" and related terms in the Unemployment Insurance Code to include electronic transmission of writings.

What the bill does (Legislative Counsel’s digest)

Existing law, the Unemployment Insurance Code, governs unemployment insurance in the state, including, among other things, the administration of unemployment and disability compensation. Existing law defines various terms for purposes of the code. This bill would define “mail,” “mailing,” or “mailed” to include a writing transmitted by the United States Postal Service or other common mail carrier or by electronic transmission, as specified.
