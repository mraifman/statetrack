AB 1912 · Deer: archery season: concealed firearms.

Repeals the ban on hunters carrying firearms during deer archery season, allowing them to carry guns while bow hunting.

What the bill does (Legislative Counsel’s digest)

Existing law establishes an archery season for the taking of deer with bow and arrow. Existing law generally prohibits a person taking or attempting to take deer during that archery season from carrying, or having under their immediate control, a firearm of any kind, except for an active or honorably retired peace officer, as specified. This bill would repeal that prohibition.
