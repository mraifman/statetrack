AB 2740 · Enforcement of money judgments: exemptions.

Clarifies that the exemption for materials used to repair or improve a home applies to a debtor's principal residence or domicile.

What the bill does (Legislative Counsel’s digest)

Existing law identifies various types of property of a judgment debtor that are exempt from the enforcement of a money judgment, including material that in good faith is about to be applied to the repair or improvement of a residence, as specified. This bill would specify that this exemption would apply to the judgment debtor’s principal place of residence or domicile.
