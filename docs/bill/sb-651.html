SB 651 · Food and agriculture.

Makes nonsubstantive wording changes to how Food and Agricultural Code provisions are construed in civil and criminal actions.

What the bill does (Legislative Counsel’s digest)

Existing law, in civil actions, requires the provisions of the Food and Agricultural Code to be liberally construed to accomplish specified purposes. Existing law, in criminal actions, requires the provisions of the Food and Agricultural Code to be construed according to the fair import of their terms, as provided. This bill would make nonsubstantive changes to the above-described provisions involving the construction of the provisions of the Food and Agricultural Code.
