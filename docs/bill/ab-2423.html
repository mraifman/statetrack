What the bill does (Legislative Counsel’s digest)

Existing law provides procedures for the formation of pest abatement districts for the purpose of pest control or abatement. The Cotton Pests Abatement District Act authorizes the organization and establishment of cotton pests abatement districts by the boards of supervisors of the Counties of Imperial, Los Angeles, Orange, Riverside, San Bernardino, San Diego, and Ventura. This bill would remove the authority to establish these districts in the Counties of Orange, San Diego, and Ventura. Existing law authorizes a local agency, by a <caml:Fraction><caml:Numerator>4</caml:Numerator><caml:Denominator>5</caml:Denominator></caml:Fraction> vote of its legislative body, to donate and convey any land and buildings owned, held, or used by the agency, subject to certain conditions.</xhtml:p><xhtml:p>This bill would make nonsubstantive changes to those provisions.</xhtml:p>"?>
