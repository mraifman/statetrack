SB 1281 · Public resources: certified community conservation corps.

Makes only nonsubstantive wording changes to existing law on certified community conservation corps in large cities.

What the bill does (Legislative Counsel’s digest)

Existing law provides for certified community conservation corps, defined as a community conservation corps that was in existence on September 30, 1999, or that is formed subsequent to that date, and that is designated by a city or a city and county to perform litter abatement, recycling, and related activities, if the city or the city and county has a population, as determined by the most recent census, of more than 250,000 persons. This bill would make nonsubstantive changes to that provision.
