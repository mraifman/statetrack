AJR 2 · Location of missing immigrant children.

Nonbinding resolution urging federal officials to locate missing immigrant children who crossed the southern border and address the crisis.

What the bill does (Legislative Counsel’s digest)

This measure would call upon the federal government to locate the missing children who were brought across the southern border of the United States under the Biden Administration and to take immediate and comprehensive action to address the crisis of missing undocumented children, as specified. The measure would also call upon the incoming administration and Congress to act with urgency and compassion to address this crisis and uphold the nation’s commitment to protecting vulnerable children.
