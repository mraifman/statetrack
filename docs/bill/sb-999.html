SB 999 · Franchise Tax Board: reporting requirements.

Moves the Franchise Tax Board's annual health coverage penalty report deadline from March 1 to April 1.

What the bill does (Legislative Counsel’s digest)

Existing law requires the Franchise Tax Board, on or before March 1, 2022, and annually on or before March 1 thereafter, to report certain information to the Legislature regarding health coverage penalties paid by households, in addition to related information. This bill would change the annual reporting deadline to April 1, as provided.
