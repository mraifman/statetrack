What the bill does (Legislative Counsel’s digest)

The Legislature adopted Assembly Constitutional Amendment No. 13 of the 2023–24 Regular Session, relating to voting. Existing law requires the Secretary of State to submit ACA 13 to the voters at the November 3, 2026, statewide general election. This measure instead would direct the Secretary of State to withdraw ACA 13 from consideration by the voters.
