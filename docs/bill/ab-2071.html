AB 2071 · Pupil instruction: digital wellness.

Requires the State Department of Education to develop a plan by 2028 to expand digital wellness instruction in public schools.

What the bill does (Legislative Counsel’s digest)

Existing law requires the State Department of Education, on or before January 1, 2024, to develop a plan to expand mental health instruction in California public schools. This bill would require the department, on or before January 1, 2028, to develop a plan to expand digital wellness instruction in California public schools, as provided.
