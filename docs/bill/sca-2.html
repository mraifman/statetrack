“This measure would prohibit the Governor from granting a pardon or commutation to specified relatives.”

What the bill does (Legislative Counsel’s digest)

The California Constitution authorizes the Governor to grant a reprieve, pardon, or commutation after sentence has been entered, except as specified. This measure would prohibit the Governor from granting a pardon or commutation to specified relatives.
