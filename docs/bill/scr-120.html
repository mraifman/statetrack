What the bill does (Legislative Counsel’s digest)

This measure would proclaim March 1, 2026, to March 7, 2026, inclusive, as National Consumer Protection Week, and March 5, 2026, as Slam the Scam Day, and would encourage all Californians to engage in consumer education efforts and to utilize available resources to prevent fraud, protect personal information, and promote transparency and fairness in the marketplace.
