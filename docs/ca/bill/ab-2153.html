AB 2153 · Voter registration: residency confirmation.

Repeals an obsolete version of county voter registration update and inactivation requirements, a cleanup change with no substantive effect.

What the bill does (Legislative Counsel’s digest)

Existing law requires county elections officials to update a voter’s registration record if change-of-address information indicates the voter has moved to a new residence address in California. Existing law requires county elections officials to change a voter’s registration status to inactive if mailings have been returned as undeliverable or if other sources of information indicate that the voter has moved out of state. This bill would repeal an obsolete version of these requirements.
