AB 1641 · Postsecondary education: segments: tribal colleges and universities.

Adds tribal colleges and universities operated by Indian tribal governments in California to the state's public higher education segments.

What the bill does (Legislative Counsel’s digest)

Existing law establishes the California Community Colleges, the California State University, the University of California, independent institutions of higher education, and private postsecondary educational institutions as the segments of postsecondary education in the state. The missions and functions of these segments are set forth in the Donahoe Higher Education Act. This bill would provide that public higher education additionally consists of tribal colleges and universities geographically located in California that are operated by an Indian tribal government, as defined.
